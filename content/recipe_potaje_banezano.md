# Potaje Bañezano (Garbanzos con Arroz) / Potaje de La Bañeza

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Castilla y León](provincial_castilla_y_leon.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented in the *Revista Española de Nutrición Comunitaria*'s 2022 survey of **forgotten dishes of Castilla y León** (photo: Luis Mantecón, judge of the Cofradía Nuestra Señora de las Angustias, La Bañeza). The source lists ingredients without quantities, which are reconstructed below. The brotherhood credit and the meatless recipe suggest a **Holy Week / Lent** dish, but the source doesn't say so explicitly.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** La Bañeza (León).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Caldoso** (soupy). Chickpeas and rice in their cooking broth.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** An *olla* (today often a pressure cooker for the chickpeas).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A meatless pot of **chickpeas and rice**, finished with a *sofrito* of spring onion and paprika poured over at the end. It is La Bañeza's counterpart to the Lenten legume-and-rice pots of the Mediterranean (compare Orihuela's [tres puñaos](recipe_arroz_de_los_tres_punaos.md)). A detail of the source recipe is the **warm water**: the chickpeas soak in warm salted water, and the cooking water must be warm when they go in. Castilian cooks traditionally held that cold water hardens chickpeas.

**Culinary science:** chickpeas cooked with crushed garlic and parsley in oil release starch that, with the rice, gives the broth its body. Frying the paprika separately with spring onion and adding it **at the end** keeps its colour and sweetness bright instead of muddy.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **La Bañeza (RENC):** Chickpeas, rice, spring onion, oil, garlic, parsley, paprika, salt. A bay leaf is optional. Rest briefly before serving.
*   ⚠️ No other town versions documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **La Bañeza (RENC 2022)**, with reconstructed quantities.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Dried chickpeas, soaked overnight in warm salted water | 300 g |
| Warm water | 2 L |
| Olive oil | 60 ml |
| Garlic + parsley, pounded | 3 cloves + 1 bunch |
| Bay leaf (optional) | 1 |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Round rice | 200 g |
| Broth at rice-in | ≈ 1.4 L (caldoso) |
| Spring onion (*cebolleta*), finely chopped | 1 bunch |
| Sweet paprika | 1 tsp |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Chickpeas:** Put them in **warm** water with the oil and the pounded garlic and parsley. Cook until tender (≈ 15 min in a pressure cooker, 1.5–2 h in an open pot).
2. **Rice:** With the pot **open**, add the rice and cook **20 min**.
3. **Sofrito:** Fry the spring onion slowly. Off the heat, add the paprika and pour it into the pot.
4. **Finish:** Bring back to the boil once and rest a few minutes.
