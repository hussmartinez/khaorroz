# Comunidad de Madrid / Madrid

> ⬅ [README](README.md)

## 1. Overview
Madrid has no rice fields and no regional rice dish of its own. Its rice culture is that of the **capital**: *casas de comidas*, *tabernas* and the *menú del día*, where rices from every region meet. Two plates are genuinely embedded in Madrid's everyday food culture: **arroz con leche**, the cold, cinnamon-dusted dessert of traditional taverns, and **arroz a la cubana**, the cheap and filling plate of white rice, tomato sauce and fried egg. Madrid's Sunday paellas are generally Valencian-style imports, often of the mixed type that Valencians would not call "valenciana".

⚠️ Madrid-specific documentation of these dishes is thin; see each file's notes.
