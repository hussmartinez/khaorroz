# Província de Castelló / Provincia de Castellón

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [València](provincial_valencia.md) · [Alacant](provincial_alicante.md)

## 1. Overview
Castellón's rice cooking is less famous than Valencia's or Alicante's, but it has strong local signatures. The fishing ports of **Vinaròs**, **Benicarló**, **Peníscola** and **El Grau de Castelló** land **galeres** (mantis shrimp) and **llagostins** (Vinaròs prawns). The coastal plain grows the **D.O. Alcachofa de Benicarló**. The cold, mountainous interior (Els Ports, Maestrat, Alt Millars) cooks rabbit, snail, mushroom and matança rices over pine and oak. The province's emblematic everyday dish is not a rice at all but the **olla de la Plana**, a stew of beans and vegetables. Rice is the festive and coastal complement, especially during the **Magdalena** festivals and Castelló's **Mes de l'Arròs**.

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **El Baix Maestrat** | Galeres, llagostins, artichoke rices | Vinaròs, Benicarló, Peníscola |
| **La Plana Alta** | "Arrocito" del Grau, marinera rices | Castelló de la Plana, El Grau, Benicàssim |
| **La Plana Baixa** | Huerta paellas, artichoke | Vila-real, Burriana, Nules |
| **L'Alcalatén / Alt Millars** | Mountain rabbit-mushroom rices | L'Alcora, Llucena, Montanejos |
| **Els Ports / Alt Maestrat** | Caldoso mountain rices, matança | Morella, Vilafranca, Albocàsser |
