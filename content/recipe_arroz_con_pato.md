# Arroz con Pato / Arròs amb Ànec (Arròs d'Ànec de l'Albufera)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** L'Albufera and La Ribera Baixa: Sueca, El Palmar, El Perellonet, Silla, Catarroja, Cullera.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry) in the paella version, the Albufera hunting tradition documented here. A clay-baked *arròs amb ànec i naps* exists as a separate household variant (see §3).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A steel paella over orange-tree or rice-straw fire, or a clay *cassola* in the oven for the turnip variant.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
L'Albufera is Europe's most important wintering wetland for ducks, and from October to January the *tirades* (regulated hunting days) traditionally supplied **wild duck** (*collverd* mallard, *sivert* teal, *bragat*) to the villages of the lake. Sueca, the largest rice-producing municipality in the Valencian Community, hosts the **Festa de l'Arròs** and the **Concurs Internacional de Paella Valenciana**. Its cooks long put duck into the pan alongside or instead of chicken. With the decline of hunting, farmed duck (often **Muscovy** or **Barbary** duck) has taken over.

**Culinary science:** duck has a thick subcutaneous fat layer and darker, myoglobin-rich meat. Rendering the skin slowly releases fat, which then becomes the frying medium for the sofrito and the rice, so the grains taste of duck. The meat must be cooked for longer than chicken (≈35–45 min in the fondo) to tenderise its collagen. Turnip, the classic partner, absorbs duck fat and balances it with its mustard-like bitterness.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)

*   **The Sueca Style:** **Duck + chicken + rabbit** in a paella valenciana frame (*bajoqueta*, *garrofó*, tomato, saffron). In winter, **artichoke** joins the pan. Little or no pimentón. Classic orange-wood fire.
*   **The El Palmar / Albufera island Style:** **Duck alone**, often wild, with **eel** (*anguila*) sometimes added, closing the circle with *all i pebre*. Garlic-heavy base. Some cooks add a few **wild mushrooms** in autumn.
*   **The Silla / Catarroja Style:** **Arròs amb ànec i naps**. Duck + **turnip** + white beans or *garrofó*, cooked in a *cassola* and finished in the oven. Closer to [arròs al forn](recipe_arroz_al_horno.md) in technique.
*   **The Cullera Style:** Duck + **coastal vegetables** (artichoke, *ajos tiernos*). Rabbit is sometimes swapped for **pork ribs**.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Sueca** (duck + chicken frame, winter artichoke).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Duck, jointed (thighs + breasts with skin, carcass chopped) | 1 (≈ 1.6 kg gross) |
| Chicken thighs | 400 g |
| Olive oil | 40 ml (the duck fat does the rest) |
| Bajoqueta (flat green beans) | 150 g |
| Garrofó (fresh or soaked) | 100 g |
| Artichokes, quartered (winter) | 4 |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Saffron | 0.3 g |
| Water | 1.8 L |
| Salt | to taste |

**Extraction:**
1. Put the duck pieces **skin-side down** in the cold pan with the oil and render slowly over medium heat for **15 min**. Spoon off excess fat, keeping ≈ 80 ml in the pan.
2. Add the chicken and brown everything for **15 min** more.
3. Push the meat to the edge and fry the beans, garrofó and artichokes for **5 min**.
4. Add the tomato and fry until jammy (5 min).
5. Add the water and saffron. Simmer for **35–40 min**, reducing to ≈ 1.25 L.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short-grain rice (Senia / Bomba) | 400 g |
| Broth | ≈ 1.2–1.3 L |
| Duck | ≈ 1.6 kg gross |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Preparation:** Joint the duck the day before and salt it lightly (a dry brine).
2. **Fondo:** As above.
3. **Rice:** Scatter and level the rice. Don't stir after this.
4. **Fire:** High for 8 min → medium-low for 8–9 min → high for 1 min (socarrat).
5. **Finish/Resting Phase:** Rest **5 min**. [**Silla turnip variant:** in a cassola, use 3 peeled turnips in wedges instead of the beans and artichoke, with the same broth ratio at 2:1. Bake at 230 °C for 20 min.]
