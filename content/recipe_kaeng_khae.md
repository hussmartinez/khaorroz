# Kaeng Khae / แกงแค

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Northern Thailand (Lanna)](provincial_lanna.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Lanna villages and towns (Chiang Mai, Lamphun, Phayao, Nan).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: a thin, herbal jungle curry of many wild and garden vegetables with chicken, frog, beef, dried fish or snails, peppery rather than fiery; eaten with sticky rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A clay pot (*mo din*) over wood or charcoal, a stone mortar for the paste; sticky rice from the *huad*, served in a *kratip*.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The curry takes its name not from *dok khae* (the khae flower) but from *phak khae*, the northern name for wild betel leaf (*cha phlu*, *Piper sarmentosum*), one of its main ingredients ([Silpa-mag – แกงแค ทำไมไม่ใช่แกงดอกแค](https://www.silpa-mag.com/?p=362); [Wikipedia mirror – Kaeng khae](https://ipfs.ssi.eecc.de/ipns/en.wikipedia-on-ipfs.org/wiki/Kaeng_khae)). The paste pounds coriander root, salt, toasted *makhwaen* seeds, dried chilli, garlic, shallot, galangal, lemongrass and pla ra; the vine *thao chakkhan* gives a signature flavour, and compared with central jungle curry it is less hot but more herbal ([Silpa-mag](https://www.silpa-mag.com/?p=362)). Eaten with sticky rice it makes a complete meal ([GotoKnow](https://www.gotoknow.org/posts/416120)).

**Culinary science:** the many leaves are added in order of toughness so that each just wilts; the toasted makhwaen gives a numbing, citrusy note like Sichuan pepper.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Kaeng khae kai / kop:** with chicken or frog ([Wikipedia mirror](https://ipfs.ssi.eecc.de/ipns/en.wikipedia-on-ipfs.org/wiki/Kaeng_khae)).
*   **Kaeng khae yot maphrao:** with young coconut shoots, sweeter ([GotoKnow](https://www.gotoknow.org/posts/416120)).
*   **Kaeng pli tan:** palm-blossom curry made the same way ([GotoKnow](https://www.gotoknow.org/posts/416120)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: kaeng khae kai (chicken).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chicken pieces | 500 g |
| Paste: dried chillies, shallots, garlic, galangal, lemongrass, coriander root, makhwaen, salt | ≈ 4 tbsp |
| Pla ra | 2 tbsp |
| Mixed vegetables (wild betel leaves, ivy gourd, long bean, pea eggplant, acacia shoots, mushrooms) | 600 g |
| Thao chakkhan vine (if available) | 1 stem |
| Water | 1 L |
| Glutinous (sticky) rice, soaked 6–8 h | 300 g |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Sticky rice | 300 g raw |
| Vegetables : meat | ≈ 1.2 : 1 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** Soak the sticky rice 6–8 h, drain, and steam it in a woven bamboo *huad* over a pot of boiling water for 20–25 min, flipping the mass once halfway; keep it warm in a lidded *kratip* basket.
2. **Paste & meat:** stir-fry the paste briefly, add chicken and pla ra, cover with water and simmer 15 min.
3. **Vegetables:** add the vegetables from hardest to softest, finishing with the leaves; simmer until just tender.
4. **Serve:** with sticky rice.
