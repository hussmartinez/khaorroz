# Fattet Djaj / فتة الدجاج

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Syria](provincial_syria.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md) · [Jordan](provincial_jordan.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** fatteh is a broad family with regional disagreement; some Syrian versions use only bread, others rice and bread. The rice-and-bread layered form is the one documented here.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Syria, Palestine, Lebanon and Jordan.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Layered platter: crisp fried or toasted bread, rice cooked in chicken broth, shredded chicken, then a garlicky yogurt sauce and toasted nuts. Soft and crunchy in the same spoonful.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A stock pot for the chicken, a rice pot, a frying pan for the bread and nuts; served on a large platter.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Fatteh* means "breaking bread": "a long list of Levantine dishes that use crumbled flatbreads". The chicken version adds rice. A Syrian recipe describes "a layered chicken platter that consists of fried (or toasted) pita bread, rice and chicken with a delicious garlic-yogurt-lime sauce and topped with toasted nuts". A Palestinian source calls *fattet dajaj* "a common weekend family meal", layered "yogurt, rice and crispy fried bread". Lebanese versions use chickpeas and a tahini-yogurt sauce.

It is the chicken cousin of Gaza's lamb [fatteh ghazawiyya](recipe_fatteh_ghazawiyya.md) and of the Chilean-Palestinian [fate](recipe_fate_chilena.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Syria (Damascus, layered):** Bread, rice, chicken, garlic-yogurt-lime sauce, nuts.
*   **Palestine:** Weekend dish; yogurt, rice and fried bread, with chicken from the broth that cooked the rice.
*   **Lebanon:** Chickpeas and a tahini-yogurt sauce, sometimes no rice.
*   **Bread-only versions (⚠️):** Some Syrian sources omit rice entirely.

### Shami (Damascene) style with a rice layer
*(Merged from the Syria research.)*

*   **Shami household version:** Chicken boiled with bay, cardamom and onion; rice (with or without minced meat); garlic-tahini yogurt; nuts and parsley.
*   **With cooked yogurt:** Some recipes add starch and egg to the yogurt and heat it ([Mawdoo3 – فتة الدجاج باللبن](https://mawdoo3.com/طريقة_عمل_فتة_الدجاج_باللبن)).
*   **Shawarma fatteh:** A modern Syrian street version uses chicken shawarma ([Mawdoo3 – فتة الشاورما السورى](https://mawdoo3.com/فتة_الشاورما_السورى)).
*   **Elsewhere:** Gaza's [fatteh ghazawiyya](recipe_fatteh_ghazawiyya.md) uses lamb and a fiery *dugga*; Damascus is also famous for chickpea fatteh (no rice).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Syrian-Palestinian layered fattet djaj.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Whole chicken or thighs | 1.2 kg |
| Onion, bay leaf, cardamom, cinnamon stick | 1, 2, 3, 1 |
| Water | 1.5 L |
| Arabic flatbread, cut in squares | 2 large |
| Plain yogurt | 600 g |
| Garlic, crushed + lemon juice | 3 cloves + 2 tbsp |
| Tahini (optional) | 2 tbsp |
| Pine nuts + almonds | 30 g + 30 g |
| Butter or ghee | 50 g |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice (Egyptian or basmati) | 300 g |
| Chicken broth for the rice | 450 ml |
| Bread : rice : chicken | 1 layer each |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Broth:** Simmer the chicken with the onion and whole spices in the water **50 min**; lift out and shred.
2. **Rice:** Fry the rinsed rice in half the butter, add 450 ml of the hot broth, cook covered 15 min, rest 10 min.
3. **Bread:** Fry or toast the bread squares until crisp.
4. **Sauce:** Whisk the yogurt with garlic, lemon (and tahini), salt.
5. **Layer:** On a platter: bread, moistened with a little broth; rice; chicken; yogurt sauce.
6. **Finish:** Fry the nuts in the remaining butter and pour them over, sizzling.
