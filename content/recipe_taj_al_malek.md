# Taj al-Malek (Stuffed Lamb Ribs) / تاج الملك

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented as a Bethlehem Christmas centrepiece in one press feature (Middle East Eye, on the cook Nisreen Zeineh). I found no published Palestinian recipe with quantities, so the master recipe is reconstructed from the standard *hashweh* technique and generic Arab stuffed-rib recipes.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bethlehem (the Anatreh quarter of the Old City) and neighbouring Christian towns.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Feast rice baked inside a rack of lamb. A slit breast and rib section, the "king's crown", is packed with *hashweh* (rice, minced meat and nuts) and slow-roasted until the rice is tender, fragrant and soaked with lamb fat.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A deep pot to pre-poach the ribs, then a covered roasting tray in the oven or bakery oven.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Middle East Eye's report on Bethlehem Christmas dinners describes *taj al-malek* as "lamb ribs filled with rice and minced meat stuffing", the centrepiece of Nisreen Zeineh's Christmas table. She says it was adopted for Christmas because it "represents joy and celebration". The same feature notes that *hashweh*, the rice and minced-meat stuffing, is "saved for special occasions like Christmas" ([Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/discover/christmas-dinner-palestine-bethlehem-dinner)). It belongs with the other Palestinian feast rices, [qidreh](recipe_qidreh.md), [djaj mahshi](recipe_djaj_mahshi.md) and [mansaf](recipe_mansaf.md).

Stuffed lamb ribs are a wider Middle Eastern festive technique: Gulf cooks make *dil' kharuf mahshi bil-ruz* ([Just Food](https://www.justfood.tv/amp/وصفات/4317/طريقة-عمل-ضلع-الخروف-المحشي-بالأرز)), and Şanlıurfa stuffs ribs with rice, liver and raisins ([Şanlıurfa municipality](https://sanliurfa.bel.tr/lang/en/icerik/28/25/stuffed-ribs)). The Bethlehem name, *taj al-malek*, and its Christmas role are local.

**Culinary science:** the bone side shields the rice from direct heat, and the fat cap bastes it, so the stuffing steams in lamb juices for 1.5–2 hours without drying out. Covering the tray for most of the cooking is essential.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Bethlehem (Zeineh family):** Lamb ribs with rice-and-mince *hashweh*, the Christmas centrepiece.
*   **Gulf-style cousin (not Palestinian):** Ribs rubbed with spices and tamarind or date molasses, with egg and nuts in the stuffing.
*   ⚠️ Other Palestinian towns are undocumented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Bethlehem Christmas ribs (reconstructed).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Lamb breast and ribs in one piece, with a pocket cut by the butcher | ≈ 1.8 kg |
| Water + onion + cinnamon stick + 4 cardamom pods + bay leaf (pre-poach) | 2.5 L |
| Seven-spice, salt, pepper (rub) | 2 tsp / to taste |
| Samneh or butter | 40 g |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Medium-grain rice, soaked 20 min | 300 g |
| Minced lamb | 200 g |
| Pine nuts + almonds, toasted | 50 g + 50 g |
| Lamb broth (from the pre-poach) for half-cooking the rice | 350 ml |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Simmer the ribs in the spiced water for **45 min**. Keep the broth.
2. Brown the mince in the samneh. Add the rice, half the nuts and the seven-spice, then 350 ml of broth, and cook covered for **8 min** (half-cooked).
3. Fill the pocket loosely, sew or skewer it shut, rub the outside with spices and set it in a tray with 250 ml of broth.
4. Cover with foil and bake at 180 °C for **1 h 15 min**. Uncover and brown at 220 °C for **15 min**.
5. Cook the leftover stuffing with 1.5× its volume of broth for 15 min as a bed of rice.
6. Rest for 10 min, slice between the ribs and serve on the rice with the remaining nuts.
