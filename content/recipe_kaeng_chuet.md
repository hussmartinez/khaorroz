# Kaeng Chuet Tao Hu Mu Sap / แกงจืดเต้าหู้หมูสับ

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand and Bangkok (Chinese-Thai influence); everyday nationwide.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: a mild clear soup of minced pork, soft tofu and Chinese cabbage, the gentle balancing bowl in a rice meal.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A pot; served in a communal bowl.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Kaeng chuet* means 'bland curry', a clear soup ([Thailandblog](https://www.thailandblog.nl/en/eat-Drink/thai-clear-soup-gang-jued/)). Thai clear soups are designed as the middle ground of a Thai meal, balancing highly seasoned dishes on the same table ([ThaiFoodMaster – Introduction to Thai clear soups](https://thaifoodmaster.com/courses/introduction-to-thai-clear-soups)). A common version adds minced pork (*mu sap*) and tofu (*tao hu*) to a broth with Chinese cabbage, spring onion and coriander ([Thailandblog](https://www.thailandblog.nl/en/eat-Drink/thai-clear-soup-gang-jued/)). It is always served with steamed rice and other dishes, never as a starter.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **With glass noodles (*wun sen*):** a popular addition.
*   **With winter melon or bitter gourd:** Chinese-Thai variants.
*   **With seaweed and egg:** modern home version (⚠️ general).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Pork or chicken stock | 1.2 L |
| Minced pork, seasoned with soy, pepper, garlic | 250 g |
| Soft (egg) tofu, sliced | 1 tube |
| Chinese cabbage | 200 g |
| Garlic oil, spring onion, coriander | to finish |
| Light soy sauce, white pepper | 2 tbsp, ½ tsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) | 400 g, rinsed (+ 520 ml water, absorption method) |
| Soup per person | ≈ 300 ml |
| Rice per person | ≈ 180 g cooked |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Bring the stock to a simmer; drop in small balls of seasoned pork.
2. Add cabbage and cook 3 min; add tofu gently.
3. Season with soy and pepper; finish with garlic oil, spring onion and coriander.
4. Cook the rice: rinse 400 g jasmine rice until the water runs nearly clear, add 520 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min, then rest 10 min covered (or use an electric rice cooker / *mo hung khao*).
5. Serve the soup in the middle of the table with rice and other dishes.
