# Província d'Alacant / Provincia de Alicante

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [València](provincial_valencia.md) · [Castelló](provincial_castellon.md)

## 1. Overview
Alicante grows almost no rice, yet it may have the greatest **diversity** of rice dishes in the Comunitat. Alicante city markets itself as the **"Ciudad del Arroz"**. Its cooking divides into three worlds:

1.  **The coast** (L'Alacantí, Marina Alta/Baixa, Santa Pola), built on **fumet** and the **ñora**-based **salmorreta**. Here you find arroz a banda, senyoret, caldero and arroz negro.
2.  **The dry interior** (Vinalopó, Alcoià), with **sarmiento**-fired rabbit and snail rices from the wine country of Pinoso, Villena, Monòver and Petrer.
3.  **La Vega Baja del Segura**, the Segura's irrigated huerta. Here you find arroz con costra, artichoke rices, serranas snails, pava borracha, and strong Murcian cross-influence (Calasparra rice, ñora).

The **ñora** (a dried round sweet pepper, grown especially around Guardamar and the Segura basin) is the province's culinary signature.

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **L'Alacantí** | Salmorreta seafood rices, arroz con pata de ternera | Alicante, El Campello, Sant Vicent, Mutxamel |
| **La Marina Alta & Baixa** | Two-course a banda, caldosos, fesols i naps | Dénia, Xàbia, Calp, La Vila Joiosa, Pego |
| **El Baix Vinalopó** | Arroz con costra (Elche), caldero (Santa Pola/Tabarca) | Elx, Crevillent, Santa Pola, Tabarca |
| **El Vinalopó Mitjà & Alt Vinalopó** | Sarmiento rabbit-and-snail rice | Pinoso, Monòver, Novelda, Petrer, Villena, Biar |
| **L'Alcoià / El Comtat** | Wood-fired rices, olleta country | Castalla, Alcoi, Ibi |
| **La Vega Baja del Segura** | Arroz y costra, conejo y serranas, artichoke rices, pava | Orihuela, Almoradí, Redován, Callosa, Cox, Torrevieja |

### La Vega Baja in focus
The Vega Baja is a distinct culinary micro-region. It was part of the Kingdom of Murcia's orbit for centuries and speaks mostly Castilian rather than Valencian. Its huerta is irrigated by Andalusi acequias from the Segura. Its rice school is defined by **Calasparra rice** (D.O., from the neighbouring Murcian mountains), **ñora picadas**, **serranas** snails gathered in the Sierra de Callosa–Orihuela, the **Alcachofa de la Vega Baja**, **embutido oriolano** (longaniza blanca and roja), Christmas **pelotas**, and Almoradí's **pava borracha**.
