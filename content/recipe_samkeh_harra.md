# Samkeh Harra Trablousieh / سمكة حرة طرابلسية

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the Tripoli origin of *samkeh harra* is stated by several recipe sources; its serving with rice is described by some and contradicted by others (pita, cold mezze). The rice pairing documented here is the hot, main-course way of eating it; *tajen* is treated as a related dish.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Tripoli and the North Lebanon coast (al-Mina), spread to Beirut, Saida and the wider Levant.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: a whole fish (or fillets) stuffed and coated with a hot chilli, garlic and coriander paste, baked under a tahini sauce; served hot over vermicelli or white rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A baking tray in the oven (once the bakery oven); a pot for the rice.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Samkeh harra* ("spicy fish") is a coastal dish credited to Tripoli, Lebanon's second city: a large sea bass or grouper filled with an herb stuffing of garlic, chilli and coriander, covered in tahini and baked, served at banquets and celebrations ([Reader's Digest Canada – samkeh harra](https://www.readersdigest.ca/?p=19985); [Transparent Arabic blog – Learn the Lebanese way to cook a hot spicy fish](https://blogs.transparent.com/arabic/?p=8183)). Recipe writers describe the tahini sauce spiced with coriander, garlic and Aleppo pepper and list Lebanese vermicelli rice and plain white rice as the most traditional pairings, though another source says Tripoli eats it stuffed in pita ([Unicorns in the Kitchen – Samke harra](https://www.unicornsinthekitchen.com/samke-harra/); [Feel Good Foodie – Samke harra](https://feelgoodfoodie.net/recipe/samke-harra/)).

Its gentler relative is ***tajen samak***: white fish under thinly sliced fried onions and tahini sauce, served cold as mezze or, hot, with rice ([Nigella.com – Samiam's tajen](https://www.nigella.com/recipes/members/samiams-tajen-white-fish-with-sesame-paste); [Gulf News – Lebanese tajen samak](https://gulfnews.com/food/cooking-cuisines/perfect-summer-meal-lebanese-tajen-samak-1.1658841666035)). For fish cooked *in* rice, see [sayadieh](recipe_sayadieh.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Tripoli (samkeh harra trablousieh):** whole fish stuffed with chilli-garlic-coriander paste, tahini on top, served hot; with rice or in pita.
*   **Beirut restaurants:** fillets in a spicy tahini sauce, often with pine nuts.
*   **Tajen (Lebanon generally):** fried onions and tahini, milder; cold mezze or hot with rice ([Nigella.com](https://www.nigella.com/recipes/members/samiams-tajen-white-fish-with-sesame-paste)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Tripoli whole fish, served hot with vermicelli rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Whole sea bass or grouper, cleaned | 1.2–1.5 kg |
| Garlic | 8 cloves |
| Fresh red chillies (or Aleppo pepper) | 2 (or 2 tsp) |
| Coriander leaves | 1 bunch |
| Walnuts, chopped (optional) | 40 g |
| Tahini | 150 g |
| Lemon juice | 80 ml |
| Olive oil | 60 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Long- or medium-grain rice | 300 g |
| Vermicelli | 40 g |
| Water for the rice | 500 ml |
| Tahini : lemon : water in the sauce | 3 : 2 : 2 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Paste:** pound the garlic, chilli, coriander (and walnuts) with salt; fry briefly in half the oil.
2. **Fish:** score the fish, rub with salt and lemon, fill the cavity with half the paste and roast at 200 °C about 25 minutes.
3. **Sauce:** whisk tahini, lemon and water to a pourable sauce, stir in the rest of the paste; pour over the fish and bake 10 minutes more.
4. **Rice:** toast the vermicelli in oil or butter, add the rinsed rice and boiling salted water, cover and cook on lowest heat 18 minutes; rest 10 minutes.
5. **Serve:** fish and sauce on a platter with the rice alongside, coriander and lemon to finish.
