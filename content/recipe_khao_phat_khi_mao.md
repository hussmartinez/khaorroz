# Khao Phat Khi Mao / ข้าวผัดขี้เมา

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok and Central Thailand, at cooked-to-order (*ahan tam sang*) stalls.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Fried rice: "drunkard's" fried rice, fiery with chili, garlic, green peppercorn and holy basil — the rice version of the noodle *phat khi mao*.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok (*kratha*) over high heat; a mortar (*khrok*) for the chili-garlic paste.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Khi mao* means "drunkard"; the dish contains no alcohol. Explanations are anecdotal: it suits drinkers' dulled palates, or it was cooked from leftovers after a night out ([Wikipedia – Drunken noodles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drunken_noodles)). The rice version uses the same base — chili, unripe peppercorns and holy basil — as the noodle dish ([Pholfoodmafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=21109); [Amporn Oleski](https://amporn-oleski.blogspot.com/2011/10/drunken-fried-rice-shrimp.html)). ⚠️ Origin stories are unverified.

**Culinary science:** green peppercorns and holy basil add resinous heat on top of chili; the rice is fried hard so it chars slightly and takes up the paste.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Seafood (*thale*):** with prawns and squid, crisp basil and fried egg ([Pholfoodmafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=21109)).
*   **Pork or chicken:** everyday stall versions.
*   **Noodle original:** see *phat khi mao* with wide rice noodles ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drunken_noodles)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Seafood with fried egg**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Cooked jasmine rice, day-old | 800 g |
| Prawns + squid | 200 g + 150 g |
| Garlic + bird's-eye chilies, pounded | 6 cloves + 8 |
| Green peppercorns on the stem | 4 sprigs |
| Holy basil | 1 cup |
| Oyster sauce / fish sauce / sugar | 2 tbsp / 1 tbsp / 1 tsp |
| Eggs (fried, to top) | 4 |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice | 400 g raw, cooked a day ahead |
| Seafood per plate | ≈ 90 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** cook 400 g jasmine rice with 520 ml water the day before; chill.
2. **Paste:** pound garlic and chilies.
3. **Fry:** fry the paste in hot oil, add seafood and peppercorns until just cooked.
4. **Rice:** add rice and sauces; toss on maximum heat until slightly smoky.
5. **Finish:** fold in the basil; top each plate with a crisp fried egg.
