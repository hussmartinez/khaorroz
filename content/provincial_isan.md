# Isan (Northeast) / ภาคอีสาน

> ⬅ [README](README.md)

## 1. Overview
Isan, the Khorat plateau between the Mekong and Cambodia, is culturally Lao and the heartland of **glutinous rice**. Most Isan families grew up farming sticky rice or working the paddies. The rice is soaked, steamed in a woven-bamboo *huad* over a pot, and served from a bamboo basket, the *kratip khao* or *kong khao* ([SBS Food](https://www.sbs.com.au/food/article/embracing-four-generations-of-isan-sticky-rice/9b7wl1hdh); [Penn Museum – sticky-rice basket from Udon Thani](https://www.penn.museum/collections/object/126844)). Meals are built for sharing: sticky rice with *som tam*, grilled chicken and larb ([Michelin Guide – What is larb](https://guide.michelin.com/th/th/article/features/what-is-larb-the-unsung-hero-of-thailand-s-isan-cuisine)). Rice works as a staple, a seasoning (*khao kua*) and a thickener (*khao buea*).

The rice year is woven into the **Heet Sip-Song**, the twelve monthly merit-making customs. In the third month, villagers offer egg-glazed grilled sticky rice (*khao jee*) to monks at *Boon Khao Jee* ([GotoKnow – Heet Sip-Song](https://www.gotoknow.org/posts/51539); [Tim's Thailand – Boon Khao Jee](https://www.timsthailand.com/boon-khao-jee/)). After the harvest, young farmers once gathered at night to pound *khao mao* from green rice, a custom that is now fading ([GotoKnow](https://www.gotoknow.org/posts/422914)).

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **Khorat (Nakhon Ratchasima)** | Gateway to Isan; *khao taen* with watermelon juice also made here | Khorat |
| **Khon Kaen & the centre** | Sticky rice, som tam, larb with khao kua | Khon Kaen, Maha Sarakham, Kalasin |
| **Udon Thani & the north** | Kratip baskets; rainbow *khao taen*, *khao mao* cakes | Udon Thani, Nong Bua Lamphu |
| **Ubon & Amnat Charoen** | Lao-Mekong cooking; late-harvest *khao mao* | Ubon Ratchathani, Amnat Charoen |
| **Roi Et & Yasothon** | Organic jasmine and sticky-rice farming | Roi Et, Yasothon |
| **Sakon Nakhon & Nakhon Phanom** | Phu Tai and Lao Mekong traditions | Sakon Nakhon, Nakhon Phanom |
| **Nong Khai & the Mekong** | Lao cross-river sticky-rice culture | Nong Khai, Loei |
| **Southern Isan (Khmer, Kui)** | Surin, Buriram, Sisaket: Khmer-influenced cooking | Surin, Buriram, Sisaket |
