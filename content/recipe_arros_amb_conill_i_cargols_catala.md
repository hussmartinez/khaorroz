# Arroz con Conejo y Caracoles Catalán / Arròs amb Conill i Cargols

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Catalunya](provincial_catalunya.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the recipe is from the Diputació de Barcelona's *Menja saludable* recipe card. Catalan town-by-town variations are **not documented** in the sources found. The variations listed are general cross-regional comparisons, not Catalan sub-regional ones.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Inland Catalonia (Barcelona province and the interior comarques).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). Cooked 18–20 min "a foc viu" in a cassola and rested before serving.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A *cassola* (earthenware casserole) over gas or wood.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Rabbit and snails is a rice combination shared across eastern Spain's dry interiors, from the Alicante [serrana rice](recipe_arroz_conejo_y_caracoles.md) to [Aragón](recipe_arroz_con_conejo_y_caracoles_aragones.md). The Catalan version stands out for three things: it is made in a **cassola** rather than a paella, it includes **green beans** (*mongeta tendra*), and it uses **nyora** (ñora) together with green pepper in the sofregit. That last detail is a south-eastern touch that has reached the Barcelona public-health recipe card. Catalonia's snail culture (*cargolades*, the Lleida *Aplec del Caragol*) supplies the four dozen snails the recipe calls for.

**Culinary science:** the snails are started in **cold water over low heat** so they emerge from their shells before dying. Any that stay closed are discarded, which is a simple freshness test. The rabbit is browned first and removed, so the sofregit fries in rabbit-flavoured oil.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Diputació de Barcelona version (documented):** Rabbit, 4 dozen snails, nyora, green pepper, green beans, Bomba rice, saffron.
*   ⚠️ Catalan sub-regional variants (Lleida, Empordà, Penedès) are undocumented here. Compare the Lleida-Pyrenees [arròs de muntanya](recipe_arros_de_muntanya_catala.md).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Diputació de Barcelona**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rabbit, small pieces, salted & peppered | 1 |
| Clean snails (*cargols nets*) | 4 dozen |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 80 ml |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Ripe tomatoes, grated, skinless | 2 |
| Nyora (ñora), flesh | 1 |
| Green pepper | ½ |
| Green beans (*mongeta tendra*), in pieces | 200 g |
| Saffron | pinch |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Bomba rice | 400 g |
| Water | ≈ 1.2–1.3 L (≈ 3 : 1 for Bomba) ⚠️ the source says only "aigua" |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Snails:** Put them in cold water over low heat until they come out of their shells, then set aside. Discard any that don't.
2. **Rabbit:** Brown it in the cassola oil and remove it.
3. **Sofregit:** In the same oil, fry the garlic, then the tomato, green pepper and nyora flesh for a few minutes.
4. **Rice:** Add the rice and toast it. Add the water and stir, then add the snails, rabbit, green beans and saffron.
5. **Fire:** Cook **18–20 min** "a foc viu" (on a lively flame), then correct the salt.
6. **Rest:** A few minutes before serving.
