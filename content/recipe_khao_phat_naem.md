# Khao Phat Naem / ข้าวผัดแหนม

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand and Isan (wherever *naem* fermented pork is sold).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Fried rice: jasmine rice fried with sour fermented pork sausage (*naem*), egg and chili, tangy and garlicky.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok (*kratha*) over high heat.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Naem*, the sour fermented pork of Thailand and Laos, is also fried with rice; Leela Punyaratabandhu documents *khao phat naem* as a home and stall fried rice ([SheSimmers](https://shesimmers.com/2015/11/fried-rice-with-cured-pork-ข้าวผัดแหนม.html)). It shares its key ingredient with [yam naem khao thot](recipe_yam_naem_khao_thot.md). ⚠️ No history of origin found.

**Culinary science:** *naem*'s lactic sourness and garlic cut the richness of fried rice; it is added late so it warms without drying.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Plain with egg:** naem, garlic, egg, spring onion ([SheSimmers](https://shesimmers.com/2015/11/fried-rice-with-cured-pork-ข้าวผัดแหนม.html)).
*   **With bird's-eye chili and lime:** sharper Isan-leaning version ⚠️ (general practice).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Naem with egg**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Cooked jasmine rice, day-old | 800 g |
| Naem (fermented pork), crumbled | 200 g |
| Garlic, chopped | 5 cloves |
| Eggs | 3 |
| Fish sauce / sugar | 1 tbsp / 1 tsp |
| Bird's-eye chilies, spring onion | 3, 3 stalks |
| Oil | 4 tbsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice | 400 g raw, cooked a day ahead |
| Naem per plate | ≈ 50 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** cook 400 g jasmine rice with 520 ml water the day before; chill.
2. **Garlic and egg:** fry garlic, scramble the eggs.
3. **Rice:** add rice and toss until hot; season with fish sauce and sugar.
4. **Naem:** add the naem and chilies for the last minute.
5. **Serve:** with spring onion, cucumber and lime.
