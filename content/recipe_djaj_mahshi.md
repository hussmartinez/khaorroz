# Djaj Mahshi / دجاج محشي

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Throughout Palestine. It is a festive dish in Bethlehem, Beit Jala and Jerusalem homes, especially at Christmas.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Feast rice baked inside a bird. A whole chicken (or turkey at Christmas) is filled with *hashweh*, spiced rice browned with minced meat and pine nuts. The grains stay separate, glossy with butter and the bird's juices, under crisp skin.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A deep pot to poach the bird in spiced broth, then a roasting tray in the oven (historically the village *tabun* or the bakery oven).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Christiane Dabdoub Nasser's *Classic Palestinian Cuisine* gives *djaj mahshi* as a festive dish of chicken stuffed with meat, rice and pine nuts, whose success "depends greatly on the clever use of fresh spices", with one bird for every four people ([ckbk – Classic Palestinian Cuisine, stuffed chicken](https://app.ckbk.com/recipe/clas68442c08s001r004/stuffed-chicken)). The same book notes that although rice is not grown in Palestine, it is "unquestionably the basic ingredient in ceremonial dishes" ([ckbk – Rice Dishes](https://app.ckbk.com/section/clas68442c04s001/-rice-dishes)).

At Christmas the dish grows into a turkey or large chicken "stuffed with mince, spiced rice or freekeh, and nuts" ([Gulf News – Christmas in the Middle East](https://gulfnews.com/friday/food/christmas-in-the-middle-east-food-traditions-from-palestine-to-egypt-1.500385045)). In Bethlehem, the chef Fadi Kattan's family serves *hashweh* on Christmas Eve, and his grandmother added chestnuts to it ([Middle East Eye – Christmas dinner in Bethlehem](https://www.middleeasteye.net/discover/christmas-dinner-palestine-bethlehem-dinner)). The same stuffing also fills [mahshi](recipe_mahshi.md) and the [festive chicken rice](recipe_ruz_bi_djaj_hashweh.md).

**Culinary science:** the rice is only half-cooked before it goes into the bird. It finishes by absorbing the juices and rendered fat inside the cavity, so it becomes rich without turning mushy. Pre-poaching the chicken in spiced broth seasons the meat and leaves a stock for cooking the extra stuffing.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Bethlehem and Beit Jala (Christmas):** A turkey or large chicken. *Hashweh* with chestnuts (Kattan family) or with almonds and pine nuts. Served on Christmas Eve or Day.
*   **Freekeh version (villages and diaspora):** Green freekeh instead of rice, as in a Palestinian-American home recipe ([Falasteenie Foodie](https://falasteenifoodie.com/tag/palestinian-recipes/)). It is listed here as a variant only.
*   **One-pan modern version:** Pieces of chicken baked over a bed of *hashweh* instead of a stuffed whole bird ([Sour and Sweets](https://sourandsweets.com/?p=17983)).
*   ⚠️ No source documents finer town-level differences, for example between Nablus and Hebron.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: classic stuffed chicken (Dabdoub Nasser structure). Quantities are reconstructed.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Whole chicken | 1 (≈ 1.5 kg) |
| Lemon (to rub the bird) | 1 |
| Water for poaching | 2 L |
| Onion, cinnamon stick, cardamom pods, bay leaf | 1 / 1 / 4 / 1 |
| Butter (or samneh) | 40 g |
| Salt, white pepper, nutmeg | to taste |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short- or medium-grain rice (Egyptian/Calrose), soaked 20 min | 250 g |
| Minced lamb or beef | 200 g |
| Pine nuts + almonds, toasted | 40 g + 40 g |
| Seven-spice (*baharat*) | 1 tsp |
| Broth for the rice | 300 ml (rice only half-cooked) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Rub the chicken with the halved lemon, rest it for 30 min, rinse and drain. Season the inside with salt and nutmeg and the outside with salt and white pepper.
2. Brown the mince in the butter until its liquid evaporates. Add the drained rice, *baharat* and half the nuts, and stir for 2 min.
3. Add 300 ml of hot broth and cook covered for **8 min** until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is half-cooked.
4. Stuff the chicken loosely, because the rice swells, and truss it. Poach it in the 2 L of water with the onion and spices for **30 min**.
5. Lift the bird into a tray, brush it with butter and roast it at 200 °C for **35–40 min** until golden. Cook any extra stuffing in a pot with 1.5× its volume of the poaching broth for 15 min.
6. Rest the bird for 10 min and carve it over a platter of the extra rice. Scatter the remaining nuts and serve with yogurt.
