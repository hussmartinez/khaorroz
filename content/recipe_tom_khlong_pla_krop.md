# Tom Khlong Pla Krop / ต้มโคล้งปลากรอบ

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** An Ayutthaya origin is a restaurant claim only.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand (Ayutthaya and Bangkok canal country).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: a smoky, sour, spicy soup of dried or smoked fish with toasted dried chilies, tamarind, galangal and lemongrass, no coconut milk, eaten with rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A pot; dried chilies and shallots are first charred on a charcoal stove (*tao than*).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Tom khlong is a strongly flavoured, non-fatty Central Thai soup; snakehead (*pla chon*) is the favourite fish, often dried or smoked, with galangal and makrut lime leaf to mask fishiness and tamarind for sourness ([Wikipedia – Tom khlong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_khlong); [Department of Thai Traditional Medicine](https://ittm.dtam.moph.go.th/images/บทความสมนไพร/27samunprai.pdf)). The *pla krop* version uses crisp-dried or fried fish. A Bangkok restaurant brands itself as Ayutthaya-style, a claim we could not corroborate ([Wongnai](https://www.wongnai.com/restaurants/3842954rn-%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%87%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%88%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%8D%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%9F%E0%B8%AA-2-%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%B6%E0%B8%81-b-1)).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Pla krop / pla yang:** crisp-dried or smoked snakehead (classic).
*   **Tom khlong pla salit:** with dried gourami.
*   **With fresh fish:** a lighter variant.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Dried or smoked fish (snakehead), in pieces | 200 g |
| Water | 1 L |
| Dried chilies and shallots, charred | 6, 6 |
| Galangal, lemongrass, makrut lime leaves | 6 slices, 2 stalks, 5 |
| Tamarind water | 3 tbsp |
| Fish sauce, lime juice | 2 tbsp, 1 tbsp |
| Holy basil or Thai basil | 1 handful |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) | 400 g, rinsed (+ 520 ml water, absorption method) |
| Soup per person | ≈ 300 ml |
| Rice per person | ≈ 180 g cooked |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Char the dried chilies and shallots over charcoal or in a dry pan.
2. Boil the water with galangal, lemongrass, lime leaves and the charred shallots and chilies.
3. Add the fish and simmer 5 min; season with tamarind, fish sauce and lime.
4. Add basil off the heat.
5. Cook the rice: rinse 400 g jasmine rice until the water runs nearly clear, add 520 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min, then rest 10 min covered (or use an electric rice cooker / *mo hung khao*).
6. Serve with rice.
