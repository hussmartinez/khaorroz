# Phat Phrik Khing / ผัดพริกขิง

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand; sold in curry shops and made-to-order stalls nationwide.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: a sweet dry curry, red curry paste stir-fried without coconut milk with pork (often crispy pork belly) and long beans, spooned over rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok (*kratha*).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Phat phrik khing* is a dry curry stir-fry of meat and yardlong beans, sold in curry shops and fast-fry-to-order restaurants across the country; despite the name, it contains no ginger ([Wikipedia – Phat phrik khing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phat_phrik_khing)). Austin Bush documents the cooking at a Bangkok stall ([Austin Bush](https://www.austinbushphotography.com/blog/blog/how-to-make-phat-phrik-khing.html)); Leela Punyaratabandhu's version is sweet and dry ([She Simmers](https://shesimmers.com/2015/06/simple-thai-food-book-sweet-dry-curry-of-pork-and-green-beans-phat-phrik-khing.html)). ThaiFoodMaster calls the old-fashioned version a traveller's food, keeping well without refrigeration ([ThaiFoodMaster](https://thaifoodmaster.com/courses/phat-phrik-khing?lang=th)).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Phat phrik khing mu krop:** crispy pork belly.
*   **With dried fish or shrimp:** the old keeping version.
*   **With chicken or tofu:** modern variants.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Red curry paste (phrik khing paste) | 50 g |
| Pork belly or crispy pork, cubed | 300 g |
| Yardlong beans, in 4 cm pieces | 250 g |
| Oil | 3 tbsp |
| Fish sauce, palm sugar | 1½ tbsp, 1½ tbsp |
| Makrut lime leaves, finely shredded | 6 |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) | 400 g, rinsed (+ 520 ml water, absorption method) |
| Stir-fry per plate | ≈ 130 g |
| Rice per plate | ≈ 180 g cooked |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Fry the paste in oil until fragrant and darker.
2. Add the pork and stir-fry until cooked and coated.
3. Add the beans with a splash of water; stir-fry until just tender.
4. Season with sugar and fish sauce until sweet-salty; add lime leaves.
5. Cook the rice: rinse 400 g jasmine rice until the water runs nearly clear, add 520 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min, then rest 10 min covered (or use an electric rice cooker / *mo hung khao*).
6. Serve over rice.
