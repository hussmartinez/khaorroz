# Arroz con Chipirones y Ajos Tiernos / Arròs amb Xipirons i Alls Tendres

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província d'Alacant](provincial_alicante.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** L'Alacantí (Alicante city), with the same pairing common along the Alicante coast in spring.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Meloso** (creamy). 2 L of fumet go in gradually for 320 g of rice, with occasional stirring. This is the classification I've given it; the source doesn't use the word.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella pan on gas.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A spring rice of the Alicante coast. Small squid (*chipirones*, Valencian *xipirons*) are paired with **young garlic** (*ajos tiernos*), which appears in the huertas from late winter. Alicante city's tourism office publishes it in its "Ciudad del Arroz" recipe collection, and it uses the city's signature **salmorreta** (garlic, tomato and ñora). The garlic is sautéed and set aside rather than browned hard, so it keeps its green, grassy sweetness.

**Culinary science:** small squid release water and toughen if they stew. Searing them first over high heat and returning them later keeps them tender. Adding boiling stock gradually and stirring now and then releases surface starch for a creamy, bound texture, the opposite of the no-stir seco paella.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Alicante city (Turismo de Alicante):** Salmorreta base, stock added gradually, 10 min high + about 8 min low, short rest.
*   **Other coastal towns:** ⚠️ Not documented. Compare [arroz con sepia y alcachofas](recipe_arroz_con_sepia_y_alcachofas.md) (La Safor/Valldigna) and [arroz negro](recipe_arroz_negro.md) for other cephalopod treatments.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Turismo de Alicante "Ciudad del Arroz" (published quantities).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 75 ml |
| Fresh chipirones, cleaned | 500 g |
| Ajos tiernos (young garlic) | 1 bunch |
| Garlic, finely chopped | 2 cloves |
| Salmorreta (garlic, tomato, ñora) | 30 g |
| Fish stock / fumet, made in advance, **boiling** | 2 L |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 320 g |
| Stock : rice | ≈ 6 : 1, added gradually (not all used if it tightens early) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Sear:** Heat the oil. Sauté the chipirones, then the young garlic, and set both aside.
2. **Garlic:** Fry the chopped garlic until golden.
3. **Toast:** Add the rice and toast for about 2 min. Stir in the salmorreta for about 1 min.
4. **Return and liquid:** Return the chipirones and young garlic. Add the boiling stock gradually and season with salt.
5. **Fire:** **10 min high heat**, stirring occasionally, then low heat for about **8 min**.
6. **Rest:** A few minutes before serving.
