# Paella con Albóndigas / Paella amb Mandonguilles (Pilotetes)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the Gandia version comes from a cook's recipe written down from phone notes and memory by a food newsletter author, who admits "no tinc gaire clares les quantitats" (I'm not very clear on the quantities). A Universitat de València recipe collection lists *pilotetes* (meatballs) as an optional addition to the paella valenciana. Search results also mention Tavernes de la Valldigna's tourism leaflets as featuring paella with meatballs and snails, but I couldn't open those leaflets.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** La Safor (Gandia) and La Valldigna (Tavernes).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella on a tripod over a wood fire on the ground.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A southern-Valencian **festive paella** in which small **meatballs** (*mandonguilles* or *pilotetes*) join the rabbit and chicken. In winter they come with **artichoke** and *garrofó*. The meatballs are browned first and set aside so they don't break up. The Gandia cook who taught it calls it the traditional paella of her area, and it was cooked at a communal paella day in València city's centre before the Fallas.

**Culinary science:** browning the meatballs first sets their outer proteins so they hold together in the broth, and it adds fond to the pan. Their breadcrumb binder slightly enriches the broth's body.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Gandia (La Safor):** Rabbit + chicken + mandonguilles + artichoke + garrofó, about 70 g of rice per person. Water is added by eye.
*   **Tavernes de la Valldigna:** Paella with **meatballs and snails**. ⚠️ Mentioned in a search summary of the town's tourism leaflets, but I couldn't open them.
*   **Recorded València version:** *Pilotetes* are optional in the paella valenciana, added a few minutes before the rice.
*   **Murcian and Alicante cousins:** pelotas in [arroz con costra](recipe_arroz_con_costra.md) and [arroz con pava](recipe_arroz_con_pava.md).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Gandia (quantities reconstructed).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| **Mandonguilles:** pork mince 250 g, breadcrumbs 40 g, 1 egg, parsley, garlic, pine nuts, cinnamon pinch, salt | ≈ 16 small |
| Rabbit + chicken, in pieces | 1 kg |
| Artichokes, in quarters | 4 |
| Garrofó | 150 g |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Pimentón dulce | 3 g |
| Olive oil | 100 ml |
| Saffron, salt, water | to taste / ≈ 1.6 L |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | ≈ 280–320 g (≈ 70–80 g per person) |
| Broth at rice-in | ≈ 1 L |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Meatballs:** Oil and salt in the paella. Brown the mandonguilles and remove them.
2. **Meat:** Brown the rabbit and chicken, then push them to the rim.
3. **Vegetables:** Artichoke in the centre in concentric circles, then the garrofó.
4. **Sofrito:** Tomato and pimentón in the centre.
5. **Broth:** Add the water, stir, return the meatballs and simmer about 25 min.
6. **Rice:** When the level looks right, add the rice and saffron. High heat for 8 min, then medium-low for 9 min.
7. **Rest:** 5 min.
