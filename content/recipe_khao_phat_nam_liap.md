# Khao Phat Nam Liap / ข้าวผัดหนำเลี๊ยบ

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok's Chinese-Thai seafood restaurants and shophouses.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Fried rice: jasmine rice wok-fried with minced pork and salted black Chinese olives (*nam liap*), briny and savoury, served with lime, cucumber and spring onion.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok (*kratha*) over a high flame.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Nam liap* are Chinese salted black olives (not Mediterranean olives), a Teochew pantry staple eaten in Thailand with plain rice soup; fried with rice they make one of the common Chinese-style fried rices in Thailand ([Pholfoodmafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=20427); [Austin Bush](https://www.austinbushphotography.com/blog/blog/how-to-make-khao-phat-nam-liabข้าวผัดหนำเลี้ยบ.html)). Austin Bush notes it from Je Ngor, a Bangkok Chinese-Thai seafood chain. ⚠️ No source documents when or where it originated.

**Culinary science:** the olives' brine and fermented-fruit umami season the rice, so very little added salt is needed; frying them in garlic oil first spreads their aroma through the fat.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **With minced pork (standard):** garlic, nam liap, pork, rice ([Pholfoodmafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=20427)).
*   **Seafood version:** with prawns or squid in seafood restaurants ([Austin Bush](https://www.austinbushphotography.com/blog/blog/how-to-make-khao-phat-nam-liabข้าวผัดหนำเลี้ยบ.html)).
*   **Side dish rather than fried rice:** *mu sap phat nam liap* eaten with khao tom ([Pholfoodmafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=20356)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Minced pork**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Cooked jasmine rice, day-old | 800 g (from 400 g raw) |
| Salted Chinese olives (*nam liap*), chopped | 4 tbsp |
| Minced pork | 200 g |
| Garlic, chopped | 6 cloves |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Light soy / sugar | 1 tbsp / 1 tsp |
| Oil | 4 tbsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 400 g raw, cooked a day ahead |
| Olive : rice | ≈ 1 tbsp per plate |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** cook 400 g jasmine rice with 520 ml water the day before; chill and break up.
2. **Aromatics:** fry garlic in the oil until pale gold, add the nam liap 30 s.
3. **Pork and egg:** add pork, cook through; push aside and scramble the eggs.
4. **Rice:** add rice and toss on high heat until every grain is coated and hot; season with soy and sugar.
5. **Serve:** with lime, cucumber and spring onion.
