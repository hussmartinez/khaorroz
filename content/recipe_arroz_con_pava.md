# Arroz con Pava (Pavo) / Arròs amb Titot

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província d'Alacant](provincial_alicante.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** turkey rice is part of La Vega Baja's festive and post-Christmas cooking, but it is **poorly documented online**. My searches found no published town-specific recipe for "arroz con pavo" from Redován, Almoradí, Elche or Pinoso. The tradition that *is* well documented is **pava borracha** (Almoradí's wine-fattened turkey) and the Christmas **cocido con pelotas**, from which this rice derives. The variations below are reconstructed from that tradition and common comarca practice and are flagged as such. Please verify them with local cooks or a comarca cookbook before treating them as canonical.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** La Vega Baja del Segura (Almoradí, Orihuela, Redován, Callosa), with spill-over into El Baix Vinalopó (Elche, Crevillent).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Meloso** (creamy). Cooked in turkey-cocido broth, rich in gelatin. A dry paella version also exists.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A deep paella or perol over wood or gas.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
La Vega Baja has a long tradition of raising **Spanish Black turkeys**. In **Almoradí** the bird is finished on feed laced with **wine**, giving the famous **pava borracha** ("drunk turkey"), whose meat is richer and more marbled. At Christmas the turkey goes into the **cocido con pelotas**: large meatballs of minced turkey and pork, breadcrumbs, pine nuts, lemon zest and parsley, often wrapped in cabbage leaves. The days after Christmas bring the rice. The leftover turkey broth, meat and *pelotas* are turned into an arroz, either meloso in the perol or dry in the paella. Structurally it is a cousin of [arroz con costra](recipe_arroz_con_costra.md), which shares the pelotas, and of [arròs al forn](recipe_arroz_al_horno.md), which is also leftover-cocido logic.

**Culinary science:** turkey drumsticks and wings are collagen-rich, so a long-simmered cocido broth has a lot of body. A wine-fed bird contributes more intramuscular fat. The pelotas' breadcrumb and egg binder partly dissolves into the rice, thickening it.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial — reconstructed, verify locally)

*   **The Almoradí Style:** Uses **pava borracha**. Meloso in a perol. Broth from the Christmas cocido. **Pelotas** nested on top. Artichoke when in season.
*   **The Redován / Callosa Style:** The turkey is **browned fresh** in a paella (like rabbit in the [conejo y caracoles](recipe_arroz_conejo_y_caracoles.md) rice), with a **ñora picada** and sometimes **serranas** snails. Dry, thin layer.
*   **The Orihuela Style:** Turkey combined with **embutido oriolano** (longaniza blanca/roja). Sometimes **finished with egg** as an "arroz y costra de pavo".
*   **The Elche / Crevillent Style:** Turkey enters the **arroz con costra** frame in place of rabbit at Christmas.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Almoradí meloso**, made from cocido broth with pelotas.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Turkey drumstick + wing (pava borracha if available) | 1.2 kg |
| Pork bone / *espinazo* | 300 g |
| Chickpeas, soaked | 150 g |
| Potato, carrot, celery, turnip | 1 each |
| Water | 3 L |
| Saffron | 0.2 g |
| **Pelotas:** minced turkey 150 g + pork 150 g, breadcrumb 60 g, 1 egg, pine nuts 20 g, parsley, lemon zest, garlic, cinnamon pinch | 4–6 balls |
| Olive oil | 60 ml |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Ñora (optional) | 1 |

**Extraction:** Simmer the turkey, pork bone, chickpeas and vegetables for **2 h**. Add the pelotas for the last **25 min**. Strain and keep **1.5 L** of broth. Shred the turkey. Separately, fry the ñora (10 s) and then the tomato in the oil (5 min) in the perol.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short-grain rice | 350 g |
| Cocido broth | 1.4–1.5 L (≈ 4:1, meloso) |
| Shredded turkey | ≈ 400 g |
| Chickpeas | ≈ 200 g |
| Pelotas | 4–6 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Preparation:** Make the cocido the day before (Christmas Day, traditionally).
2. **Toasting:** Add the rice to the tomato sofrito and stir for 1 min. Add the turkey and chickpeas.
3. **Liquid Addition:** Boiling broth. Correct the salt.
4. **Fire:** Strong for 8 min → medium for 9 min, with one or two gentle stirs. Nest the pelotas on top at min 12.
5. **Finish/Resting Phase:** Rest 2 min. Serve with lemon.
