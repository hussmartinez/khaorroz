# Khanom Thuai (Khanom Thuai Talai) / ขนมถ้วย (ขนมถ้วยตะไล)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the method is well documented, but I found no documented origin.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand, sold at markets nationwide.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Rice-flour dessert steamed in tiny cups: a soft, chewy, sweet pandan-scented base topped with a salty, creamy coconut layer. Eaten warm or at room temperature.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** Small ceramic cups (*thuai talai*) set in a steamer over a charcoal stove.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Khanom thuai talai is an old-style sweet with two parts: a sweet, soft base and a salty, rich coconut top. It takes its name from the little *thuai talai* cups it is steamed in ([Phol Food Mafia – kanom tuay](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/recipe/kanom-tuay-%e0%b8%82%e0%b8%99%e0%b8%a1%e0%b8%96%e0%b9%89%e0%b8%a7%e0%b8%a2-%e0%b8%95%e0%b8%b2%e0%b8%a1%e0%b8%aa%e0%b8%b1%e0%b9%88%e0%b8%87)). The base uses rice flour, arrowroot (*thao yai mom*), mung-bean flour, pandan juice, coconut sugar and coconut milk; the top is rice flour, coconut cream and salt. The cups are pre-heated and filled three-quarters full; the base is steamed about 8 min, then the top is added and steamed about 5 min more ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/articles/3509979/)). A Siam University food-technology study analysed the dessert's texture ([Siam University Journal of Food Technology](https://e-library.siam.edu/e-journal/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Journal-of-food-technology-siam-university-vol13-no2-jul-dec-4.pdf)).

**Culinary science:** pre-heating the cups sets the batter edges at once, which gives the dessert its typical dip in the centre that the coconut top fills.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice flour (base) | 100 g |
| Arrowroot flour (base) | 20 g |
| Pandan juice (base) | 250 ml |
| Coconut sugar (base) | 120 g |
| Coconut milk (base) | 100 ml |
| Coconut cream (top) | 300 ml |
| Rice flour (top) | 30 g |
| Salt (top) | 1 tsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice flour total | 130 g |
| Cups | about 30 small *thuai talai* |
| Base : top | 3 : 1 per cup |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Heat the cups:** Steam the empty cups 5 min.
2. **Base:** Knead the flours with the pandan juice, then add the sugar and coconut milk; strain.
3. **Steam:** Fill the cups three-quarters full and steam 8 min.
4. **Top:** Whisk the coconut cream, rice flour and salt; spoon on and steam 5 min more. Cool slightly and unmould.
