# Arroz Cortijero de Jaén / Arroz Cortijero (Country-Estate Rice)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Andalucía](provincial_andalucia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the recipe comes from the Jaén provincial tourism site (*Jaén Paraíso Interior*), which gives ingredients and a method but **no town, history or texture**. Town variations are not documented. The Jaén rabbit rice mentioned below comes from the same site, which also lists it without quantities.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Province of Jaén (olive country, cortijos). Exact towns are undocumented.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). 1 L of water for 500 g of rice over about 25 min leaves the grains absorbed and loose.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wide pan or cazuela at the cortijo, over olive-wood fire or gas.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A rice of the **olive-grove cortijos** of Jaén, the world's largest olive-oil-producing province. What makes it unusual among Spanish rices is that its flavour comes from **cured pork (unaged serrano ham and chorizo)** and **green olives**, fried in a full glass of **spicy (*picante*) extra-virgin olive oil**, the bitter-peppery early-harvest style Jaén is famous for. It is quick, cheap and made from pantry stores, the kind of rice made for farmhands during the olive harvest.

**Culinary science:** chorizo releases paprika-coloured fat into the oil, so no saffron or colourant is needed. The olives add brine and bitterness, balancing the richness of the cured meat. Toasting the rice in that seasoned fat coats each grain before the boiling water goes in.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Jaén Paraíso Interior version (documented):** Ham, chorizo, onion, red pepper, green olives, garlic, parsley, spicy EVOO.
*   **Jaén rabbit rice (same source):** Rabbit, green pepper, mushrooms, artichokes and tomato, topped with roasted pepper strips and served lukewarm. ⚠️ No quantities given.
*   ⚠️ Other towns undocumented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Jaén Paraíso Interior** (quantities as published).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Spicy extra-virgin olive oil (Jaén) | 1 glass (≈ 150 ml) |
| Onions, chopped | 2 |
| Red peppers, in pieces | 2 |
| Unaged serrano ham, broken into pieces | 100 g |
| Chorizo, broken into pieces | 100 g |
| Pitted green olives | 100 g |
| Garlic, chopped | 3 cloves |
| Parsley | 1 small bunch |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 500 g |
| Boiling water | 1 L (2 : 1 by volume) |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Sofrito:** Fry the onion and pepper in the oil until the onion turns golden.
2. **Cured meats:** Add the chorizo and ham, then the olives, parsley and garlic.
3. **Toast:** Add the rice and sauté briefly.
4. **Liquid:** Pour in 1 L of boiling water and season.
5. **Cook:** About **25 min** (depending on the rice variety) until absorbed.
6. **Rest:** 3–5 min off the heat.
