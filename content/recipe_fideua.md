# Fideuá / Fideuà de Gandia

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

> ℹ️ **Not a rice.** Fideuà is made with short noodles (*fideus*) instead of rice. It is included here because it was born as a variant of the seafood paella, it is cooked in the same pan with the same broth logic, and in La Safor it is treated as a member of the rice family.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** La Safor (Gandia and its port, El Grau de Gandia), spread along the whole Valencian coast.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). The noodles absorb all the broth and the tips curl upward (*punta*) at the end.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A steel paella over a gas ring or wood fire. Originally it was cooked aboard a fishing boat.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The dish is dated to the first quarter of the 20th century, and the usual story places it on a Gandia fishing boat called the *Santa Isabel*. In the version told by the local gastronomic association, the boat's cook ran out of rice for the crew's seafood paella and used noodles instead. In another version, the rice was simply forgotten when the boat put to sea. Either way, the noodles took up the fish broth just as rice would, and the result spread from the boats to the port taverns. Since the 1970s Gandia has hosted an international fideuà contest (sources disagree whether it began in 1974 or 1975). The *Associació Gastronòmica Fideuà de Gandia* certifies restaurants that follow the "authentic" recipe.

**Culinary science:** durum-wheat noodles have no amylopectin-rich surface starch like short-grain rice, so they don't turn creamy. They hydrate quickly (about 8–10 min) and then fry slightly in the remaining oil once the broth has evaporated. That frying is what makes the tips curl. Gandia's recipe calls for **monkfish (*rape*)** and **Norway lobster (*cigala*)**, which enrich the fumet, and excludes clams and mussels because they release grey, salty water.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)

*   **The Gandia Canon:** *Fideo* nº 3–4 (thin to medium, **not hollow**, per most local sources). Monkfish, cigalas, prawns and sepia. Rockfish fumet. **No clams, no mussels.** Served with allioli.
*   **The "fideo gordo" school:** Some cooks prefer thick, **hollow** noodles (*fideo con alma*) that take up more broth. Sources disagree on which is more traditional.
*   **Dénia / Marina Alta:** Often finished **in the oven or under the grill** for the last minutes to force the tips up, and served with allioli and *salmorreta*.
*   **Fideuà negra:** Coloured with sepia ink, a coastal-restaurant variant parallel to [arroz negro](recipe_arroz_negro.md).
*   **Inland "fideuà de muntanya":** Rabbit, pork rib and mushrooms. A modern hybrid, not part of the Gandia canon.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Gandia canon** (fideo nº 3–4, monkfish + cigala, no bivalves).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rockfish fumet (see [arroz a banda](recipe_arroz_a_banda.md)) | 1.2 L |
| Monkfish (*rape*), in 3 cm cubes | 300 g |
| Cigalas (Norway lobster) | 4–8 |
| Red prawns | 8 |
| Sepia, diced | 200 g |
| Olive oil | 100 ml |
| Garlic | 3 cloves, minced |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Pimentón dulce | 3 g |
| Saffron | 0.2 g |

**Extraction:** Sear the cigalas and prawns for 1 min per side and set them aside. Sear the sepia until it is caramelised (6–8 min), then the monkfish for 1 min, and set the monkfish aside. Add the garlic, then the pimentón (5 s), then the tomato (5 min).

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Fideo nº 3–4 | 400 g |
| Fumet | ≈ 1.0–1.2 L (≈ 2.5–3:1 by volume) |
| Allioli | 150 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Toasting:** Fry the noodles in the sofrito oil for **2–3 min**, stirring, until they turn lightly golden.
2. **Liquid Addition:** Add the boiling fumet with the saffron, level the noodles and check the salt. Put the monkfish back in.
3. **Fire Manipulation:** High heat for 5 min, then medium for 4–5 min. Lay the shellfish on top at minute 6.
4. **Finish:** Once the broth has gone, give it 1–2 min more on the fire, or 2–3 min under a hot grill, so the noodles stand up (*punta*).
5. **Resting Phase:** Rest 3 min. Serve with allioli.
