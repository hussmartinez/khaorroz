# Kaeng Phet / แกงเผ็ด

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** No dated origin was found for the red curry itself; history below is general.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand; red coconut curries are eaten throughout the country.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: a red coconut-milk curry made from dried red chilies, with chicken, beef or pork and bamboo shoots or eggplant, spooned over jasmine rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok (*kratha*) or pot; a stone mortar (*khrok*) for the dried-chili paste.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Kaeng phet* ('spicy curry') is the generic Central Thai red curry: dried red chilies soaked and pounded with shallot, garlic, galangal, lemongrass, makrut lime zest, coriander root, toasted coriander and cumin seed and shrimp paste, then fried in coconut cream ([Wikipedia – Red curry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_curry)). The same paste is the base of [kaeng phet pet yang](recipe_kaeng_phet_pet_yang.md) and of the dry stir-fried [phat phrik khing](recipe_phat_phrik_khing.md).

Like all *kaeng*, it is a *kap khao*: in Thai, side dishes are called 'with rice', and the curry is meant to season a mound of plain rice rather than be eaten alone ([Wikipedia – Thai cuisine](https://ipfs.ssi.eecc.de/ipns/en.wikipedia-on-ipfs.org/wiki/Thai_cuisine)).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Kaeng phet kai no mai:** chicken with bamboo shoots, a home and market classic.
*   **Kaeng phet nuea:** beef, simmered longer; common in *khao kaeng* shops.
*   **Kaeng phet mu:** pork with Thai eggplant.
*   **With fruit (court-influenced):** pineapple, lychee or rambutan, as in the duck version.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Red curry paste | 60 g |
| Coconut milk, cream and thin milk separated | 600 ml |
| Chicken or beef, sliced | 400 g |
| Bamboo shoots (boiled) or Thai eggplant | 200 g |
| Fish sauce | 2 tbsp |
| Palm sugar | 1 tsp |
| Makrut lime leaves, Thai basil | 4, 1 handful |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) | 400 g, rinsed (+ 520 ml water, absorption method) |
| Curry per plate | ≈ 200 ml |
| Rice per plate | ≈ 180 g cooked |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Fry the coconut cream until the oil separates.
2. Fry the red paste until deep and fragrant (4–5 min).
3. Add the meat and coat; add the thin coconut milk and simmer until tender (chicken 8 min, beef 30–40 min).
4. Add bamboo shoots or eggplant and lime leaves; season with fish sauce and palm sugar.
5. Finish with basil.
6. Cook the rice: rinse 400 g jasmine rice until the water runs nearly clear, add 520 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min, then rest 10 min covered (or use an electric rice cooker / *mo hung khao*).
7. Serve over or beside rice.
