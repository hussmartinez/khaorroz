# Arroz del Pedrón de Parayo / Arroz de San Pedro con Anguilas y Cangrejos

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Norte](provincial_norte.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented in the *Revista Española de Nutrición Comunitaria*'s 2022 survey of **forgotten dishes of Cantabria**. The source itself says the quantities "no son fijas" (are not fixed), because each friend brought what they could. The amounts below are scaled from the source's large-batch list (1 kg rice) down to four people. Native river crayfish and European eel are now protected or heavily regulated. Use legal substitutes (American red crayfish, conger eel).

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Cantabria, in the Pas-Pisueña valleys around Villasevil (Santiurde de Toranzo) and the **Pedrón de Parayo** (a large stone meeting-place).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). Cooked in a paella with twice the broth of the rice, then rested.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella over an outdoor fire.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A rice of friendship and of **San Pedro** (29 June). In the **1940s–50s**, says the RENC source, a group of friends met by the Pedrón de Parayo to talk and rest from their daily work. "Two days before, some set lines to catch **eels**. The day before, others went to catch **crayfish** in Villasevil. Others **stole the tomatoes**. And with everything they brought, they made a rice dish." It is a rare **freshwater** rice of the Cantabrian north: river crayfish and eels, flamed with brandy, spiced with **clove** and pepper, and cooked in a paella.

**Culinary science:** the crayfish are gutted (*capar*: twist and pull the middle tail fin so the intestine comes out with it) to avoid bitterness. Flaming the brandy burns off the alcohol but leaves the aromatics. Clove, unusual in Spanish rices, pairs with the oily eel. White wine reduced over high heat adds acidity to cut the fat.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Pedrón de Parayo / Villasevil (RENC):** Eels **or conger**, crayfish, onion, firm tomatoes, white wine, brandy, clove, pepper. Twice the broth of the rice.
*   ⚠️ No other villages documented. Compare León's [arroz con cangrejos de La Bañeza](recipe_arroz_con_cangrejos_banezano.md).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **RENC 2022**, scaled from 1 kg of rice to 400 g.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Eels or conger, in pieces | 400 g |
| River crayfish, gutted | 400 g |
| Onions, finely chopped | 1.5 large |
| Tomatoes (not very ripe), peeled, diced | 2 |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Dry white wine | 80 ml |
| Brandy | 40 ml |
| Cloves | 2 |
| Black pepper, salt | to taste |
| Olive oil | 70 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 400 g |
| Broth (fish or water) | **2 × the rice** (≈ 0.8–1 L) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Crayfish:** Fry them in oil until they start to turn red. Add the brandy and **flame** it until it is consumed. Set aside.
2. **Sofrito:** In another pan, fry the onion, garlic and tomato. Season and add the cloves and pepper.
3. **Combine:** When the sofrito is done, add the crayfish, the white wine and the eel pieces. Keep over high heat until the wine reduces.
4. **Rice:** Pour everything into the paella, add the rice and sauté it.
5. **Broth & fire:** Add the broth. Cook **5 min on a high flame**, then **15 min on a lower one**.
6. **Resting:** Rest **5 min** and serve.
