# Arroz Negro / Arròs Negre

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md) · [Província d'Alacant](provincial_alicante.md) · [Província de Castelló](provincial_castellon.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** The whole Valencian coast. Strongest in the Valencia city fishing quarters (El Cabanyal, El Grau), La Safor (Gandia), La Marina Alta (Dénia) and the Castellón ports (El Grau de Castelló, Vinaròs).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry), with socarrat.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A steel paella over wood or gas. A modern variant is finished in the oven.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Arròs negre has been documented in Valencia since at least the 17th century. It is the fishermen's response to **cuttlefish** (*sepia*) and **squid** (*calamar*), whose ink sacs were too valuable as flavour to throw away. The ink (*tinta*) colours the rice jet black and adds a briny, iodine-rich, umami-heavy depth from its melanin-protein complexes and free glutamates. It is always served with **allioli**. The dish appears in Catalonia and the Balearics as well, but in Valencia it keeps a tight, pared-down form: sepia, ink, garlic, tomato, fish stock.

**Culinary science:** cephalopod ink is mostly melanin bound to proteins. It is heat-stable, so the colour survives long cooking, but the ink can taste metallic if scorched. That is why it goes in with the stock, not into the hot oil. Cuttlefish has firm collagen-rich flesh and is pre-seared hard to drive off water and caramelise its surface. Its released juices are part of the *fondo*.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)

*   **The Valencia City / El Cabanyal Style:** **Sepia** (cuttlefish) only, in small dice. Garlic + tomato + *pebre roig*. Fumet from rockfish. Ink from the sepia's own sac, sometimes supplemented with sachets. Allioli on the side.
*   **The Alicante / Dénia Style:** Uses **salmorreta** (ñora, garlic, tomato, parsley) as the base, and often adds **chipirones** or diced **calamar** plus peeled **gamba roja**. The colour is deeper and reddish-black.
*   **The Castellón / Grau Style:** Sometimes pairs the ink rice with **alcachofa de Benicarló** in winter (*arròs negre amb carxofa*), or with *galeres* (mantis shrimp). Lighter fumet.
*   **The La Safor / Gandia Style:** Onion is sometimes allowed in the base (unusual elsewhere in Valencia), and a little green pepper. Often finished with **ajos tiernos**.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Valencia city** (sepia + own ink + rockfish fumet). Alicante salmorreta substitution noted.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Fumet de roca (see [arroz a banda](recipe_arroz_a_banda.md) fumet) | 1.3 L |
| Whole cuttlefish with ink sac | 600 g gross (≈ 400 g cleaned) |
| Ink (own sacs + sachets if needed) | 8–12 g total |
| Olive oil | 80 ml |
| Garlic | 3 cloves, minced |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Pebre roig (sweet paprika) | 3 g |
| [Alicante: Salmorreta instead of tomato + paprika] | 60 g |

**Extraction:** Clean the cuttlefish carefully: remove the ink sac **intact** and set it aside in a little fumet. Dice the body into 1 cm pieces. Sear it in the hot oil until all its liquid evaporates and the edges brown (**8–10 min**). Add the garlic (30 s), then the pebre roig (5 s), then the tomato. Fry until the tomato is jammy (**5 min**).

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short-grain rice | 400 g |
| Fumet | 1.2–1.3 L |
| Ink | dissolved in 100 ml of the hot fumet |
| Allioli | 150 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Preparation:** Dissolve the ink in hot fumet and strain it through a fine sieve (no lumps).
2. **Toasting:** Add the rice to the fondo and toast for **2 min**.
3. **Liquid Addition:** Pour in the boiling fumet and the ink liquid and stir once to level. Correct the salt.
4. **Fire Manipulation:** High heat for **8 min** → medium-low for **8 min** → high for **1–2 min** for socarrat. (Optional modern finish: oven at 185 °C for the last 6–8 min.)
5. **Finish/Resting Phase:** Rest **3–5 min**. Serve with allioli. Lemon is not traditional.
