# Castilla-La Mancha y Extremadura / La Meseta Sur y las Vegas del Guadiana

> ⬅ [README](README.md)

## 1. Overview
These are two inland regions with very different relationships to rice. **Extremadura is Spain's second rice producer**, after Andalucía. The **Vegas del Guadiana** (Vegas Altas and Vegas Bajas) irrigate more than 25,000 ha of paddy. **Castilla-La Mancha** grows almost none, but its game culture (hare, partridge, rabbit) and its Lenten salt-cod tradition shape the rices it does cook. In the Albacete Sierra, a milk-free Christmas **arroz con miel** survives as a documented forgotten dish. The flavour markers are **pimentón de la Vera** (Extremadura), **saffron from La Mancha** (D.O. Azafrán de La Mancha), holm-oak fires and dehesa game.

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **La Mancha (Ciudad Real, Albacete plains, Toledo)** | Hare and partridge rices, Lenten cod | Ciudad Real, Valdepeñas, Albacete |
| **Serranía de Cuenca** | Lenten cod dishes | Cuenca |
| **Sierra de Alcaraz y Segura** | Arroz con miel (Christmas) | Bogarra |
| **Montes de Toledo** | Partridge | Los Yébenes |
| **Vegas del Guadiana (Extremadura)** | Paddy country: hare rice, pimentón | Don Benito, Villanueva de la Serena, Badajoz |
