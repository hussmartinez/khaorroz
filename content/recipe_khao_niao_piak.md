# Khao Niao Piak (Khao Niao Dam Piak, Piak Lamyai) / ข้าวเหนียวเปียก (ข้าวเหนียวดำเปียก, เปียกลำไย)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Nationwide; especially Central Thailand and the north (longan country).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Sticky rice dessert: glutinous rice (white, or black for *dam piak*) simmered in plenty of water until the grains burst and thicken it into a spoonable sweet porridge, sweetened with palm sugar and topped with salted coconut cream. Served warm.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A pot over a low charcoal fire, stirred often.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Piak* means "wet": this is sticky rice cooked to a pudding rather than steamed. *Khao niao piak lamyai* (with longan) is described as an old-style Thai sweet that is sweet, rich and refreshed by the fruit. It uses old glutinous rice, palm and white sugar, longan, water, coconut cream, salt and pandan ([TrueID Food](https://food.trueid.net/detail/yb0XJXWD38Nb)). Black glutinous rice gives the purple *khao niao dam piak*, often cooked with some white sticky rice for body and served with ripe mango or durian ([Phol Food Mafia – black sticky rice with mango](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=35392); [Phol Food Mafia – with durian](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=41194)).

**Culinary science:** glutinous rice starch is almost pure amylopectin, which swells and dissolves into the water when simmered for a long time, giving a thick, glossy body without any thickener.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Piak lamyai (longan):** a northern-season favourite ([TrueID](https://food.trueid.net/detail/yb0XJXWD38Nb)).
*   **Dam piak with mango:** black and white glutinous rice, coconut top and mango ([Phol Food Mafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=35392)).
*   **Dam piak with durian:** durian flesh folded in at the end ([Phol Food Mafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=41194)).
*   **Simple home version:** black rice boiled 25 min with brown sugar and a little coconut milk ([Wongnai](https://www.wongnai.com/recipes/ugc/2ffbb5205e004e85b6cf1ead144e6ae0)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: black sticky rice with coconut top.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Black glutinous rice | 180 g |
| White glutinous rice | 50 g |
| Water | 1 L |
| Pandan leaves | 3 |
| Palm sugar | 80 g |
| White sugar | 40 g |
| Salt | pinch |
| Coconut milk (top) + salt | 120 g + pinch |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice : water | about 1 : 4 |
| Serving | 1 bowl with 2 tbsp coconut top |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Soak:** Soak the black and white rice together for 1 h (or overnight).
2. **Simmer:** Boil with the water and pandan, then simmer over low heat, stirring often, for 40–60 min until the grains burst and the liquid is thick.
3. **Sweeten:** Stir in the palm sugar, white sugar and salt; cook 5 min.
4. **Top:** Warm the coconut milk with a pinch of salt. Serve the rice warm with the coconut spooned over (add mango or longan if you like).
