# Khao Man Kai Thot / ข้าวมันไก่ทอด

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** The fried variant is documented at shops and in the press but no history of when it appeared was found; treat the history paragraph as context.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok and the Central Plains, at Hainanese chicken-rice shops.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** One-plate rice: chicken-fat rice (*khao man*) topped with crisp fried chicken instead of poached, with a sweet chili dip.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** Rice pot (*mo hung khao*) for the chicken-fat rice; a wok of hot oil for the chicken.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
It is the fried-chicken branch of [khao man kai](recipe_khao_man_kai.md). Bangkok Hainanese shops such as Jae Yong near the Chao Pho Suea shrine sell both boiled and fried chicken over the same chicken-fat rice ([Bangkok Biz News](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/lifestyle/food/1087739)); "two-colour" plates with both kinds are a shop speciality ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/articles/3833724/)). Its popularity is shown by KFC Thailand's "khao man kai thot" menu ([Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/n/?p=36840)).

**Culinary science:** rice toasted in rendered chicken fat with garlic and ginger, then cooked in chicken stock, gives the savoury base; the crisp batter adds contrast the poached version lacks.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Bangkok Hainanese shops:** boiled and fried chicken on the same rice ([Bangkok Biz News](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/lifestyle/food/1087739)).
*   **Kai song si ("two-colour chicken"):** half boiled, half fried on one plate ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/articles/3833724/)).
*   **Fast-food version:** fried chicken over herb rice ([Naewna](https://www.naewna.com/n/?p=36840)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Hainanese shop, fried chicken**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chicken thighs, boneless | 600 g |
| Rice flour + tapioca starch | 60 g + 30 g |
| Garlic, coriander root, white pepper, fish sauce (marinade) | 4 cloves, 2, 1 tsp, 1 tbsp |
| Chicken fat (or oil) + garlic + ginger (rice) | 3 tbsp, 4 cloves, 3 slices |
| Chicken stock | 600 ml |
| Sweet chili sauce, cucumber, coriander | to serve |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice | 400 g raw |
| Stock for rice | ≈ 560 ml |
| Fried chicken per plate | ≈ 150 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Marinate:** rub chicken with pounded garlic, coriander root, pepper and fish sauce, 30 min.
2. **Rice:** fry rinsed, drained rice in chicken fat with garlic and ginger 3 min; add hot stock and cook covered until absorbed; rest 10 min.
3. **Fry:** dredge chicken in the flours and deep-fry at 170 °C until golden and cooked (8–10 min); slice.
4. **Serve:** rice in a dome, chicken on top, cucumber, coriander and sweet chili sauce.
