# Arroz y Habichuelas / Arroz con Habichuelas Murciano

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Región de Murcia](provincial_murcia.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** La Huerta de Murcia (the peñas huertanas around Murcia city, e.g. Algezares).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Caldoso** (soupy). A bean pot with the rice cooked in it.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A tall olla on the stove.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The Murcian huerta's equivalent of the Valencian [arròs amb fesols i naps](recipe_arroz_con_fesols_i_naps.md) and of the Vega Baja's [tres puñaos](recipe_arroz_de_los_tres_punaos.md). It is a **meatless** bean-and-vegetable pot made with **turnip**, **alcaciles** (Murcian for artichokes), **tabillas** (Murcian for green beans, in season), **habas**, **cardoon stalk** and potato, finished with a sofrito of a whole head of garlic, **ñoras**, *ajos tiernos* and tomato. The Ayuntamiento de Murcia and the Federación de Peñas Huertanas include it in their huerta cookbook *Un paseo por la huerta*, which measures it in handfuls: "1 puñado de habichuelas y medio puñado de arroz por persona".

**Culinary science:** the cookbook "scares" the beans with **cold water three times** when they come to the boil (*romper la cocción*). This traditional trick evens out cooking so the skins don't split before the centres are tender. Ñora fried with the garlic colours the oil red without burning paprika, and the paprika goes in off the heat.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Huerta de Murcia (Peñas Huertanas):** Habichuelas + turnip + alcaciles + tabillas, whole garlic head + 2 ñoras + ajos tiernos in the sofrito, colourant, potato. Rice added when the potatoes boil.
*   **Generic Murcian version (Cronista):** Adds **habas** (1 kg) and **penca de cardo**, green pepper. The beans cook 1.5 h. Recommends **Calasparra** rice. Rests 10 min.
*   **Vega Baja cousin:** [Arroz de los tres puñaos](recipe_arroz_de_los_tres_punaos.md) adds lentils.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Huerta de Murcia (Peñas Huertanas, handful measure converted).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Dried habichuelas (white beans), soaked overnight | 4 handfuls (≈ 320 g) |
| Turnip, peeled | 1 |
| Alcaciles (artichokes), cleaned & halved | 2 |
| Tabillas (green beans), in season | 8 |
| Potatoes | 2 medium |
| Whole head of garlic | 1 |
| Ñoras | 2 |
| Ajos tiernos | 4 |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 1 |
| Pimentón | 1 tbsp |
| Colourant / saffron, olive oil, salt | to taste |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice (Calasparra recommended) | 2 handfuls (≈ 160–200 g) |
| Liquid | Enough to stay soupy (≈ 2 L) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Beans:** Bring the soaked beans to the boil, then add cold water. Repeat **twice more**. Add the turnip and simmer.
2. **Sofrito:** Fry the garlic head and ñoras, then the ajos tiernos, alcaciles and tabillas. Add the tomato. Off the heat, add the pimentón, then tip everything into the olla.
3. **Potatoes:** Add them with the colourant. When they boil, add the rice.
4. **Rice:** Simmer **≈ 15–20 min**, watching it doesn't stick. Check the salt.
5. **Rest:** A few minutes (up to 10).
