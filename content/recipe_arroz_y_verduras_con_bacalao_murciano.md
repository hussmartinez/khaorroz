# Arroz y Verduras con Bacalao / Arroz Huertano de Verduras (Murcia)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Región de Murcia](provincial_murcia.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** La Huerta de Murcia.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). 400 g of rice in about 2 L of water, cooked in the paella for 15 min and rested. My classification is seco, though this much water may finish closer to meloso.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella on the stove.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A huerta vegetable rice with a characteristic Murcian touch: **salt cod that is toasted on the griddle** (*asado a la plancha hasta tostarlo*) and shredded, not soaked into the base. Artichoke, cauliflower, broad beans, green beans, young garlic, mushrooms, potato and red pepper are each fried **separately** in the paella and set aside, so every vegetable keeps its own texture and colour. The recipe comes from the Ayuntamiento de Murcia and Federación de Peñas Huertanas cookbook *Un paseo por la huerta*. It sits between the everyday [arroz huertano](recipe_arroz_con_costillejas_y_verduras_murcia.md) and the Lenten cod rices.

**Culinary science:** toasting dried salt cod caramelises its surface proteins, adding a smoky, savoury note the rice absorbs. The cod goes in for just the last 5 minutes so it doesn't toughen.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Peñas Huertanas (published quantities).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Fresh artichokes (hearts, in lemon water) | ½ kg |
| Cauliflower | 300 g |
| Broad beans, shelled | 150 g |
| Green beans | 150 g |
| Ajos tiernos | 1 bunch |
| Mushrooms | 100 g |
| Potato | 1 |
| Red pepper | ½ |
| Ripe tomatoes, grated | 2 |
| Pimentón | 1 tbsp |
| Salt cod (dry), toasted & shredded | 100 g |
| Saffron threads, olive oil, salt | to taste |
| Water | 2 L |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 400 g |
| Water | 2 L |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Prep:** Cut all the vegetables small and keep the artichoke hearts in lemon water. Toast the cod on a griddle and shred it.
2. **Fry:** Fry each vegetable separately in the paella and set aside. Fry the tomato with the vegetables.
3. **Paprika:** Add the pimentón, stirring so it doesn't burn, then the water.
4. **Boil:** 4–5 min, then add the rice, saffron and salt.
5. **Rice:** Cook **15 min**. Add the shredded cod at **min 10**.
6. **Rest:** A few minutes.
