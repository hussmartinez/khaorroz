# Arroz al Horno con Guisantes / Arròs al Forn amb Pèsols

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** recorded in a Valencian home-recipe collection hosted by the Universitat de València (uv.es/baldovi), with **no town attribution**.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Province of València (spring home cooking).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). Baked 18–20 min.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A flat earthenware cassola in the oven.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The **spring** version of [arròs al forn](recipe_arroz_al_horno.md). Instead of the winter chickpeas and cocido leftovers, it uses **fresh peas** (and optionally broad beans) from the huerta, cooked first in salted saffron water that then becomes the rice's stock. The usual sausage trio (**botifarra, blanquet and pork belly**) stays, plus 6–7 *alls tendres* (young garlic). It shows the seasonal logic of the baked-rice family: the vessel and oven stay the same, and the huerta decides the rest.

**Culinary science:** pea-cooking water is sweet and starchy, a light vegetable stock. Frying the rice with the garlic and paprika before it goes into the cassola seals the grains, so they stay separate in the oven.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Recorded version:** Peas + optional broad beans + botifarra + blanquet + pork belly + young garlic.
*   **Winter siblings:** [arròs al forn](recipe_arroz_al_horno.md) (chickpeas, cocido), [arròs al forn amb panses i cigrons](recipe_arros_al_forn_amb_panses_i_cigrons.md) (Lent), [arròs al forn de fesols i naps](recipe_arros_al_forn_de_fesols_i_naps.md).
*   ⚠️ Town variants are not documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: recorded version (published quantities).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Peas (fresh, shelled) | 500 g |
| Broad beans (optional) | 200 g |
| Water (for the peas) | 1 L |
| Botifarra, blanquet, pork belly slices | 1 each / 4 slices |
| Alls tendres | 6–7 |
| Olive oil | 200 ml |
| Pimentón | 1 tsp |
| Saffron, salt | to taste |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 400 g |
| Pea liquid | ≈ 0.8 L (adjust) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Peas:** Cook the peas (and beans) with salt and saffron in 1 L of water.
2. **Meats:** Fry the sausages and pork belly in oil and set them in the cassola.
3. **Rice:** Fry the garlic, then the rice, then add the pimentón.
4. **Assemble:** Put it in the cassola with the pea liquid and the peas. Adjust the salt and liquid.
5. **Oven:** **≈ 18–20 min** at 220–240 °C.
6. **Rest:** 5 min.
