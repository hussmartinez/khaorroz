# Mu Thot Kratiam Rad Khao / หมูทอดกระเทียมราดข้าว

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok and Central Thailand, at rice-and-stir-fry stalls.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** One-plate rice: pork fried with masses of garlic, coriander root and white pepper, laid over jasmine rice with a fried egg and cucumber.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok (*kratha*); a stone mortar (*khrok*) for the garlic-coriander root-pepper paste (*sam kloe*).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Called "a little-known classic", it rests on the old Thai trio of garlic, coriander root and white pepper (*sam kloe*) ([Hot Thai Kitchen](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/garlic-pepper-pork)). Thai cooks serve it over steamed rice with a fried egg, cucumber and coriander ([Pholfoodmafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=25976); [Rachel Cooks Thai](https://rachelcooksthai.com/fried-pork-with-garlic/)). ⚠️ No source dates it.

**Culinary science:** fry the garlic only to light gold — darker turns bitter; the garlic-scented oil is kept for frying the egg ([Hot Thai Kitchen](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/garlic-pepper-pork)).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Pork neck, sliced:** marinated in oyster and seasoning sauce ([Pholfoodmafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=25976)).
*   **Pork butt:** preferred for fat by some cooks ([Hot Thai Kitchen](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/garlic-pepper-pork)).
*   **Kai / kung thot kratiam:** chicken or prawns cooked the same way.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Pork neck with fried egg**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Pork neck, thinly sliced | 500 g |
| Garlic (Thai), chopped | 2 heads |
| Coriander root + white peppercorns, pounded | 4 + 1 tsp |
| Oyster sauce / seasoning sauce / sugar / dark soy | 2 tbsp / 1 tbsp / 2 tsp / 1 tsp |
| Duck or hen eggs | 4 |
| Oil | 6 tbsp |
| Cucumber, coriander | to serve |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice | 400 g raw |
| Pork per plate | ≈ 120 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** Cook 400 g jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) with 520 ml water; rest 10 min covered.
2. **Marinate:** pork with the pounded coriander root and pepper, half the garlic and the sauces, 30 min.
3. **Garlic:** fry the remaining garlic in oil to light gold; lift out; keep the oil.
4. **Pork:** fry the pork in the garlic oil until caramelised at the edges.
5. **Serve:** over rice, scatter fried garlic, add a fried egg, cucumber and coriander.
