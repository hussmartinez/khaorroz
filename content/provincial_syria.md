# Syria / سوريا

> ⬅ [README](README.md)

## 1. Overview
Syria's kitchen has two great poles. **Damascus** (al-Sham) cooks in a register close to Lebanon, Palestine and Jordan: restrained spicing, cumin and safflower in fillings, rose and orange-blossom water in sweets. **Aleppo** (Halab), long a caravan and trading city with Turkish and Armenian influence, is described as more heavily seasoned, especially with red pepper (the famous *Aleppo pepper* and pepper paste). It uses tomato paste and sour notes (sumac, pomegranate molasses, tamarind) in its stuffed vegetables, and many vegetables are cooked in yogurt. Aleppo's food is often called the culinary heart of the country.

Between and beyond them sit distinct regional kitchens. **Homs and Hama** are known for their pastries and sweets; Homs is also known for flat, pastry-like kibbeh, and Hama for the quality of its yogurt dishes. The **coast** (Latakia, Tartus) adds fish and its own kibbeh techniques. **Hauran** in the south is wheat country. The **Euphrates valley and the Jazira** (Deir ez-Zor, Raqqa, Hasakah) lean on bread-and-broth *tharid* dishes rather than rice.

**Rice vs. bulgur.** Cracked wheat (*burghul*), along with freekeh, is Syria's everyday grain: kibbeh, tabbouleh, *mujaddara* made with bulgur, bulgur pilafs. **Rice** is the grain of hospitality and celebration. It is the stuffing of vegetables and vine leaves, the bed for yogurt stews, the filling of the wedding *ouzi*, and the base of milk puddings. Vermicelli rice (*ruz bi-sh'ariyyeh*) is the standard accompaniment.

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **Damascus & the Ghouta** | Ouzi, shakriyeh with vermicelli rice, rose- and orange-blossom milk puddings | Damascus, Douma |
| **Aleppo (Halab)** | Stuffed vegetables (*mahshi*) with sour sauces, the layered rice-milk-orange pudding (*mbattan*), Aleppo pepper | Aleppo |
| **Homs & Hama (Orontes valley)** | Yogurt dishes (Hama's shakriyeh), sweets and pastries; safflower in Homs | Homs, Hama |
| **The coast** | Fish and kibbeh variants; stuffed vegetables | Latakia, Tartus, Baniyas |
| **Hauran (south)** | Wheat and bulgur country; rice for feasts | Daraa, Bosra |
| **Euphrates & Jazira (east)** | Bread-and-broth *tharid* (e.g. okra tharid); rice less central ⚠️ | Deir ez-Zor, Raqqa, Hasakah |
