# Tom Kha Kai / ต้มข่าไก่

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** The 1890 first-record claim comes from secondary sources.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand; eaten nationwide.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: a soup of chicken in coconut milk perfumed with young galangal, lemongrass and makrut lime leaf, soured with lime, served alongside rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A pot or charcoal hot-pot (*mo fai*).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Tom kha* means 'boiled galangal'. Secondary sources say a recipe is first recorded in a Thai cookbook around 1890, and that early versions used duck before chicken became standard ([Wikipedia – Tom kha kai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_kha_kai); [Thailand Foundation](https://thailandfoundation.or.th/?p=519487)). The Michelin Guide sets it among the four great Thai soups with tom yum, tom khlong and tom som ([Michelin](https://guide.michelin.com/th/th/article/features/thai-soups-explained-tom-kha-tom-yum-tom-klong-and-tom-som)). Like all Thai soups it is eaten with rice, not as a separate course.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Tom kha kai:** chicken, the standard.
*   **Tom kha pla:** fish.
*   **Tom kha het:** mushrooms, vegetarian.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chicken thigh, sliced | 400 g |
| Coconut milk | 600 ml |
| Water or stock | 300 ml |
| Young galangal, sliced | 10 slices |
| Lemongrass, makrut lime leaves | 2 stalks, 5 |
| Mushrooms | 150 g |
| Fish sauce, lime juice, chilies | 3 tbsp, 3 tbsp, 3 |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) | 400 g, rinsed (+ 520 ml water, absorption method) |
| Soup per person | ≈ 300 ml |
| Rice per person | ≈ 180 g cooked |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Simmer coconut milk and water with galangal, lemongrass and lime leaves for 3 min.
2. Add chicken and mushrooms; cook gently 6–8 min without boiling hard.
3. Off the heat, season with fish sauce, lime juice and chilies.
4. Cook the rice: rinse 400 g jasmine rice until the water runs nearly clear, add 520 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min, then rest 10 min covered (or use an electric rice cooker / *mo hung khao*).
5. Serve with rice.
