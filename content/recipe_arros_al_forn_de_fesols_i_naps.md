# Arroz al Horno de Alubias y Nabos / Arròs al Forn de Fesols i Naps

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** recorded in a Valencian home-recipe collection hosted by the Universitat de València (uv.es/baldovi), which says it was "amplament popular, o era, a la ciutat de València" (widely popular in València city, or once was). No other sources were found.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** València city (L'Horta).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). The baked counterpart of a caldoso.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A flat earthenware cassola in the oven.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The well-known [arròs amb fesols i naps](recipe_arroz_con_fesols_i_naps.md) is a soupy winter pot. This is its **oven-baked** form, and a **meatless** one: just beans, **turnip or nap-i-col** (kohlrabi), a head of garlic, oil, paprika and black pepper. According to its source it was once a common dish in València city, which makes it a good example of a dish that has quietly disappeared. Nap-i-col (kohlrabi), rarely seen in Valencian markets today, was the preferred brassica.

**Culinary science:** in the oven, the starch from the beans and the sweetness of the turnip concentrate into a dry, savoury crust on top, a very different result from the same ingredients simmered as a caldoso. Briefly frying the rice with paprika seals the grains.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **València city (recorded):** Beans + turnip or nap-i-col + garlic head, no meat.
*   **Caldoso form:** [Arròs amb fesols i naps](recipe_arroz_con_fesols_i_naps.md) (with pork; L'Horta, La Safor, La Ribera).
*   ⚠️ No other town variants are documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: recorded version (published quantities).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Dried white beans, soaked | 250 g |
| Turnip or nap-i-col (kohlrabi) | 500 g |
| Whole head of garlic | 1 |
| Olive oil | 250 ml |
| Pimentón | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper, saffron, salt | to taste |
| Water | ≈ 1.5 L |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 500 g |
| Bean broth | ≈ 1 L (adjust to about 2 : 1 by volume) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Beans:** Start the soaked beans, garlic, turnip and salt in cold water and boil until tender.
2. **Rice:** Fry the rice briefly in oil with the pimentón.
3. **Assemble:** Put it in the cassola with the broth, beans and turnip. Adjust the salt and water.
4. **Oven:** **≈ 20 min** at 220–240 °C.
5. **Rest:** 5 min.
