# Chu Chi Pla / ฉู่ฉี่ปลา

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** No dated history found; documented at recipe level.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: fried fish (often mackerel or seabass) under a thick, reduced red curry sauce of coconut cream with shredded makrut lime leaf, eaten with rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok (*kratha*) for frying the fish and reducing the sauce.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Chu chi* is a dry, thick red curry built on red curry paste, coconut cream and makrut lime leaves, cooked down until it clings ([ThaiFoodMaster – Chu chee](https://thaifoodmaster.com/thai_food_recipes/thai_curry_recipes/chuu-chee/67538)). It is usually served over fried fish, mackerel being common, or prawns, and eaten with jasmine rice ([Temple of Thai – chu chee paste](https://www.templeofthai.com/food/curry_paste/chu-chee-2190031386.php); [Camile Thai](https://www.camile.ie/blog/chu-chee-curry-recipe)).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Chu chi pla thu:** mackerel.
*   **Chu chi kung:** prawns.
*   **Chu chi pla kapong:** seabass, restaurant style.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Whole fish or fillets (mackerel, seabass) | 600 g |
| Oil for frying | as needed |
| Red curry paste | 40 g |
| Coconut cream | 300 ml |
| Fish sauce, palm sugar | 1½ tbsp, 1 tbsp |
| Makrut lime leaves, finely shredded | 6 |
| Red chili, sliced | 1 |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) | 400 g, rinsed (+ 520 ml water, absorption method) |
| Fish per plate | ≈ 150 g with sauce |
| Rice per plate | ≈ 180 g cooked |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Fry the fish until crisp; drain.
2. In the wok, reduce half the coconut cream until it splits; fry the paste.
3. Add the rest of the cream; season and reduce to a thick sauce.
4. Pour over the fish; top with lime leaves and chili.
5. Cook the rice: rinse 400 g jasmine rice until the water runs nearly clear, add 520 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min, then rest 10 min covered (or use an electric rice cooker / *mo hung khao*).
6. Serve with rice.
