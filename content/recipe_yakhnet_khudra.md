# Yakhnet Khudra / يخنة الخضار

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md) · [Jordan](provincial_jordan.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md) · [Syria](provincial_syria.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** a generic household stew documented by Arabic recipe sites; its regional association with Palestine and Jordan comes from those sites, not from historical sources.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** The Levant household kitchen; Arabic sources call meat-and-vegetable yakhneh "famous in Palestine and Jordan".
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: mixed vegetables (potato, carrot, zucchini, peas, tomato) with meat or chicken in a light tomato broth, served with vermicelli rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A large stew pot; a lidded rice pot.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
When there is a little of everything in the kitchen, the Levantine cook makes **yakhnet khudra**: vegetables added "in order of their cooking time, starting with potato, then carrot, with zucchini and tomato last", covered with water or chicken broth, and simmered about half an hour. It is served with vermicelli rice. Arabic recipe sites describe meat-and-vegetable yakhneh as "famous in Palestine and Jordan and served with rice". A minced-meat version with potato, carrot, peas and mushrooms takes about 40 minutes for four people.

It is the catch-all member of the *ruz w yakhneh* family; see [yakhnet batata](recipe_yakhnet_batata.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Palestine / Jordan (lamb or beef):** Meat cubes, carrot and potato dominant, tomato; white rice.
*   **Chicken version:** Chicken broth base; vermicelli rice.
*   **Minced-meat version:** Quicker, with peas and mushrooms.
*   **Lamb with vegetables (Ramadan):** A heartier version with lamb pieces for the iftar table.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Palestinian-Jordanian yakhnet khudra with lamb and vermicelli rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Lamb or beef, cubed | 500 g |
| Potatoes, cubed | 300 g |
| Carrots, sliced | 200 g |
| Zucchini, cubed | 200 g |
| Peas | 150 g |
| Onion + garlic | 1 + 3 cloves |
| Tomato paste + crushed tomatoes | 2 tbsp + 300 g |
| Bay leaf, cardamom, 7-spice | 2, 3 pods, 1 tsp |
| Olive oil | 40 ml |
| Water or stock | 1 L |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice (medium-grain) | 300 g |
| Vermicelli | 50 g |
| Water for the rice | 450 ml |
| Butter | 30 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Meat:** Brown the meat with the onion; add the water, bay, cardamom and salt; simmer **60 min**.
2. **Vegetables in order:** Add the potato, then the carrot 5 min later, then the tomato paste and tomatoes; after 15 min add the zucchini and peas.
3. **Stew:** Simmer **15 min** more until everything is tender.
4. **Rice:** Toast the vermicelli in butter, add the rice and boiling salted water, cook covered 15 min, rest 10 min.
5. **Serve:** Stew over the rice.
