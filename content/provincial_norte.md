# Norte: Galicia, Asturias, Cantabria y País Vasco / A Cornixa Cantábrica

> ⬅ [README](README.md)

## 1. Overview
Green, Atlantic Spain grows no rice, but it cooks rice with two great local resources: **shellfish** and **dairy**. On the coast, from the Galician *rías* to the Basque ports, rice is a **caldoso** vehicle for clams, lobster, velvet crab and monkfish, cooked in iron **potas** or clay *tarteiras*. Inland, Asturias braises its free-range **pitu de caleya** and serves it over rice. And the whole north, Asturias above all, is the heartland of **arroz con leche**, with the burnt-sugar *requemado* and the **Festival del Arroz con Leche de Cabranes**.

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **Rías Baixas (Galicia)** | Arroz con almejas (ameixas), albariño | Carril/Vilagarcía, Cambados, O Grove |
| **A Mariña Lucense / Rías Altas** | Arroz caldoso con bogavante in iron potas | Rinlo (Ribadeo), Viveiro |
| **Central & eastern Asturias** | Arroz con pitu de caleya, arroz con leche requemado | Cabranes, Villaviciosa, Oviedo, Llanes |
| **Cantabria** | Clam and anchovy rices, arroz con leche | Santoña, Laredo, Santander |
| **País Vasco** | Clams in salsa verde with rice | Getaria, Bermeo |
