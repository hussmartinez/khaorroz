# Khanom Krok / ขนมครก

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Nationwide street sweet, with its old centre in Ayutthaya and the central plain.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Rice-flour and coconut cups cooked in a cast-iron dimpled pan. They have a crisp shell and a molten, salty-sweet coconut-cream top, and are eaten in pairs.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** The ***ta khanom krok***, a cast-iron (or clay) plate with rows of round dimples, over charcoal.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Khanom krok is described as a much-loved sweet of the **Ayutthaya** era, when *khanom krok* pans were already sold. Traditionally the batter was **soaked rice ground on a stone mill** with thin coconut milk, cooked rice and grated half-ripe coconut, topped with coconut cream. Palace cooks (*chao wang*) elaborated the toppings: shrimp, egg, pork, taro and corn (Khaosod). In 2024 TasteAtlas ranked it **fourth among the world's best pancakes** (Thai Government PR Department).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Classic street style:** Plain, or with spring onion or corn on top.
*   **Palace style (*chao wang*):** Shrimp, egg, pork, taro or corn toppings.
*   **Khanom krok bai toey:** Pandan-green batter.
*   **Old-style batter:** Stone-milled soaked rice instead of bought rice flour.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **classic khanom krok** (about 28 cups).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice flour | 150 g |
| Cooked jasmine rice | 50 g |
| Thin coconut milk | 300 ml |
| Grated young coconut | 40 g |
| Salt | ½ tsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Topping: coconut cream + sugar + salt + rice flour | 250 ml + 60 g + ½ tsp + 1 tbsp |
| Garnish: sliced spring onion or sweetcorn | 3 tbsp |
| Oil, for the pan | as needed |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Base batter:** Blend the rice flour, cooked rice, thin coconut milk, grated coconut and salt until smooth. Rest **30 minutes**.
2. **Topping batter:** Mix the coconut cream, sugar, salt and rice flour until smooth.
3. **Heat:** Heat the dimpled pan over charcoal and oil each cup.
4. **Cook:** Fill each cup two-thirds with base batter. When the edges set (**1–2 minutes**), top with the coconut-cream batter and garnish. Cover and cook **3–4 minutes**, until the bases are crisp and the tops just set.
5. **Serve:** Scoop out with a curved spoon and pair the halves face to face.
