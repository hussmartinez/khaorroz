# Ardi Shawki Mehshi / أرضي شوكي محشي

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md) · [Syria](provincial_syria.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Lebanon (spring artichoke season, coastal and Mount Lebanon markets) and Syria.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: artichoke bottoms filled with spiced minced meat and pine nuts, braised in a tomato or lemony sauce and served over fluffy rice that soaks up the sauce.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A shallow pan to fry the artichoke cups, then a wide pot or oven dish to braise them; a separate pot for the rice.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Mehshi* is the umbrella Arabic word for stuffed vegetables, and artichoke bottoms are one of its most refined forms ([Anissa Helou – Mehshi or stuffed vegetables](https://anissahelou.substack.com/p/mehshi-or-stuffed-vegetables)). Unlike zucchini or vine-leaf mehshi, the filling is usually meat and pine nuts without rice, and the rice is served alongside: one Lebanese recipe braises the stuffed bottoms in tomato sauce and serves them over Lebanese rice ([The Salt and Sweet](https://thesaltandsweet.com/?p=566)), another calls it comfort food whose lemony sauce goes well with fluffy rice ([Every Little Crumb](https://everylittlecrumb.com/?p=10314)). A Syrian version stuffs only the hearts with meat and serves them with rice ([Syrian Cooking – Ardi shoki blahmi](https://www.syriancooking.com/2010/04/04/ardi-shoki-blahmi-stuffed-artichoke-hearts/)); some older Lebanese recipes add rice to the filling itself ([Lebanese Cookbook blog – Stuffed artichokes with rice](https://lebanese-cookbook.blogspot.com/2011/03/stuffed-artichokes-with-rice.html)).

Fresh artichokes are trimmed to their bottoms in spring; frozen bottoms from Middle Eastern grocers now make it a year-round dish. It is a spring cousin of the artichoke rices of Spain, such as [arroz con alcachofas de Benicarló](recipe_arroz_con_alcachofas_benicarlo.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Lebanese tomato version:** fried bottoms filled with meat, onion and pine nuts, braised in tomato sauce, over vermicelli or plain rice ([Discover Lebanon](https://discoverlebanon.com/en/recipes/artichokes-stuffed.php); [The Salt and Sweet](https://thesaltandsweet.com/?p=566)).
*   **Lemony (white) version:** braised in stock with lemon and garlic ([Every Little Crumb](https://everylittlecrumb.com/?p=10314)).
*   **Yogurt version:** bottoms baked under a yogurt sauce ([Taste of Beirut – Artichoke bottoms with a yogurt sauce](https://tasteofbeirut.com/artichokes-bottoms-with-a-yogurt-sauce)).
*   **Syria (ardi shoki bi-lahmeh):** hearts only, served with rice ([Syrian Cooking](https://www.syriancooking.com/2010/04/04/ardi-shoki-blahmi-stuffed-artichoke-hearts/)).
*   **Rice-filled version (⚠️ older home recipe):** rice and lamb in the filling ([Lebanese Cookbook blog](https://lebanese-cookbook.blogspot.com/2011/03/stuffed-artichokes-with-rice.html)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Lebanese tomato version over vermicelli rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Artichoke bottoms (fresh-trimmed or frozen) | 12 |
| Minced lamb or beef | 450 g |
| Onion, finely chopped | 2 |
| Pine nuts | 40 g |
| Lebanese seven spices / allspice | 1½ tsp |
| Tomato paste | 2 tbsp |
| Water or stock | 500 ml |
| Lemon juice | 2 tbsp |
| Butter or ghee, olive oil | 30 g / 60 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Long- or medium-grain rice | 300 g |
| Vermicelli | 40 g |
| Water for the rice | 500 ml |
| Stuffed bottoms per person | 3 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Filling:** fry the onion and pine nuts in butter, add the meat and spices and cook until browned; season.
2. **Cups:** fry the artichoke bottoms briefly in olive oil (or brush and roast) and fill them generously with the meat.
3. **Braise:** arrange in a pan, pour over the stock mixed with tomato paste and lemon, cover and simmer (or bake at 180 °C) 30–35 minutes until tender.
4. **Rice:** toast the vermicelli in a little butter, add the rice, then boiling salted water; cover and cook on lowest heat 18 minutes; rest 10 minutes.
5. **Serve:** rice on the plate, three artichokes on top, sauce spooned over.
