# Olla de Benicarló (con Arroz) / Olla de Benicarló amb Arròs

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de Castelló](provincial_castellon.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented in the *Revista Española de Nutrición Comunitaria*'s 2022 survey of **forgotten dishes** of the Valencian Community (photo credited to Eduardo Boix López). It is an **olla** (bean pot) finished with rice rather than a rice dish proper. It is included because the rice is cooked in the pot and it is the northern cousin of [arròs amb fesols i naps](recipe_arroz_con_fesols_i_naps.md). Town variations beyond the RENC version are not documented.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** El Baix Maestrat (Benicarló).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Caldoso** (soupy).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A tall *olla* on the hearth or stove.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Castellón's iconic winter dish is the [olla de la Plana](provincial_castellon.md), a bean-and-vegetable stew. Benicarló's version adds **cured ham, salt pork and onion blood-sausage** to white beans, **cardoon** and potato, and finishes with a modest amount of **rice** cooked directly in the pot. It is a one-pot meal of the cold months, when cardoon is in season and the pig-slaughter cured meats are ready.

**Culinary science:** cured ham and *tocino* release salt and glutamates into the bean water, so it becomes a stock without any bones. Cardoon, a relative of the artichoke, adds a gentle bitterness and needs about 30 min to soften. The rice is added last and in small proportion (≈ 40 g per person), so it thickens the broth slightly rather than dominating.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **The Benicarló Style (RENC):** White beans, ham, *tocino magro*, 2 morcillas de cebolla, cardoon, potato, tomato, pimentón, saffron, Valencia rice.
*   **The Olla de la Plana (Castelló, Vila-real):** Usually **without rice**. Adds carrot, cabbage, turnip, green beans and chard.
*   **Other Baix Maestrat towns:** ⚠️ Not documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **RENC 2022 (Benicarló)**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Dried white beans, soaked overnight **without salt** | 468 g |
| Serrano ham | 100 g |
| Tocino magro (streaky salt pork) | 250 g |
| Morcillas de cebolla | 2 |
| Ripe tomato, chopped | 1 medium |
| Cardoon, peeled, in pieces | 1 bunch |
| Potatoes, diced | 3 medium |
| Pimentón | 1 tbsp |
| Water | to cover generously (≈ 3 L) |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Arroz de Valencia | 160 g |
| Olive oil | 1 tbsp |
| Saffron | 7–8 threads |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Beans:** Put the beans in water. When it starts to boil, add the tocino, ham, morcillas and tomato.
2. **Simmer:** Cook until the meat is very tender (**≈ 45 min**).
3. **Vegetables:** Add the potato, cardoon and pimentón.
4. **Rice:** After **10 min**, add the oil, rice and saffron. Cook **20 min**.
5. **Finish:** Taste for salt (the ham is salty) and serve soupy.
