# Arroz al Horno de Anguilas y Judías / Arròs al Forn d'Anguiles i Bajoques

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** recorded in a Valencian home-recipe collection hosted by the Universitat de València (uv.es/baldovi), with **no town attribution**. The link to L'Albufera and its eels is my inference from the ingredients.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Province of València, most plausibly the L'Albufera eel country (⚠️ inferred).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). Baked 30 min or more, with less than double water.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A flat earthenware cassola in the oven.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A rare baked rice that puts **eel**, the fish of L'Albufera's *all i pebre*, together with the **paella vegetables**: *bajoqueta*, *tavella* and *garrofó*. A whole head of garlic boils with the beans, then the eel pieces cook in that broth for 10–15 min before everything goes into the cassola with fried rice and paprika. It bridges the wetland's two great dishes, the paella and the eel stew.

**Culinary science:** eel is very fatty and gelatinous, so it enriches the broth and keeps the baked rice moist. That is why the source uses **less than double** the water and a **longer bake** (about 30 min): the fat slows evaporation.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Recorded version:** Eel + bajoqueta + tavella + garrofó + garlic head + tomato + paprika + saffron.
*   **Related:** The recorded *paella valenciana* in the same collection allows **eel** as an optional addition. Pego (Marina Alta) once made an *arròs amb anguila* (see the Alicante lost-rices registry in [provincial_alicante.md](provincial_alicante.md)).
*   ⚠️ Town variants are not documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: recorded version (published quantities).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Eel, in pieces | 500 g |
| Bajoqueta | 250 g |
| Tavella + garrofó | 125 g + 125 g |
| Whole head of garlic | 1 |
| Tomato, chopped | 100 g |
| Olive oil | 200 ml |
| Pimentón, saffron, salt | to taste |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 400 g |
| Broth | **less than** 2 × rice volume (≈ 0.7–0.75 L) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Vegetables:** Boil the bajoqueta, tavella, garrofó, garlic head and saffron in water until nearly cooked.
2. **Eel:** Add the eel pieces and cook **10–15 min**.
3. **Rice:** Fry the tomato in oil, add the rice and pimentón.
4. **Assemble:** Put everything in the cassola with the broth, eel and vegetables.
5. **Oven:** **≈ 30 min or more**, depending on the liquid, at 200–220 °C.
6. **Rest:** 5 min.
