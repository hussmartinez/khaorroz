# Mnazalet Zahra / منزلة الزهرة

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Syria](provincial_syria.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md) · [Jordan](provincial_jordan.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the Shami *mnazalet zahra* is documented by one Arabic recipe source; the rice pairing is documented for a closely related layered cauliflower-and-mince tray. Town attributions are not documented.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bilad al-Sham (Syria, Palestine, Jordan); a winter dish, when cauliflower (*zahra* in Levantine dialect, *qarnabit* in standard Arabic) is in season.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: fried cauliflower florets simmered with spiced minced meat until tender, served hot with white rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A frying pan for the florets, a wide pot or oven tray for the stew; a lidded rice pot.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Cauliflower is fried before almost anything else in the Levant: it goes into [maqluba](recipe_maqluba.md), is eaten in bread with tahini, and is stewed as **mnazalet zahra**. One Arabic source calls it "one of the tastiest Shami dishes, fried cauliflower florets cooked with minced meat", seasoned with cumin, coriander, garlic and ghee. A layered version, cauliflower and minced meat with onion and tomato baked in a tray, is served "hot with bread, white rice and salad".

It is the cauliflower sister of [mnazalet batinjan](recipe_mnazalet_batinjan.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Shami stovetop version:** Fried florets, minced meat, cumin, coriander, garlic, ghee, boiling water; simmered covered.
*   **Layered tray version:** Boiled florets and mince layered with onion and tomato and baked; served with rice.
*   **Béchamel tray (modern, ⚠️):** A festive oven version with white sauce, more recent.
*   **Palestine / Jordan (⚠️):** The same florets are more often used in maqluba; mnazaleh is the weekday alternative.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Shami stovetop mnazalet zahra with white rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Cauliflower, in florets | 1 large (about 1.2 kg) |
| Minced lamb or beef | 400 g |
| Onion, chopped | 1 |
| Garlic, crushed | 4 cloves |
| Ground cumin + ground coriander | 1 tsp each |
| Ghee (*samneh*) | 30 g |
| Frying oil | for frying |
| Boiling water | 300 ml |
| Fresh coriander, salt, black pepper | to taste |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice (Egyptian or medium-grain) | 300 g |
| Water for the rice | 450 ml |
| Butter or ghee | 30 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Fry:** Salt the florets and fry until deep golden; drain.
2. **Meat:** In the ghee, fry the onion, then the mince, cumin, coriander, salt and pepper until browned.
3. **Stew:** Add the cauliflower and boiling water, cover and cook **15–20 min** on medium heat.
4. **Rice:** Rinse the rice, fry in butter, add boiling salted water, cook covered 15 min, rest 10 min.
5. **Finish:** Stir in the crushed garlic and chopped coriander; serve with the rice.
