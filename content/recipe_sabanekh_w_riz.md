# Sabanekh w Riz / سبانخ ورز

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** All of Lebanon in winter and spring, when spinach is in season.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Stew over rice (*yakhnet sabanekh*). Wilted spinach with minced meat or lamb, onion, garlic and coriander, finished with lemon and toasted pine nuts. A tangy green stew served over vermicelli rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wide pot on the stove; a small pan for toasting the pine nuts.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Sabanekh w riz* ("spinach and rice") is one of the quickest of the Lebanese home stews; several recipes make it in about 30 minutes. It is defined by its **lemony** finish and **toasted pine nuts**, and it is eaten over vermicelli rice like [bazella](recipe_bazella_w_ruz.md) and [bamieh](recipe_bamyeh_ma_ruz.md). It also has a meatless form with chickpeas or beans for fasting days.

**Culinary science:** spinach collapses to about a tenth of its raw volume. Adding it in batches keeps the pot from flooding, and short cooking keeps it bright green. Lemon is added at the end, because long cooking with acid turns chlorophyll dull olive-brown.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Minced beef version:** Quick weekday style.
*   **Minced lamb with pine nuts:** The richer style.
*   **Vegan / fasting version:** Chickpeas or beans instead of meat.
*   ⚠️ Town-level variations are not documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: minced lamb with pine nuts.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Fresh spinach, washed and chopped | 1 kg |
| Minced lamb or beef | 350 g |
| Olive oil | 3 tbsp |
| Onion, chopped | 1 |
| Garlic, crushed | 4 cloves |
| Fresh coriander, chopped | ½ bunch |
| Pine nuts, toasted | 40 g |
| Lemon juice | 60 ml |
| Seven-spice, salt, pepper | 1 tsp, to taste |
| Water | ≈ 250 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Vermicelli rice | 300 g rice + 50 g vermicelli |
| Spinach (raw) : meat | ≈ 3 : 1 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Base:** Fry the onion in the oil, add the meat and brown it, breaking it up, then add the spices.
2. **Garlic-coriander:** Add the garlic and coriander for 1 min.
3. **Spinach:** Add the spinach in batches with the water, letting each batch wilt. Simmer **10–15 min**.
4. **Finish:** Stir in the lemon juice and half the pine nuts, and adjust the salt.
5. **Serve:** Serve over vermicelli rice, topped with the remaining pine nuts.
