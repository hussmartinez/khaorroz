# Paella a la Riojana / Arroz a la Riojana

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Aragón, Navarra y La Rioja](provincial_aragon_navarra_rioja.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the only specific source is a 2024 Nueve Cuatro Uno (Logroño) report on a rice workshop given by the *arrocero* **Pepe Fortea**. It describes the Riojan paella debate and his advice, but gives **no full recipe**. The blueprint below is reconstructed from that advice and from Riojan pantry staples. The common assumption that "a la riojana" means chorizo and peppers comes from *patatas a la riojana*, not from a documented rice tradition.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** La Rioja (Logroño and the Ebro valley).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). Paella style; potato is added to help dry the rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella over gas, or wood (vine shoots in the vineyards).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
La Rioja has a popular "paella riojana" culture of fiestas and *cuadrillas*, and a lively debate about **chorizo**. Pepe Fortea's 2024 workshop conceded that the paella *a la riojana* "no debería llevar chorizo": the sausage has too strong a flavour and dominates the rice. Instead he proposed Riojan products: **potato**, which helps dry the rice, and **strips of roasted pepper** from Riojan preserves. The chorizo instinct comes from the region's best-known dish, *patatas a la riojana* (potato, chorizo, *choricero* pepper), whose flavours easily migrate into a rice.

**Culinary science:** potato releases starch and absorbs liquid, which speeds up the drying of a seco rice. Roasted peppers added late bring sweetness and smokiness without the fat and paprika load of chorizo, so the rice stays clean.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Pepe Fortea's "Riojan products" line:** No chorizo. Potato, roasted pepper strips.
*   **Popular fiesta style:** ⚠️ Often includes **chorizo** and pork. Widespread but undocumented in recipe sources.
*   ⚠️ No town-specific versions found.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **reconstructed "Riojan products" paella** (no chorizo).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chicken thighs or pork ribs | 600 g |
| Olive oil | 70 ml |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Sweet paprika | 1 tsp |
| Potato, in 1 cm dice | 200 g |
| Water or light stock | 1.5 L |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Round rice | 400 g |
| Broth at rice-in | ≈ 1.15 L |
| Roasted red pepper strips (Riojan preserve) | 200 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Brown** the meat (15 min). Add the garlic, paprika (5 s) and tomato (5 min).
2. **Broth:** Add the water and simmer 20 min. Add the potato for the last 5 min.
3. **Rice:** Scatter and level. High heat for 8 min, then medium-low for 8 min.
4. **Peppers:** Lay the roasted pepper strips on top at min 12.
5. **Finish:** Brief high heat (1 min), then rest 4 min.
