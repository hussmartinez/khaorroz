# Arroz con Rape y Gamba Roja / Arròs amb Rap i Gamba Roja

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província d'Alacant](provincial_alicante.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** L'Alacantí (Alicante city). The *gamba roja* (red prawn) is the prize of the Alicante–Dénia–Santa Pola fleets.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). An oven pass and a final socarrat burst.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella on gas or wood, with a brief oven pass at 180 °C.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A modern Alicante classic in the "Ciudad del Arroz" canon. It combines two luxury catches: **monkfish** (*rape*, Valencian *rap*), a firm, gelatinous fish that holds its shape, and **red prawn**, whose heads hold an intensely flavoured coral. The technique is nose-to-tail. Monkfish head and skin, and the prawn heads and shells, are sautéed and removed to strengthen the fond. The raw prawn heads are squeezed to make an **allioli** with confit garlic, which is stirred in at the end.

**Culinary science:** monkfish collagen dissolves quickly. Adding the diced tail only **5 minutes before the end** keeps it juicy. Prawn-head juice is rich in enzymes and fat-soluble carotenoids (astaxanthin). Emulsifying it raw into allioli captures aromas that boiling would drive off.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Alicante city (Ciudad del Arroz):** Salmorreta + ajos tiernos, 17 min (3 min high, then medium), oven at 180 °C, socarrat burst, prawn-head allioli.
*   **Older coastal versions:** Monkfish also appears in caldoso form in Valencian home cooking, e.g. the *arrossos caldosos amb peix* family (rap variant) of a Universitat de València recipe collection. ⚠️ Not town-attributed.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Alicante "Ciudad del Arroz" (400 g Bomba published; other quantities reconstructed).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Monkfish tail (diced) + head/skin | 500 g + trimmings |
| Red prawns | 8–12 |
| Ajos tiernos | 1 bunch |
| Salmorreta | 1 tbsp |
| Fish stock (hot, saffron-infused) | 1.2 L |
| Olive oil | 70 ml |
| **Allioli:** squeezed prawn-head juice + confit garlic + EVOO | 4–6 tbsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Bomba rice | 400 g |
| Stock | 1.2 L |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Prep:** Dice the monkfish tail and slice the thick part. Peel the prawns and squeeze the raw heads for juice, then blend it with confit garlic and EVOO into allioli.
2. **Fond:** Sear the monkfish slices (set aside for plating). Briefly fry the remaining monkfish trimmings and the prawn heads and shells, then remove them.
3. **Base:** Gently sweat the ajos tiernos without browning. Add the salmorreta, then the stock.
4. **Rice:** At the boil, rain in the rice with salt. **3 min high**, then medium.
5. **Fish:** **5 min before the end**, add the diced monkfish, prawn tails and saffron.
6. **Oven and socarrat:** Pass through the oven at **180 °C** to dry the surface, then give it a few seconds on maximum heat. Stir in the allioli on serving. Total rice time ≈ 17 min.
