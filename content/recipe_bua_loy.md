# Bua Loy / บัวลอย

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** recipes are well documented (Thairath, Pholfoodmafia, Wongnai); no historical source on the dish's origin was found.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Nationwide; a classic of Bangkok and central-Thai dessert shops.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** A rice-flour sweet in coconut milk. Small chewy balls of **glutinous-rice flour** (often coloured with pandan, butterfly pea or taro) are served in warm salted-sweet coconut milk, often with a **sweet poached egg** (*khai wan*).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A brass or stainless pot for boiling the balls, a second pot for the coconut milk, and small bowls.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Bua loy* means "floating lotus", for the balls rising to the surface as they cook. It is the Thai member of the glutinous-rice-ball family that includes Chinese *tangyuan*. A Thai consumption study notes that rice-flour sweets such as bua loy are popular at every age. A **Vietnamese-style** version (*bua loy yuan*) has a filling like *khanom thian*. With no historical source available, its age remains unknown.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Classic:** White, pandan-green and butterfly-pea-blue balls in coconut milk.
*   **Taro (*bua loy phueak*):** Taro mashed into the dough.
*   **With sweet egg (*khai wan*):** An egg poached in the coconut milk.
*   **Vietnamese-style (*bua loy yuan*):** Filled balls.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **tricolour bua loy with sweet egg**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Coconut milk | 600 ml |
| Palm sugar + white sugar | 80 g + 40 g |
| Salt | 1 tsp |
| Pandan leaves, knotted | 2 |
| Eggs (optional, for khai wan) | 4 |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Glutinous-rice flour | 200 g |
| Tapioca starch | 20 g |
| Coloured liquids: pandan juice, butterfly-pea water, plain water | about 150 ml total |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Dough:** Divide the flour and starch into three and knead each with one coloured liquid into a soft dough.
2. **Shape:** Roll into balls about 1 cm across.
3. **Boil:** Drop them into boiling water. When they float, cook **1 minute** more and transfer to cold water.
4. **Coconut milk:** Heat the coconut milk with the sugars, salt and pandan until just simmering. Poach the eggs in it if you're using them.
5. **Serve:** Put the balls in bowls and ladle the warm coconut milk (and an egg) over them.
