# Cardillos con Arroz / Tagarninas con Arroz

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Castilla-La Mancha & Extremadura](provincial_castilla_la_mancha_extremadura.md) · [Andalucía](provincial_andalucia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented in the *Revista Española de Nutrición Comunitaria*'s 2022 survey of **forgotten dishes** of Extremadura, which notes that it is also found in Andalucía and Castilla-La Mancha. The source measures rice in **handfuls** and gives **no liquid quantity**. Its method also adds red pepper that is missing from its ingredient list. The liquid ratio below is reconstructed. No town variations are documented.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Extremadura, with the same dish in Andalucía and Castilla-La Mancha.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Meloso** (creamy). Simmered gently, then rested 5 min.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A cazuela on a wood or gas stove.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Cardillos* (Spanish oyster thistle, *Scolymus hispanicus*), called *tagarninas* in Andalucía, are a **wild green foraged** from field edges and fallow land. They are gathered mainly from December to February, after the rains. Their peeled midribs taste like a cross between artichoke and asparagus. In poor rural households they stretched a pot of rice into a meal without meat. The RENC includes the dish among Extremadura's **forgotten or occasional dishes**, part of a disappearing knowledge of wild plants alongside the *cornagüelas* and *espárragos trigueros* in the same survey.

**Culinary science:** the stalks are fibrous, so the **strings must be stripped** first ("para no encontrárnoslas al masticar"). Hot pimentón fried briefly in oil with garlic gives the dish its colour and heat without meat fat.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Extremadura (RENC):** Onion, red pepper, tomato, bay, **hot pimentón** (*pimentón picante*, the La Vera style), garlic.
*   **Andalucía (tagarninas):** Tagarninas are classically made *esparragás*, with a mortar paste of fried garlic, cumin and pimentón thickened with fried bread. ⚠️ Not documented as a rice version.
*   ⚠️ Castilla-La Mancha variants undocumented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Extremadura (RENC 2022)**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Cardillos, cleaned and de-stringed | 2 bunches (≈ 500 g cleaned) |
| Olive oil | 60 ml (4 tbsp) |
| Onion | 1 |
| Red pepper (in method) | 1 |
| Tomatoes, diced | 2 |
| Garlic | 4 cloves |
| Bay leaves | 2 |
| Pimentón picante | 1 tsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| White rice | 6 handfuls (≈ 300 g) |
| Water | ≈ 1.2 L (reconstructed; ≈ 4 : 1, meloso) |
| Salt | to taste |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Clean:** Strip all the strings from the cardillos and chop them.
2. **Sofrito:** Fry the onion in the oil. Add the red pepper and diced tomato.
3. **Cardillos:** Gradually add the cardillos, the pimentón picante and the garlic (sautéed separately), stirring.
4. **Liquid:** Add the water, salt and bay.
5. **Rice:** When it boils, add the rice and simmer gently until done (≈ 18 min).
6. **Rest:** **5 min** off the heat before serving.
