# Paella de Cebolla y Bacalao / Paella amb Ceba i Alls Secs (i Bacallà)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** recorded in a Valencian home-recipe collection hosted by the Universitat de València (uv.es/baldovi), which gives **no town attribution**. The town variations are not documented.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Province of València (home cooking; Lent and fast days).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella over wood or gas. The cassola variant is cooked in an earthenware casserole.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A pared-down **fast-day paella**: a lot of thinly sliced **onion**, unpeeled dried garlic, and soaked, flaked **salt cod**. Onion is normally excluded from Valencian seco rices, but here it *is* the dish, slow-fried until translucent and sweet. The same collection records two siblings. *Paella d'espinacs i alls tendres* swaps the onion for spinach and young garlic. *Arròs de bacallà en cassola* is a creamier casserole with peas and potato.

**Culinary science:** onion sugars caramelise slowly in plenty of oil, giving sweetness and body without any meat. Short-soaked cod (3–4 h) stays slightly salty, so it seasons the rice. The water goes in **hot**, so the grain starts cooking immediately.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Paella amb ceba i alls secs:** Onion + dried garlic + cod (as below).
*   **Paella d'espinacs i alls tendres:** 500 g spinach + 5–6 young garlic replacing the onion, fried garlic → spinach → cod → rice. See also [arroz de boquerones y espinacas](recipe_arroz_de_boquerones_y_espinacas.md).
*   **Arròs de bacallà en cassola:** Potato and peas boiled in the cassola. Garlic, onion, cod and rice fried separately and added. Finishes "dry, creamy and whole".
*   ⚠️ Town variants are not documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: paella amb ceba i alls secs (published quantities).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Salt cod, soaked 3–4 h, flaked | 125 g |
| Onion, finely sliced | 200 g |
| Garlic, unpeeled | 3–4 cloves |
| Olive oil | 200 ml |
| Pimentón, saffron, pinch of black pepper, salt | to taste |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 400 g |
| Hot water | ≈ 0.8 L |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Garlic and onion:** Fry the garlic, then the onion with salt, until translucent.
2. **Cod and rice:** Add the cod, then the rice, and fry together.
3. **Liquid:** Pimentón (seconds), then the **hot** water, saffron and pepper.
4. **Fire:** Adjust the heat until the grain is dry.
5. **Rest:** Briefly.
