# Arroz con Pitu de Caleya / Arroz con Pitu

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Norte (Galicia, Asturias, Cantabria, País Vasco)](provincial_norte.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central and eastern Asturias, the rural *aldeas* and *caleyas* (country lanes) of the Principality.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Meloso** (creamy). Asturias.com describes it as cooked with twice as much broth as rice, giving a juicy rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A cast-iron *pota* or clay *cazuela* on the *cocina de carbón* (coal/wood kitchen range).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The **pitu de caleya** is a free-range rooster or chicken that roams the *caleyas*. It is slaughtered at about **a year old**, while farm chickens barely reach two months. It now has a quality label, *Pitu de Caleya*. Its firm, flavourful meat needs a **long braise**: Turismo Asturias gives about 5 hours of total preparation. The *guiso de pitu* is Asturias's festive Sunday dish, and serving it **with rice** turned it from village food into a restaurant classic. The pitu is braised with onion and green pepper, the broth is **strained so no vegetables end up in the rice**, the rice is cooked in that broth, and the pitu is laid on top at the end.

**Culinary science:** an older bird has more collagen and well-developed muscle fibres. Braising for 2–4 hours converts collagen to gelatin, giving a sticky broth that makes the rice meloso without stirring. Straining the vegetables keeps the texture clean.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Turismo Asturias version:** Pitu braised with **onion and green pepper**. Broth **strained**. Rice cooked in the broth, pitu added at the end.
*   **Asturias.com version:** About 4 *pocillos* of rice, ½ kg chicken, ½ pepper, 2 garlic cloves, **optional saffron**, broth at **2× the rice**, sometimes with **roasted pepper strips**.
*   **Directo al Paladar version:** Pitu + onion, garlic, green pepper, **bay**, **pimentón**, **white wine**, chicken stock. Rice cooked 15–18 min, then rested 5 min.
*   **Eastern Asturias (Llanes / Cangas de Onís) (reconstructed):** A splash of **cider** sometimes replaces the wine.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Turismo Asturias structure** (strained broth).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Pitu de caleya (or old free-range hen/rooster), jointed | 1 (≈ 2 kg) |
| Olive oil | 80 ml |
| Onions | 2 |
| Green peppers | 2 |
| Garlic | 4 cloves |
| Bay leaf | 2 |
| Pimentón dulce | 4 g |
| Dry white wine (or cider) | 250 ml |
| Water or chicken stock | 2 L |

**Extraction:** Brown the pitu (**15 min**). Add the onion, pepper and garlic and cook slowly (**20 min**). Add the pimentón, wine (reduce) and water. Braise covered for **2.5–3.5 h** until tender. Lift out the pitu. **Strain the broth**, pressing the vegetables. Keep ≈ 1 L.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Round rice | 400 g |
| Strained pitu broth | ≈ 0.9–1 L (≈ 2:1 volume, after asturias.com) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** Bring the broth to a boil in the cazuela, add the rice (and saffron if using).
2. **Fire:** Simmer **16–18 min**.
3. **Finish:** Lay the pitu pieces on top. Rest **5 min** covered.
