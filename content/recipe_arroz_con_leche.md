# Arroz con Leche / Arròs amb Llet / Arroz con Leche Requemado

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Norte (Galicia, Asturias, Cantabria, País Vasco)](provincial_norte.md) · [Madrid](provincial_madrid.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Throughout Spain. Its stronghold is **Asturias** (Cabranes, the Comarca de la Sidra), with strong traditions in Cantabria, Madrid, and the Valencian and Catalan lands (*arròs amb llet*).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Meloso** (creamy) (dessert).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A heavy pot (*pota*) over a slow wood or coal range. The Asturian *requemado* is finished with a red-hot iron (*quemador*/*rusiente*), today often a blowtorch.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Rice cooked in sweetened milk is one of the oldest rice preparations in Spain. Sweet rices with milk or honey are generally traced to Andalusi and medieval Mediterranean cooking (the documentation of that lineage is general rather than specific). Asturias, a dairy region, made it its signature dessert. **Arroz con leche requemado**, with a crust of sugar caramelised with a hot iron, is described by Turismo Asturias as one of the identifying marks of Asturian confectionery. Since the 1970s the village of **Cabranes** (Santolaya) has held the **Festival del Arroz con Leche**, a Fiesta of Regional Tourist Interest with a contest, a parade, a market and free tastings; its 46th edition was in May 2025.

**Culinary science:** the Asturian method cooks the rice very slowly, **2–2.5 hours**, stirring constantly, so the grains release a lot of amylopectin while the milk reduces and caramelises slightly (Maillard and lactose browning). The result is thick and ivory-coloured. Citrus peel and cinnamon infuse into the milk fat.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Asturias (Cabranes, Comarca de la Sidra):** Very long cooking (2–2.5 h). Lemon peel + cinnamon stick. Butter at the end in some houses. **Requemado**: 100 g sugar + 10 g cinnamon burnt on top with an iron. Some versions cook the rice first in water with **anise or brandy**.
*   **Cantabria:** Similar dairy-rich style, usually finished with **ground cinnamon** rather than burnt sugar.
*   **Madrid:** The traditional dessert of *casas de comidas* and *tabernas*. Shorter cooking, **lemon peel + cinnamon stick**, chilled for at least 2 h and dusted with cinnamon. Served cold in small *cazuelitas* (source: general Spanish-style recipe; Madrid-specific documentation is thin ⚠️).
*   **València / Catalunya (arròs amb llet):** Lemon and cinnamon, sometimes **orange peel**. Looser, served cold. See also the milk-free Christmas [arroz con miel de Bogarra](recipe_arroz_con_miel_bogarra.md) (Albacete).
*   **Galicia (reconstructed):** Lemon + cinnamon; in some areas milk from the *lareira* (hearth) pot.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Asturian requemado**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Whole milk (high-fat, ideally Asturian) | 1.8 L |
| Water (pre-cook) | 200 ml |
| Lemon peel (no pith) | 1 lemon |
| Cinnamon stick | 1 |
| Butter (optional) | 30 g |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Round rice (bomba-type) | 200 g |
| Sugar (in pudding) | 150 g |
| Requemado topping: sugar + ground cinnamon | 100 g + 10 g |
| Salt | pinch |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Pre-cook:** Simmer the rice in the water for **5 min** until the water is absorbed.
2. **Infuse:** Add the milk, lemon peel and cinnamon and bring to a bare simmer.
3. **Slow cook:** Cook on the **lowest heat for 2–2.5 h**, stirring very often so it doesn't catch. Remove the peel and cinnamon at about 1 h.
4. **Sweeten:** Add the sugar (and butter) for the last **20 min**, which avoids scorching.
5. **Cool:** Pour into clay *cazuelitas* and chill for at least 2 h.
6. **Requemado:** Sprinkle the sugar-cinnamon mix and caramelise with a hot iron or blowtorch until dark amber. (Madrid/Cantabria: dust with cinnamon only.)
