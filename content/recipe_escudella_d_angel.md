# Escudilla de Ángel / Escudella d'Àngel (Arroz con Leche Mallorquín Antiguo)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Illes Balears](provincial_baleares.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented in the *Revista Española de Nutrición Comunitaria*'s 2022 survey of **forgotten dishes** of the Balearic Islands. It was recorded in the 18th century and appears in Mallorcan cookbooks of 1886 and 1931, but it is now **out of use**. The source gives a large festival-scale batch, which is scaled down below. No town variations are documented.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Mallorca.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Meloso** (creamy). A thick, spoonable sweet cream of ground rice, milk, eggs and cinnamon.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A large *cassola* or pot on the stove, stirred continuously.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
"Angel's bowl" was a prestigious Mallorcan **festive sweet**, "muy celebrada, especialmente para los días de fiesta". It was first recorded in the 18th century and was usual until the mid-20th century. It appears in Pere d'Alcàntara Penya's *La cuyna mallorquina* (Palma, 1886) and in *Cuina popular de Mallorca* (Sóller, 1931). It differs from ordinary [arroz con leche](recipe_arroz_con_leche.md) in three ways: the rice is **washed, dried and ground into flour**; it is enriched with **many eggs**; and it is cut with **meat broth** made from lean pork. That mix of sweet and savoury is a survival of medieval and early-modern European cooking, in the same family as *blancmange* (*menjar blanc*).

**Culinary science:** ground rice gelatinises quickly and evenly into a smooth cream with no whole grains. The eggs thicken it further as a custard, so it has to be stirred continuously to stop them curdling. The meat broth adds savoury depth and gelatin.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Mallorca (1886 / 1931 cookbooks, RENC):** Rice flour, milk, eggs (half of them yolks only), sugar, cinnamon, pork broth.
*   ⚠️ No other variants documented. Compare the Albacete [arroz con miel de Bogarra](recipe_arroz_con_miel_bogarra.md) and the Asturian *requemado* in [arroz con leche](recipe_arroz_con_leche.md).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **RENC 2022**, scaled from the published festival batch (3.2 kg rice, 8 L milk, 6 dozen eggs, 2.4 kg sugar, 66.7 g cinnamon) by ≈ 1/16.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity (scaled) |
|---|---|
| Lean pork, for broth | 1 small piece (≈ 100 g) |
| Water for broth | 400 ml |
| Cinnamon, ground | 4 g (≈ 3 g in the water + 1 g with sugar) |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity (scaled) |
|---|---|
| Rice, washed, dried, ground to flour | 200 g |
| Milk | 500 ml |
| Eggs | 4–5 (half used whole, half yolks only) |
| Sugar | 150 g |
| Broth : egg-milk mixture | 1 : 3 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Broth:** Simmer the pork in water to make a light broth, then set aside.
2. **Rice flour:** Wash the rice in warm water, dry it completely and grind it to flour.
3. **Cream base:** Whisk the rice flour into **half** the milk until smooth.
4. **Custard:** Heat the other half of the milk with the sugar. Add half the eggs whole and the rest as yolks only. **Stir continuously**, adding the rice-flour milk, until it thickens.
5. **Broth:** Add one part meat broth for every three parts of the egg-milk mixture and stir.
6. **Serve:** Sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon to taste.
