# Arroz de Ayuno de Calasparra / Arroz de Vigilia con Bacalao

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Región de Murcia](provincial_murcia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the only source I found is Wikipedia's article on Calasparra rice, which describes *arroz de ayuno* as a meatless Lenten rice made with salt cod and garden vegetables. I found no detailed local recipe, so the method below is reconstructed from Murcian and Valencian Lenten rice practice. Verify it locally, for example during the Calasparra *Jornadas Gastronómicas*.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Calasparra and the Noroeste of Murcia, the D.O. Arroz de Calasparra area (which also reaches Hellín in Albacete).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A steel paella over wood.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Calasparra has grown rice since at least the **14th century**. In **1986** its rice became the first rice in the world to receive a **Denominación de Origen**, and the first non-wine D.O. in Spain. Only **bomba** and **balilla × sollana** may be sold under the D.O. Alongside the area's rabbit and mountain-snail rice, the Lenten table had the **arroz de ayuno** ("fasting rice"): no meat, just **salt cod** (*bacalao*) and vegetables from the local huerta.

**Culinary science:** desalted cod releases gelatinous juices when it is fried briefly in oil, and these help bind a meatless broth. Calasparra rice is slow-ripened in cold water, so it stays whole through the 18–20 minutes it needs.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Calasparra (documented core):** Bacalao + garden vegetables.
*   ⚠️ **Reconstructed:** In winter, cauliflower and artichoke. In spring, broad beans and peas. Some families add *garbanzos* (chickpeas), as in Lenten potajes.
*   **Relatives:** The Valencian [arròs empedrat](recipe_arroz_empedrado.md) and the Vega Baja *arroz de vigilia* are the nearest Lenten cousins.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: reconstructed Calasparra Lenten rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Salt cod, desalted 36 h, flaked | 300 g |
| Olive oil | 90 ml |
| Cauliflower florets | 250 g |
| Artichokes, eighths | 3 |
| Red pepper, strips | ½ |
| Garlic | 3 cloves |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Pimentón dulce | 3 g |
| Saffron | 0.3 g |
| Water or vegetable stock | 1.2 L |

**Extraction:** Fry the pepper strips and set them aside. Fry the cauliflower and artichokes until golden (**8 min**). Add the garlic and the cod (**1 min**), then the pimentón and tomato (**4 min**). Add the water and saffron and simmer **10 min**.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Calasparra rice | 360 g |
| Stock | ≈ 1.1 L |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Taste for salt before adding any**, because the cod carries salt.
2. Scatter the rice in an even layer. Don't stir after this point.
3. Cook high for 8 min, then medium-low for 10 min.
4. Rest 5 min.
