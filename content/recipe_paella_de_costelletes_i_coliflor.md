# Paella de Costillas y Coliflor / Paella de Costelletes i Col-i-flor

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** recorded in a Valencian home-recipe collection hosted by the Universitat de València (uv.es/baldovi), which **gives no town attribution**. The town variations below are therefore not documented.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Province of València (home cooking; town not specified).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). Cooked "until dry", covered with a lid or cloth if it dries too fast.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella over wood or gas.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A **winter** paella of the pig-and-cabbage season. Pork ribs and a piece of **pork liver** are browned with **cauliflower** florets (and optional *alls tendres*) until the cauliflower takes colour. It shows how flexible the Valencian paella frame is: when the summer beans of the [paella valenciana](recipe_paella_valenciana.md) are out of season, households switched to cauliflower and pork. The collection serves it, like its other paellas, with lime quarters.

**Culinary science:** browned cauliflower develops nutty pyrazines and sweet notes. Liver adds iron-rich savour, and some of it breaks down into the broth. Black pepper (half a teaspoon) is unusually generous for a Valencian paella and suits the pork.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Recorded version (uv.es/baldovi):** Ribs + liver + cauliflower + optional young garlic, paprika, black pepper, saffron.
*   **Related:** [Arroz con bacalao y coliflor](recipe_arroz_con_bacalao_y_coliflor.md) (the Lenten cod-and-cauliflower version) and [arròs de sèpia i col-i-flor](recipe_arros_de_sepia_i_coliflor.md) (caldós).
*   ⚠️ Town variants are not documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: recorded version (published quantities).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Pork ribs, small | 200 g |
| Pork liver | 125 g |
| Cauliflower florets | 500 g |
| Alls tendres (optional) | 5–6 |
| Olive oil | 150 ml |
| Pimentón | ½ tbsp |
| Black pepper | ½ tsp |
| Saffron, salt | to taste |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 400 g |
| Water | 2 × rice volume (≈ 0.9–1 L) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Brown:** Brown the ribs and liver in the oil. Add the cauliflower and garlic and fry until coloured.
2. **Paprika and water:** Add the pimentón (seconds), then the water, saffron and pepper. Boil.
3. **Rice:** When the meat is cooked, adjust the salt and water and add the rice.
4. **Fire:** High heat, then low, until dry. Cover with a lid or cloth if it dries too quickly.
5. **Rest:** Briefly. Serve with lime quarters.
