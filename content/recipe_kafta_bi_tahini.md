# Kafta bi-Tahini / كفتة بالطحينة

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** sources disagree on the traditional accompaniment: several Lebanese and Palestinian home cooks serve it with (vermicelli) rice, others say the Palestinian way is with bread. Both are documented; the rice service is given here.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Palestine and Lebanon (and Jordan, through Palestinian home cooking).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: baked kafta (spiced minced-meat patties), usually with potatoes, under a tangy lemon-tahini sauce, served with vermicelli rice or bread.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** An oven tray (*saniyeh*), originally sent to the neighbourhood bakery oven.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Kafta bi-tahini* is a tray dish: kafta mixed with onion and parsley, pressed into the tray or shaped into patties with sliced potatoes, baked, then covered with tahini thinned with lemon and water and baked again until the sauce sets. Palestine in a Dish calls it Palestinian comfort food, "often baked with potatoes"; Lebanese versions add pine nuts. Reem Kassis published a *kofta and tahini bake* in the Washington Post.

The side is debated. One Lebanese casserole is paired with vermicelli rice and another site suggests "rice or flatbreads". In comments on a Palestinian recipe, one reader says the traditional side is rice and another says the Palestinian way is with bread.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Palestine (home style):** Kafta flattened into the tray, potato slices, tahini-lemon; bread or rice (contested).
*   **Lebanon:** Kafta balls in a light tahini sauce topped with pine nuts; vermicelli rice.
*   **Arab Table version:** Lean beef or leg of lamb with plenty of parsley.
*   **Potato-free version:** Kafta and tahini only, a faster weeknight dish.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Palestinian-Lebanese kafta bi-tahini with potatoes and vermicelli rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Minced beef or lamb (kafta) | 700 g |
| Onion, grated + parsley, chopped | 1 + 1 cup |
| 7-spice, salt, pepper | 1.5 tsp total |
| Potatoes, 1 cm slices | 600 g |
| Tahini | 180 g |
| Lemon juice + water | 80 ml + 250 ml |
| Garlic, crushed | 2 cloves |
| Pine nuts (Lebanese garnish) | 30 g |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice (medium-grain) | 300 g |
| Vermicelli | 50 g |
| Water for the rice | 450 ml |
| Butter | 30 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Kafta:** Mix the meat, onion, parsley and spices; press into the tray or shape 12 patties.
2. **First bake:** Fry or roast the potato slices briefly; arrange with the kafta and bake at 200 °C for **20 min**.
3. **Sauce:** Whisk the tahini, lemon, garlic and water to a pourable sauce with a little salt.
4. **Second bake:** Pour the sauce over and bake **15–20 min** until set and lightly golden.
5. **Rice:** Toast the vermicelli in butter, add the rice and boiling salted water, cook covered 15 min, rest 10 min.
6. **Serve:** Top with fried pine nuts; serve with the rice (or bread).
