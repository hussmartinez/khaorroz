# Arrossos d'Espanya: A Field Atlas of Spanish Rice

A granular, multilingual (Castellano / Valencià / Català / English) atlas of Spain's rice traditions. It is rooted in the **Valencian Community** (València, Alacant/Alicante, Castelló/Castellón) and extends to every other rice-cooking region of Spain. **304 recipes** document **town-by-town variations** wherever the sources support them, plus forgotten and endangered village rices. For the long story see [A History of Rice in Spain](history.md).

## 1. How to Use This Atlas
*   **Region pages** describe cultural sub-regions and link to their recipes.
*   **Recipe files** follow a fixed layout: Geographic & Texture Profile → Historical & Cultural Context → Town-by-Town Variations → 4-Person Master Recipe → Authority Links → **Related Dishes** (cousins in other towns and regions, grouped by dish family).
*   **Nomenclature:** each dish carries its Spanish and local names (*Arroz* / *Arròs*, *Paella*, *Caldero*).
*   **⚠️ Confidence flags:** poorly documented dishes carry an explicit note; reconstructed variations are labelled as such. Verify them locally before citing them as canonical.
*   **Quantities** are for 4 people. Liquid ratios depend on the variety: Bomba absorbs ~15–20 % more than Senia/Bahía.

## 7. Shared Technique Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Fondo** | The flavour base: browned proteins + sofrito + reduced broth |
| **Sofregit / Sofrito** | Garlic and tomato (± pimentón) fried until jammy. Onion is mostly excluded in Valencian seco rices |
| **Salmorreta** | Alicante paste of ñora, garlic, tomato and parsley |
| **Ñora** | Small round dried sweet pepper (Segura basin). Fry it for seconds only |
| **Picada** | Mortar-pounded paste (garlic, ñora, parsley, fried bread or almond) stirred into the broth |
| **Socarrat** | Caramelised rice crust on the pan base, formed in the last 1–2 min |
| **Sarmiento / Sarment** | Vine-pruning wood; fast, very hot fire |
| **Serranas / Vaquetes** | Mountain snails (*Sphincterochila* / *Iberus*) |
| **Morralla** | Small mixed rockfish for stock |
| **Allioli** | Garlic + oil emulsion, served with seafood rices |

## 8. Methodology & Limitations
*   Research was run in Spanish, Valencian/Catalan and English using "Paella", "Arroz…" and "Arròs…" nomenclature. Sources are prioritised in this order: (1) Generalitat / municipal tourism and town-hall documents, (2) regional press (El Español Alicante/Valencia, AQUÍ Medios, Valencia Bonita, Alicante Plaza), (3) established recipe media (Directo al Paladar, 3Cat Cuines, Consum).
*   **Town-by-town variations** are documented from these sources where available. Where sources were thin (marked ⚠️), variations are reconstructed from comarca practice and clearly flagged. Please verify them with local cooks before citing them as canonical.
*   Every link was found through live web search in November 2026. Some regional press URLs may change over time.
