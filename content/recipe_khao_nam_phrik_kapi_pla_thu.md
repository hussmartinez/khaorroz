# Khao Nam Phrik Kapi Pla Thu / ข้าว น้ำพริกกะปิ ปลาทู

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand (Bangkok, the Gulf coast at Samut Songkhram/Mae Klong for *pla thu*).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: the classic Central Thai set of plain rice, a pungent shrimp-paste chili relish (*nam phrik kapi*), crisp-fried short mackerel (*pla thu*) and a platter of raw and blanched vegetables.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** Stone mortar (*khrok hin*) for the relish; a wok for frying the mackerel; *pla thu* sold steamed in small bamboo baskets.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A dip or relish (*khrueang chim*) is considered an essential element of the traditional Thai meal, and *nam phrik kapi* is the quintessential Central Thai relish: fermented shrimp paste pounded with small chilies, garlic, sugar and lime ([Wikipedia – Nam phrik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nam_phrik); [Wikipedia – Thai cuisine](https://ipfs.ssi.eecc.de/ipns/en.wikipedia-on-ipfs.org/wiki/Thai_cuisine)). Eaten with crisp-fried mackerel (*pla thu*), a heap of rice and vegetables such as okra, Thai eggplant, long beans and cabbage, it forms the everyday *khao nam phrik* set ([Bangkok Glutton](https://bangkokglutton.com/page/138)). Dusit University lists it among the Central Thai food sets ([SDU Sharing](https://sdusharing.dusit.ac.th/?p=29634)).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **With pla thu:** the classic Central pairing.
*   **With fried catfish or grilled snakehead:** common substitutes.
*   **Southern Thailand:** much spicier relish, served with cooling cucumber ([Bangkok Glutton](https://bangkokglutton.com/page/138)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Shrimp paste (*kapi*), toasted | 2 tbsp |
| Garlic, bird's-eye chilies | 4 cloves, 8–10 |
| Palm sugar, fish sauce, lime juice | 1 tbsp, 1 tbsp, 3 tbsp |
| Pea eggplants (optional, crushed) | 2 tbsp |
| Short mackerel (*pla thu*), steamed | 8 small |
| Vegetables: cucumber, long beans, Thai eggplant, cabbage, okra (raw or blanched) | 500 g |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) | 400 g, rinsed (+ 520 ml water, absorption method) |
| Relish per person | ≈ 2 tbsp |
| Mackerel per person | 2 small fish |
| Rice per person | ≈ 200 g cooked |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Toast the shrimp paste wrapped in banana leaf or foil.
2. Pound garlic and chilies, then the shrimp paste; season with sugar, fish sauce and lime; stir in pea eggplants.
3. Fry the mackerel until crisp.
4. Cook the rice: rinse 400 g jasmine rice until the water runs nearly clear, add 520 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min, then rest 10 min covered (or use an electric rice cooker / *mo hung khao*).
5. Serve the relish in a small bowl at the centre with the fish, vegetables and rice; each mouthful takes a little of everything.
