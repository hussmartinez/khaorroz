# Khanom Tako / ขนมตะโก้

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the published recipes agree on the method, but I found no documented origin.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand (Bangkok, Ayutthaya, Phetchaburi markets), sold nationwide.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Rice-flour dessert in two layers: a sweet, jelly-firm bottom (often with taro, water chestnut or corn) under a salty, silky coconut-cream top thickened with rice flour, set in little pandan-leaf boxes (*krathong bai toei*).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A brass pan for stirring the batter over charcoal, and small boxes folded from pandan or banana leaf.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Tako is one of the classic two-layer Thai sweets. It comes in many fillings (taro, water chestnut, corn) and is sold in banana-leaf or pandan cups, small cups or large trays ([Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/food/2887408)). The base uses mostly rice flour with a little tapioca flour, cooked with sugar and pandan water. The top layer is coconut milk thickened with a little rice flour and seasoned with salt, for example 360 g coconut milk, 25 g rice flour and 1 tsp salt ([Phol Food Mafia – taro tako](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=45642); [Wongnai](https://www.wongnai.com/recipes/ugc/c23f2b7d1a3948468a70fe9d4dc5172b)). The leaf boxes are filled with the sweet layer first, and the coconut top is spooned on afterwards ([GotoKnow – folding pandan boxes](https://www.gotoknow.org/posts/214202)).

**Culinary science:** rice flour's amylose sets into a firm gel as it cools, while coconut fat and salt in the top layer contrast with the sweet base.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Taro tako (tako phueak):** diced taro in the base ([Phol Food Mafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=45642)).
*   **Water-chestnut tako (tako haeo):** pandan-green base with water chestnut ([Wongnai](https://www.wongnai.com/recipes/ugc/c23f2b7d1a3948468a70fe9d4dc5172b)).
*   **Corn and sago:** corn kernels and sago pearls ([Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/food/2887408)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice flour (base) | 120 g |
| Tapioca flour (base) | 30 g |
| Pandan juice (base) | 500 ml |
| Sugar (base) | 150 g |
| Water chestnut or taro, diced | 150 g |
| Coconut milk (top) | 360 g |
| Rice flour (top) | 25 g |
| Salt (top) | 1 tsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice flour total | 145 g |
| Base : top | about 2 : 1 by volume |
| Pandan boxes | about 20 small |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Base:** Whisk the rice and tapioca flours, pandan juice and sugar; cook in a brass pan over low heat, stirring, until thick and glossy. Fold in the water chestnut.
2. **Fill:** Spoon into the pandan boxes, about two-thirds full.
3. **Top:** Cook the coconut milk, rice flour and salt, stirring, until it just thickens.
4. **Finish:** Spoon the top over the set base and leave to cool before serving.
