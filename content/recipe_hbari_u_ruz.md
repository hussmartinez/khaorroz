# Hbari u Ruz (Gaza Squid with Toasted Rice) / حباري ورز

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** recorded in *The Gaza Kitchen* ("Calamari and Toasted Rice": "an extremely satisfying dish, with richly flavored rice and browned calamari", with flavour from onions "stirred over a slow flame until brown and sticky"). The book's ingredient list and method weren't accessible, so the blueprint is reconstructed from that headnote and Gaza seafood technique. The Arabic spelling of *hbari* (Gaza dialect for squid) is an inference.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** The Gaza coast: Gaza City's port and fishing quarter, Deir al-Balah, Khan Yunis.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Coastal seafood rice. Rice toasted in oil and cooked dry and fluffy, coloured brown by sticky caramelised onions, with browned squid on top.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A heavy pot or deep pan on a gas ring.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Gaza's fishermen work a narrow, heavily restricted sea. Squid, sardines and small fish have long fed the city. This dish follows the same coastal logic as Levantine fish rice ([sayadieh](recipe_sayadieh.md)): onions cooked very slowly until dark and jammy colour and flavour the rice. Here browned squid replaces fish. A Spanish reader will recognise the kinship with [arroz con chipirones](recipe_arroz_con_chipirones_y_ajos_tiernos.md) and [arroz negro](recipe_arroz_negro.md), Mediterranean cephalopod rices built on a long-cooked base.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Gaza City (documented):** Caramelised onions, browned squid, toasted rice.
*   **Gaza spicing (⚠️):** Gaza cooks typically finish seafood with green chilli, garlic, dill and lemon, the "herby, spicy, peppery, lemony" coastal style. Not specific to this dish in the sources.
*   **Lebanese/Syrian coast comparison:** Squid usually goes into *sayadieh*-style rice or is fried and served beside plain rice.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Gaza City (reconstructed).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Squid, cleaned, in rings and tentacles | 700 g |
| Onions, thinly sliced | 3 large (≈ 600 g) |
| Olive oil | 6 tbsp |
| Garlic, crushed | 3 cloves |
| Cumin, coriander seed (ground) | 1 tsp each |
| Green chilli, chopped (optional) | 1 |
| Lemon, dill | to serve |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Medium/long-grain rice, rinsed | 300 g |
| Fish stock or water | 600 ml |
| Salt | 1½ tsp |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Onions:** Cook the onions in 4 tbsp of the oil over a slow flame, stirring often, until deep brown and sticky (30–40 min).
2. **Squid:** In a very hot pan with the remaining oil, brown the squid in batches (1–2 min). Add the garlic, spices and chilli for the last 30 seconds, then set aside.
3. **Toast the rice:** Stir the rice into the onions and toast for 3 minutes until the grains turn opaque and golden.
4. **Cook:** Add the hot stock and salt, cover and cook on low for 18 minutes.
5. **Finish:** Fold half the squid through the rice, lay the rest on top, and finish with dill and lemon wedges.
