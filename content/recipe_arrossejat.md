# Arroz Rossejat / Arrossejat (L'Ametlla de Mar)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Catalunya](provincial_catalunya.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the town's festival, the **Diada de l'Arrossejat** in L'Ametlla de Mar (40 editions), is well documented, but **I found no published recipe**. The method below is reconstructed from the *rossejat* technique (toasting the dry grain in oil, then cooking it in fish broth), which is documented for the town's sister dish, *fideus rossejats de la Cala*. Verify it with the Confraria de Pescadors or the festival organisers.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Baix Ebre coast: L'Ametlla de Mar (La Cala), L'Ampolla.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella or flat cassola on the fishing boats, over a small fire.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
L'Ametlla de Mar's **Diada de l'Arrossejat** has run for about **40 years**. Around fifty amateur cooks compete for prizes, and the festival champions the dish as heritage "born on the fishing boats". It is the rice twin of the town's **fideus rossejats**, a fisherman's noodle dish built on fish broth and served alongside a plate of fish and potatoes. *Rossejar* means "to make golden": the dry rice (or noodles) is **fried in oil until golden** before the broth goes in.

**Culinary science:** toasting the dry grain in fat browns its surface starches and seals it. The rice then absorbs broth more slowly, stays separate and tastes nutty. This is the same principle behind fideuà and its *rossejat* noodle relatives. The fish broth, made from the boat's unsold catch, carries all the flavour.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **L'Ametlla de Mar (La Cala):** Arrossejat with rockfish broth and allioli. The fideus version is served with fish and potatoes.
*   ⚠️ **L'Ampolla / Ebre coast (reconstructed):** Prawns from the Delta may be added on top.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: reconstructed L'Ametlla de Mar style.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rockfish (morralla) + heads | 1.2 kg |
| Water | 2.2 L |
| Onion, tomato, garlic (for the broth) | 1 / 1 / 3 cloves |
| Olive oil | 100 ml |
| Garlic, sliced | 3 cloves |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 100 g |
| Pimentón dulce | 2 g |

Simmer the fish with the onion, tomato, garlic and water for **30 min**, then strain. You need about **1.2 L**.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 380 g |
| Fish broth | ≈ 1.15 L |
| Allioli | 150 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rossejar:** Fry the dry rice in the oil, stirring constantly, until **golden** (**5–6 min**).
2. Add the garlic, then the tomato and pimentón (**2 min**).
3. Add the boiling broth. Cook high for 8 min, then medium-low for 8 min, without stirring.
4. Rest 3 min. Serve with allioli, and with the broth's fish and potatoes if you like.
