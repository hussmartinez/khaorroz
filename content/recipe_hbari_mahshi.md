# Hbari Mahshi (Gaza Rice-Stuffed Squid) / حباري محشي

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Gaza City (around the old fishing port) and, historically, Jaffa (Yafa).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Stuffed seafood (*mahashi* of the sea). Small squid are filled with rice, shrimp, dill and spices and slow-baked until meltingly tender, with soft, well-seasoned rice inside.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A dry pan for the tentacles, then a covered baking dish in a low oven.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*The Gaza Kitchen* adapts this dish from the one served at Abu Hasira's seafood restaurant beside Gaza's old fishing port: "The final result is meltingly tender, a very elegant meal" ([ckbk – Abu Hasira's Rice-and-Shrimp-Stuffed Squid](https://app.ckbk.com/recipe/gaza70968c10s001ss004r003/abu-hasiras-rice-and-shrimp-stuffed-squid)). *Hbari* is the local word for squid and octopus in Gaza City and old Yafa ([ckbk – The Gaza Kitchen](https://app.ckbk.com/recipe/gaza70968c10s001ss004r002)). An AP report on the book describes "tiny squid stuffed with dill, spices, raisins and rice" ([Ahram Online](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/67924.aspx)). The Abu Hasira family also gave the book its [sayadieh](recipe_sayadieh.md), and the same kitchen produces [hbari u ruz](recipe_hbari_u_ruz.md), squid with toasted rice.

**Culinary science:** the chopped tentacles are dry-fried first to drive off their water, which concentrates flavour for the stuffing. Squid either cooks very fast or very slowly; this recipe uses a long, low bake (150 °C), so the collagen relaxes and the bodies turn tender rather than rubbery.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Abu Hasira (Gaza City port):** Rice + shrimp + dill; a slow bake.
*   **Raisin version:** Dill, spices, raisins and rice, as in the AP description.
*   **Yafa:** The word *hbari* is shared with old Yafa. ⚠️ No Yafa-specific recipe was found.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Abu Hasira style. Quantities are reconstructed because the published page is truncated.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Small squid, cleaned (tentacles kept) | 500 g |
| Small shrimp, peeled and chopped | 150 g |
| Onion, finely chopped | 1 |
| Garlic + fresh dill + green chili | 4 cloves / 1 bunch / 1 |
| Olive oil | 4 tbsp |
| Cumin, coriander, salt, pepper | ½ tsp each / to taste |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short-grain rice, soaked 20 min | 150 g |
| Raisins (optional) | 30 g |
| Tomato-water or fish stock for the dish | 250 ml |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Preheat the oven to 150 °C. Chop the tentacles finely and dry-fry them on high heat until their liquid evaporates.
2. Add the oil, onion, garlic and chili and cook for 5 min. Add the shrimp, spices, drained rice, raisins and half the dill, and stir for 2 min (the rice stays raw).
3. Fill each squid body two-thirds full, because the rice swells, and close it with a toothpick.
4. Lay the squid in a dish, pour in the stock, cover tightly and bake for **1 h 15 min**.
5. Uncover, baste and bake for 10 more min. Check that the rice is tender.
6. Scatter the remaining dill and serve with lemon and a Gaza chili salad (*dagga*).
