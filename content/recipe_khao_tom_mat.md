# Khao Tom Mat / ข้าวต้มมัด (Khao Tom Phat / ข้าวต้มผัด)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Nationwide. It's found in almost every province, and is especially associated with the Ok Phansa / Tak Bat Thewo merit-making.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** A banana-leaf parcel of sticky rice. Glutinous rice is stir-fried in sweet-salty coconut milk, wrapped around a piece of ripe banana (often with black beans), tied in pairs and boiled until dense, chewy and fragrant.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok (*kratha*) to fry the rice, banana leaves (young *kluai tani* leaves are preferred) with bamboo or *tok* string ties, and a large pot for boiling.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Khao tom mat* means "tied boiled rice", and its other name, *khao tom phat*, means "stir-fried boiled rice". It is everyday street and market food, but also a ***khrueang thai than***, an offering given to monks at the alms-round after Ok Phansa, beside its tailed cousin [khao tom luk yon](recipe_khao_tom_luk_yon.md). *Silpa Wattanatham* notes that the parcels don't have to be tied in pairs, even though that is the familiar form. Their origin date isn't documented. They belong to the large Southeast Asian family of leaf-wrapped sticky rice (compare Khmer *num ansom*).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Central (classic):** *Kluai nam wa* banana with black beans, in *kluai tani* leaves, tied in pairs.
*   **Black sticky rice version:** Purple-black glutinous rice instead of white.
*   **Southern counterpart:** Kapho-leaf [khanom tom](recipe_khao_tom_luk_yon.md) for Chak Phra.
*   **Grilled cousin:** *Khao niao ping* (banana-filled sticky rice grilled in leaf over charcoal) is common in Isan and Lanna markets.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **central khao tom mat with banana and black beans**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Coconut milk | 400 ml |
| Sugar | 80 g |
| Salt | 1½ tsp |
| Black beans, soaked and boiled until just tender | 50 g |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Glutinous rice, soaked 3 h | 400 g |
| Ripe *kluai nam wa* bananas, halved lengthwise | 6 |
| Banana leaves, wiped and softened over flame | 12 pieces |
| Makes | 12 parcels (6 pairs) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Fry:** Stir the drained rice in the coconut milk, sugar and salt over low heat until the liquid is absorbed and the grains are half-cooked (**15 minutes**). Fold in the beans and cool for 30 minutes.
2. **Wrap:** Spread rice on a leaf, lay a banana half on it, cover with more rice and roll into a firm log, folding the ends under.
3. **Tie:** Pair two parcels face to face and tie them with string.
4. **Boil:** Cover with water and boil for **1–1.5 hours**.
5. **Serve:** Cool and unwrap. Eat as a snack or offer at the alms-round.
