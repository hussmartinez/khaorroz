# Arroz con Alcachofas de Benicarló / Arròs amb Carxofes de Benicarló

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de Castelló](provincial_castellon.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** El Baix Maestrat (Benicarló, Vinaròs, Peníscola, Cálig), extending south into La Plana Alta.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry), the classic paella form. Local restaurants also serve it meloso; see §3.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A steel paella over wood or gas, or a clay *cassola*.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The **Alcachofa de Benicarló** has held a **Denominación de Origen** since 18 September 1998. It is a rounded, compact, tender *Blanca de Tudela*-type artichoke that grows in the mild coastal plain between the Serra d'Irta and the sea. Every January Benicarló celebrates the **Festa de la Carxofa** with tastings, and local restaurants make artichoke rice in seco, meloso and caldoso versions. The Generalitat notes that the traditional paella "admits the seasonal Benicarló artichoke very well". In the Baix Maestrat the artichoke rice ranges from inland pork-and-artichoke paellas to coastal versions with **galeres** (mantis shrimp; see [recipe_arroz_con_galeras_y_alcachofas.md](recipe_arroz_con_galeras_y_alcachofas.md)) or **boquerones**.

**Culinary science:** artichoke contains **cynarin** and chlorogenic acids, which make other foods taste sweeter afterwards and turn brown through polyphenol oxidation. Frying the artichoke pieces **first, in plenty of oil**, until their cut faces caramelise reduces the oxidation and adds nutty Maillard notes. Lemon on the cut surfaces delays browning before cooking.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)

*   **The Benicarló Style:** Artichoke-forward, with **pork** (*magro* or ribs) and sometimes **sepia**. Artichokes in quarters, fried until golden. Seco.
*   **The Vinaròs Style:** Artichoke + **galeres** or **llagostins** (seafood-leaning). Often **meloso**. Fish stock.
*   **The Peníscola Style:** Artichoke + **boquerones** or **sepia** + ajos tiernos. Seco.
*   **The Inland Baix Maestrat (Cálig, Sant Jordi) Style:** Artichoke + **rabbit** or **pork ribs**, sometimes **habas**. Paella-valenciana frame.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Benicarló** (pork + artichoke, seco).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 100 ml |
| Alcachofas de Benicarló (D.O.), cleaned, quartered, lemon-rubbed | 8 (≈ 600 g cleaned) |
| Pork loin / magro, cubed + pork ribs | 250 g + 250 g |
| Garlic | 3 cloves |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Pimentón dulce | 3 g |
| Saffron | 0.3 g |
| Water or light chicken-pork stock | 1.5 L |

**Extraction:** Fry the artichokes in the hot oil until golden at the edges (**6–8 min**), then set them aside. Brown the pork (**10 min**). Add the garlic, pimentón (5 s) and tomato (5 min). Add the water and simmer for **20 min**. Return the artichokes for the last **5 min**.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short-grain rice | 400 g |
| Stock in the pan | ≈ 1.2 L |
| Artichokes | 8 |
| Pork | 500 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Preparation:** Clean the artichokes into lemon water, then dry them well before frying.
2. **Fondo:** As above.
3. **Rice:** Scatter and level. Don't stir after this.
4. **Fire:** High for 8 min → medium-low for 8 min → high for 1–2 min.
5. **Finish/Resting Phase:** Rest 4 min.
