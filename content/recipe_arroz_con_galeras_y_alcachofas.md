# Arroz con Galeras y Alcachofas / Arròs amb Galeres i Carxofes

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de Castelló](provincial_castellon.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** El Baix Maestrat coast: Vinaròs, Benicarló, Peníscola. It continues north into the Ebro Delta (Terres de l'Ebre).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Meloso** (creamy), the most common restaurant presentation during the Jornades de la Galera. Seco and caldoso versions also appear on the same menus.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A steel paella or clay cassola over gas.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The **galera** (*Squilla mantis*, mantis shrimp) is the winter jewel of the Vinaròs and Benicarló trawler fleets. Its sweet, lobster-like flesh is hard to extract, so it is ideal for **flavouring stock**. Vinaròs runs the **Jornades Gastronòmiques de la Galera** every February, and their menus feature rice with galeres in seco, meloso and caldoso forms, most often paired with **alcachofa de Benicarló**, whose season (Nov–Mar) matches the galera's. Other pairings documented on those menus include galeres with **chickpeas and spinach**, with **monkfish and baby cuttlefish**, and with **boletus**.

**Culinary science:** the galera's shell holds most of its flavour (astaxanthin-bound fats and free amino acids). Crushing and frying the bodies and simmering them into the stock extracts that flavour. A few whole galeres are kept back and laid on top for the final minutes so they stay juicy. A meloso finish suits the dish because the gelatinous shellfish stock binds with rice starch.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)

*   **The Vinaròs Style:** Galeres + Benicarló artichoke. **Meloso**. Fumet made from galeres and rockfish. Garlic + tomato sofrito.
*   **The Benicarló Style:** More artichoke than galera. **Seco** in the paella.
*   **The Peníscola Style:** Galeres + **sepia** + ajos tiernos.
*   **The Ebro Delta (Catalan border) echo:** Galeres + **delta rice** + sometimes **clams**, cooked **caldós**.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Vinaròs meloso**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Galeres (mantis shrimp), fresh | 800 g (≈ 16–20) |
| Rockfish / fish bones | 500 g |
| Water | 2 L |
| Olive oil | 90 ml |
| Alcachofas de Benicarló, in eighths | 6 |
| Garlic | 3 cloves |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Pimentón dulce | 2 g |
| Saffron | 0.2 g |

**Extraction:** Keep 8 of the best galeres whole. Crush the rest and fry them in 30 ml of the oil (**4 min**). Add the fish and water and simmer for **30 min**. Strain, pressing hard. Keep **1.5 L**.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short-grain rice | 350 g |
| Galera stock | 1.4–1.5 L (≈ 4:1, meloso) |
| Whole galeres for the top | 8 |
| Artichokes | 6 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Preparation:** Make the stock and clean the artichokes.
2. **Sofrito:** Fry the artichokes in the rest of the oil (6 min). Add the garlic, pimentón and tomato (5 min).
3. **Toasting:** Rice in, 1 min.
4. **Liquid Addition:** Boiling stock and saffron.
5. **Fire:** Strong for 8 min → medium for 8 min, stirring gently 2–3 times. Lay the whole galeres on top at **min 12**.
6. **Finish/Resting Phase:** Rest 2 min. The texture should be loose, and the rice should ripple when the pan is shaken.
