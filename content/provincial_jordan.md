# Jordan / الأردن

> ⬅ [README](README.md)

## 1. Overview
Jordan's rice cooking grows out of three overlapping traditions: the **Bedouin** of the desert east and south (herds, milk, meat, bread baked on the *saj*), the **fellahin** of the northern and western highlands (wheat, bulgur, olive oil, vegetables), and the **urban** kitchens of al-Salt, Irbid and later Amman, shaped by Damascus, Jerusalem and Nablus. Rice was not native to any of them. It arrived as a traded grain and, during the 20th century, gradually **displaced bulgur** as the festive grain. Sources date the shift anywhere from the late 19th century to the 1950s.

The national dish, [mansaf](recipe_mansaf.md), brings these worlds together: lamb from the herds, **jameed** (sun-dried, salted yogurt, above all from al-Karak) from the Bedouin and village dairy, **shrak** bread, and rice. In 2022 UNESCO inscribed it on its list of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. The Bedouin south adds the pit-oven feast, [zarb](recipe_zarb.md). Everyday Jordanian home cooking shares the wider Levantine repertoire: **maqluba** (the upside-down rice also central to Palestinian cooking, see [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md)), vegetable stews (*yakhni*) such as okra or green beans served over rice with vermicelli, and stuffed vegetables. On the Red Sea, **Aqaba**'s signature dish is the fish-and-rice **sayadieh**, with rice browned with onions and cooked in fish broth.

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region | Rice personality | Key places |
|---|---|---|
| **Amman & Zarqa** | Urban melting pot: mansaf for occasions, maqluba and *yakhni* with rice at home | Amman, Zarqa |
| **Al-Salt & al-Balqa** | Celebrated mansaf; *samn balqawi* (Balqa clarified butter); seasonal-vegetable cooking | Al-Salt, Fuheis |
| **Al-Karak & the south** | Home of the most prized **jameed karaki**, made from sheep's milk each spring | Al-Karak, Zahoum, Tafileh |
| **Irbid, Ajloun & the north (Hauran edge)** | Mansaf ringed with bulgur *kubbeh*; urban *shakriyeh* with fresh yogurt; strong bulgur and freekeh tradition | Irbid, Ajloun, Jerash, Ramtha |
| **Madaba & the plateau** | Village mansaf and maqluba | Madaba, Dhiban |
| **Ma'an, Wadi Rum & the Bedouin desert** | Older meat, butter and bread forms of mansaf; **zarb** pit-oven feasts | Ma'an, Wadi Rum, Hisma |
| **Aqaba & the Red Sea** | **Sayadieh** (fish and rice); a fish mansaf is reported ⚠️ | Aqaba |
| **Jordan Valley (al-Ghor)** | Farming valley; vegetable stews with rice | Deir Alla, al-Shuneh |
