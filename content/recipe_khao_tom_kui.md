# Khao Tom Kui / ข้าวต้มกุ๊ย

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok's Chinatown (Yaowarat) and night-time rice-soup shops (*khao tom to rung*) nationwide.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Rice porridge set: plain, watery Teochew rice gruel (*muay*) eaten with many small salty side dishes — stir-fried morning glory, salted egg, pickled greens, braised pork, fried fish.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A big aluminium or clay pot for the gruel; woks for the side dishes; rows of trays (*thad*) for the ready-made sides.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Khao tom kui* is the Thai name for the Teochew plain rice gruel with sides; once seen as "poor people's food", it became one of Thailand's most popular late-night meals, with sides such as stir-fried morning glory, stir-fried clams and garlic chives with tofu ([Silpa-mag](https://www.silpa-mag.com/culture/article_108493)). Night shops (*khao tom to rung*) also sell fish or prawn khao tom, noodles and yam salads ([Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.co.th/lp/apollo-nongchok/catalog/item/ร้านข้าวต้มกุ๊ยหนองจอก-EcdJL6V)). ⚠️ Origins of the word *kui* are explained in a Thairath article whose text we could not read ([Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/food/2755540)). Distinct from the seasoned [khao tom](recipe_khao_tom.md) with meat cooked into the broth.

**Culinary science:** unseasoned gruel is the neutral, hydrating base that lets very salty preserved sides (salted egg, *chai po*, pickled mustard) carry the meal.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Yaowarat Teochew shops:** dozens of pre-cooked sides on trays, chosen by pointing ([Silpa-mag](https://www.silpa-mag.com/culture/article_108493)).
*   **Khao tom to rung (all-night):** cooked-to-order stir-fries plus khao tom pla/kung ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/articles/5749310/)).
*   **Home version:** plain gruel with salted egg, *chai po* omelette and pickled cabbage.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Teochew-style plain gruel with four sides**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice | 200 g |
| Water | 2 L |
| Morning glory (*phak bung*), garlic, chili, soybean paste | 300 g, 4 cloves, 2, 1 tbsp |
| Salted duck eggs | 2 |
| Preserved radish (*chai po*) + eggs (omelette) | 40 g + 3 |
| Pickled mustard greens (*phak kat dong*) | 100 g |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice : water | 1 : 10 |
| Sides | 3–5 small plates for the table |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Gruel:** boil the rinsed rice in the water, then simmer 25–30 min until grains burst but the liquid stays thin.
2. **Morning glory:** stir-fry fast in a smoking wok with garlic, chili and soybean paste (*phak bung fai daeng*).
3. **Chai po omelette:** fry the chopped preserved radish, pour over beaten egg and fry until set.
4. **Serve:** ladle gruel into bowls; set out the sides, salted eggs halved and the pickled greens.
