# Islas Baleares / Illes Balears

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Catalunya](provincial_catalunya.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

## 1. Overview
The Balearic Islands grow almost no rice, but they have their own rice cooking. It is mostly **caldós** (soupy) and spiced, and it comes from the **winter pig slaughter** (*matances*) and the **fishing boats**. Mallorca's emblem is the **arròs brut** ("dirty rice"), whose spice-and-liver picada clouds the broth. Eivissa's equivalent is the **arròs de matances**. Menorca built its rice on the **caldereta de llagosta** of Fornells. Across the islands, fishermen's **bullit de peix** (fish boiled with potatoes) is followed by rice cooked in its broth.

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Island / area | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **Mallorca – interior & Tramuntana** | Arròs brut (game, liver picada, spices), mushrooms | Inca, Sóller, Pollença, Llucmajor |
| **Mallorca – coast** | Fishermen's arròs caldós de peix | Palma, Port de Sóller, Cala Rajada |
| **Eivissa & Formentera** | Arròs de matances, bullit de peix amb arròs a banda | Sant Antoni, Santa Eulària, Sant Josep |
| **Menorca** | Arròs de llagosta (caldereta family) | Fornells, Ciutadella |

**Not yet documented:** *Arròs sec* (a dry rice of Mallorca and Eivissa) was requested, but I found no source naming it in this research. It may exist under another local name.

**False friend:** Menorca's ***arròs de la terra*** ("rice of the land") contains **no rice**. It is coarsely ground **wheat** baked with butifarrons, sobrassada, pork ribs and sweet potato, traditionally on pig-slaughter day, and is described as an Arab-heritage dish ([Descobreix Menorca – Country rice](https://www.descobreixmenorca.com/en/?p=10331)). It is not listed as a rice recipe.
