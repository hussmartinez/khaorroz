# Nam Phrik Long Ruea / น้ำพริกลงเรือ

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** Accounts differ on its creator (Queen Sadub Ladawan vs. Chao Chom Sadub, a royal consort); the boat-outing story is traditional attribution without a located primary source.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok court cuisine (Suan Sunandha Palace), reign of Rama V.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: a fried shrimp-paste chili relish combined with crisp catfish floss, sweet pork and salted egg, eaten with rice and vegetables; a court dish.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok for frying the relish; a stone mortar (*khrok*).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Nam phrik long ruea* ('chili relish for the boat') is a fried *nam phrik* eaten with vegetables. Tradition dates it to the reign of King Rama V and a boat outing at Suan Sunandha Palace, when leftover ingredients such as crisp catfish and sweet pork were fried into the relish. English sources credit Queen Sadub Ladawan and say the recipe was recorded by M.L. Neuang Ninrat, while a Silpa-mag tag page credits Chao Chom Sadub, a royal consort ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nam_phrik_long_ruea); [Silpa-mag](https://www.silpa-mag.com/tag/%e0%b8%99%e0%b9%89%e0%b8%b3%e0%b8%9e%e0%b8%a3%e0%b8%b4%e0%b8%81%e0%b8%a5%e0%b8%87%e0%b9%80%e0%b8%a3%e0%b8%b7%e0%b8%ad)). It sits alongside the court rices [khao chae](recipe_khao_chae.md) and [khao tang na tang](recipe_khao_tang_na_tang.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Court style:** with crisp catfish floss (*pla duk fu*), sweet pork (*mu wan*) and salted duck egg.
*   **Home style:** shrimp-paste relish fried with sweet pork only.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Nam phrik kapi (see the khao nam phrik set) | 6 tbsp |
| Oil | 3 tbsp |
| Palm sugar | 1 tbsp |
| Crisp catfish floss (*pla duk fu*) | 60 g |
| Sweet pork (*mu wan*), shredded | 80 g |
| Salted duck eggs, halved | 2 |
| Fresh vegetables (cucumber, long beans, cabbage) | 400 g |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) | 400 g, rinsed (+ 520 ml water, absorption method) |
| Relish per person | ≈ 3 tbsp with toppings |
| Rice per person | ≈ 200 g cooked |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Fry the nam phrik kapi in oil with palm sugar until thick and glossy.
2. Fold in the sweet pork; top with catfish floss and salted egg.
3. Cook the rice: rinse 400 g jasmine rice until the water runs nearly clear, add 520 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min, then rest 10 min covered (or use an electric rice cooker / *mo hung khao*).
4. Serve with rice and vegetables, as on a royal boat picnic.
