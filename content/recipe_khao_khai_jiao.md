# Khao Khai Jiao / ข้าวไข่เจียว

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** All of Thailand; a Bangkok street and home staple.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** One-plate rice: hot jasmine rice under a puffy, deep-fried omelette with crisp lacy edges, eaten with sriracha-style chili sauce and cucumber.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A deep wok (*kratha*) with plenty of very hot oil; a ladle to baste.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Khai jiao* is the Thai omelette fried in a lot of hot oil so that it puffs and browns at the edges; over rice it becomes the cheapest, quickest comfort plate, eaten at breakfast or late at night ([Michelin Guide](https://guide.michelin.com/sg/en/article/dining-in/recipe-khai-jiao-fluffy-thai-style-omelette); [Makansutra](https://makansutra.com/khao-kai-jiao-aka-a-crispy-omelette-thai-style)). ⚠️ No source dates its origin. It also climbed to the top end: Bangkok's Jay Fai serves a celebrated crab omelette ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/eat-drink/2021/09/30/egg-citing-thailand-from-michelin-starred-crab-omelettes-to-golden-thread-n/2009521)).

**Culinary science:** pouring beaten egg from a height into oil at about 180 °C makes steam bubbles that inflate the omelette; a splash of fish sauce (and in some homes lime juice or rice flour) seasons and helps the crisp lace form.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Plain (*khai jiao*):** eggs and fish sauce only, over rice with chili sauce ([Michelin Guide](https://guide.michelin.com/sg/en/article/dining-in/recipe-khai-jiao-fluffy-thai-style-omelette)).
*   **Khai jiao mu sap:** with minced pork beaten in.
*   **Khao khai jiao song khrueang:** topped with a stir-fry of minced pork and vegetables ([Wikipedia – List of Thai dishes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Thai_dishes)).
*   **Khai jiao cha-om / pu:** with acacia leaves, or crab ([The World of Street Food](https://www.theworldofstreetfood.com/2025/12/creamy-thai-omelette-with-lump-crab.html)).
*   **Khao khai khon:** a soft, gently cooked omelette over rice, a recent café style.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Plain Bangkok khai jiao with minced pork**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Eggs | 8 |
| Minced pork (optional) | 120 g |
| Fish sauce | 4 tsp |
| White pepper | pinch |
| Neutral oil for frying | ≈ 500 ml |
| Sriracha-style chili sauce, cucumber | to serve |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice | 400 g raw |
| Omelette per plate | 2 eggs |
| Oil temperature | ≈ 180 °C |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** Cook 400 g jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) with 520 ml water; rest 10 min covered.
2. **Beat:** for each plate whisk 2 eggs with 1 tsp fish sauce, pepper and a quarter of the pork until foamy.
3. **Fry:** heat the oil in the wok until shimmering; pour the egg in from about 20 cm so it puffs.
4. **Baste and flip:** spoon oil over the top for 30–40 s, flip, fry until golden and crisp at the edges; drain.
5. **Serve:** slide onto rice with chili sauce and cucumber slices.
