# Yam Naem Khao Thot / ยำแหนมข้าวทอด

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok and Isan (Lao in origin, from Tha Deua, Vientiane).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Rice salad: deep-fried curried rice balls broken into crunchy pieces and tossed with sour fermented pork (*naem*), lime, chili, shallot, peanuts and herbs; eaten wrapped in lettuce.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok of oil for the rice balls; a mixing bowl; lettuce leaves to wrap.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Known in Laos as *nam khao*, the dish is described as a popular Lao appetiser from Tha Deua near Vientiane; it reached Isan and then Bangkok with Lao and Isan migrants who came to work, where it is known as *yam naem khao thot* ([Wikipedia – Nam khao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nam_khao)). ⚠️ Sources are mostly one Wikipedia article and its mirrors; no independent dating was found.

**Culinary science:** cooked rice bound with red curry paste and egg, fried into balls, gives a crisp shell and chewy core; breaking the balls exposes both textures to the lime dressing and the lactic sourness of *naem*.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Lao *nam khao* (Tha Deua):** the original, with Lao fermented pork ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nam_khao)).
*   **Bangkok *yam naem khao thot*:** sweeter dressing, served with cabbage and herbs ([Pholfoodmafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=20191)).
*   **With fresh pork (*naem mu sot*):** where *naem* is unavailable ([ImportFood](https://importfood.com/recipes/recipe/1271-crispy-rice-larb-pork-yum-khao-tod-naem-moo-sod)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Bangkok yam naem khao thot**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Cooked jasmine rice | 600 g |
| Red curry paste | 2 tbsp |
| Egg | 1 |
| Grated coconut (optional) | 2 tbsp |
| Fermented pork (*naem*), crumbled | 200 g |
| Shallots, spring onion, coriander, mint | to taste |
| Roasted peanuts, dried chilies | 50 g, 4 |
| Lime juice / fish sauce / sugar | 3 tbsp / 1½ tbsp / 1 tsp |
| Oil for deep-frying, lettuce leaves | as needed |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice | ≈ 220 g raw (600 g cooked) |
| Naem : fried rice | ≈ 1 : 3 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** cook 220 g jasmine rice with 300 ml water; cool.
2. **Balls:** mix rice with curry paste, egg and coconut; shape into tight balls.
3. **Fry:** deep-fry at 180 °C until deep golden and crisp (6–8 min); drain and cool.
4. **Toss:** break the balls into chunks; toss with naem, shallot, herbs, peanuts, fried chilies, lime, fish sauce and sugar.
5. **Serve:** with lettuce leaves to wrap.
