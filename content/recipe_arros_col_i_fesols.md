# Arroz, Col y Alubias / Arròs, Col i Fesols (Delta de l'Ebre)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Catalunya](provincial_catalunya.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** The Ebro Delta (Terres de l'Ebre): Deltebre (Jesús i Maria), Sant Jaume d'Enveja, Amposta.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Caldoso** (soupy). The RENC source states "el arroz debe presentarse caldoso".
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A large *cassola* or *olla* on the hearth. It was traditionally made in big batches.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The Ebro Delta concentrates most of Catalonia's rice cultivation. This is its **everyday winter pot**: rice, cabbage and white beans with a little salt pork. Families cooked it **in large quantities to last 4–5 days**, because every ingredient came from their own land and it cost almost nothing. Enriqueta Piñana, born in Jesús i Maria (Deltebre), told the *Revista Española de Nutrición Comunitaria* that her family ate it **three days a week**. It is still served in winter, at local festivals and in Delta homes, and it is recorded in the book *La cuina del delta de l'Ebre*.

**Culinary science:** the beans are cooked with the panceta, so their liquid becomes a pork-flavoured, starch-rich stock. Cabbage fried into the sofrito caramelises its sugars before the long simmer. The 5-minute rest lets the rice swell without losing the soupy body.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Deltebre (Jesús i Maria), RENC version:** Panceta, cabbage, white beans, onion, garlic, tomato, colourant.
*   **Richer variants (documented in RENC):** Add other pork cuts: **ears, bones, morcillas**.
*   Compare the Valencian [arròs amb fesols i naps](recipe_arroz_con_fesols_i_naps.md) and the Mallorcan [arròs brut](recipe_arros_brut.md).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Deltebre (RENC 2022)**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Dried white beans (*fesols*), soaked overnight | 200 g |
| Panceta | 100 g |
| Water for the beans | ≈ 2.5 L |
| Cabbage (*col*), washed, chopped | 1 medium |
| Onion | ½ |
| Garlic | 1 clove |
| Tomato | 1 |
| Olive oil | 50 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Delta rice | 400 g |
| Bean-and-panceta broth | ≈ 2 L (caldoso) |
| Colourant / salt | to taste |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Beans:** Cook the soaked beans with the panceta in plenty of water until tender (≈ 1 h).
2. **Sofrito:** In a cassola, fry the onion, garlic and tomato with the cabbage until well done.
3. **Combine:** Add the bean broth and the panceta.
4. **Rice:** Bring to the boil, add the rice, colourant, salt and the beans, and cook **≈ 15 min**.
5. **Rest:** **5 min**, then serve soupy.
