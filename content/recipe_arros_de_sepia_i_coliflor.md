# Arroz Caldoso de Sepia y Coliflor / Arròs de Sèpia i Col-i-flor

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** recorded in a Valencian home-recipe collection hosted by the Universitat de València (uv.es/baldovi) in its *arrossos sucosos / caldosos* section, with **no town attribution**.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Province of València (coastal home cooking).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Caldoso** (soupy).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** An earthenware casserole (*cassola*) or pot.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A winter soupy rice in which the **cuttlefish is boiled for half an hour first**. This is unusual, since most recipes sear it. The boiling makes a broth and tenderises the cuttlefish. Fried **cauliflower** and paprika are then added. It belongs to the Valencian *arrossos caldosos amb peix* family recorded in the same collection, alongside spinach and ray, cuttlefish, prawns and mussels, eel, and monkfish.

**Culinary science:** cuttlefish collagen tenderises with about 30 min of simmering. Searing gives colour, but boiling gives a gelatinous, savoury broth that coats the rice. Salt goes in **after tasting the broth**, because cuttlefish brings its own salinity.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Recorded version:** Cuttlefish boiled 30 min, cauliflower fried with paprika, 15 min more, then the rice.
*   **Caldós family (same source):** Spinach + beans or chickpeas + ray; prawns and mussels; eel; monkfish. Keep about 3 parts solids to 2 parts liquid.
*   **Seco cousin:** [Arroz con sepia y alcachofas](recipe_arroz_con_sepia_y_alcachofas.md).
*   ⚠️ Town variants are not documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: recorded version (published quantities; the source gives about 200 g of rice).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Cuttlefish, cleaned, in pieces | 400 g |
| Cauliflower pieces | 400–500 g |
| Olive oil | 200 ml |
| Pimentón | 1 tsp |
| Saffron, black pepper, salt | to taste |
| Water | ≈ 1.5 L |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 200 g (source) – 280 g (main course) |
| Broth at rice-in | ≈ 1.2 L |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Cuttlefish:** Boil it in the casserole for **≈ 30 min**.
2. **Cauliflower:** Fry it in the oil, add the pimentón, and add both to the casserole.
3. **Simmer:** **≈ 15 min**. Taste the broth, then salt it.
4. **Rice:** Adjust the liquid. Add the rice, saffron and pepper. Simmer **16–18 min**.
5. **Serve** at once, soupy.
