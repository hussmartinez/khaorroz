# Khanom Babin / ขนมบ้าบิ่น

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md) · [Southern Thailand](provincial_southern_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** both origin stories are unproven: "Pa Bin" of Tha Ruea, Ayutthaya, in the Fifth Reign, and the Portuguese *queijada de Coimbra*.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Ayutthaya (Tha Ruea) and the central plains; a famous souvenir sweet of Ko Yao Yai (Phang Nga) in the south.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Sticky rice dessert: small round cakes of glutinous rice flour, young coconut, coconut cream, sugar and egg, baked or griddled until golden and crisp at the edges and chewy inside.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A shallow iron mould or flat pan over charcoal, with charcoal on the lid. A banana-leaf liner is traditional.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Two stories explain the name. One holds that the cake comes from Tha Ruea district in Ayutthaya, made in the Fifth Reign by an aunt (*pa*) called Bin, so "khanom pa Bin" became *babin*. The other links it to the Portuguese *queijada de Coimbra*, with "-bra" heard as *ba* ([Wikipedia – Khanom babin](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khanom_babin)). Neither has a primary source. Old-style recipes use shredded mature-young coconut (*maphrao thuenthuek*) with glutinous rice flour, sugar and salt. A black-sticky-rice version mixes black and white glutinous rice flours ([Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/food/2632213)). On Ko Yao Yai, families pass down a young-coconut *babin* that is the island's best-known souvenir ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5633802/)).

**Culinary science:** glutinous rice flour gives the chewy, elastic middle. Coconut and sugar on the hot metal caramelise into a lacy, crisp rim.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Tha Ruea, Ayutthaya:** the claimed birthplace ([Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khanom_babin)).
*   **Ko Yao Yai, Phang Nga:** young-coconut family recipe ([Daily News](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5633802/)).
*   **Black sticky rice babin** ([Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/food/2632213)).
*   **Griddled at home:** pan-cooked rather than baked ([Wongnai](https://www.wongnai.com/recipes/thai-coconut-pancake)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Glutinous rice flour | 200 g |
| Young-mature coconut, shredded | 200 g |
| Coconut cream | 150 ml |
| Sugar | 150 g |
| Egg | 1 |
| Salt | ½ tsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Batter per cake | 2 tbsp |
| Cakes | about 16 |
| Baking | 15–20 min at 180 °C (or 5 min per side on a pan) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Batter:** Mix the flour, sugar and salt, then knead in the coconut, coconut cream and egg into a thick batter.
2. **Mould:** Oil small moulds (or line them with banana leaf) and fill them.
3. **Bake:** Bake (or griddle covered, with heat above and below) until golden and crisp-edged.
4. **Serve:** Eat warm.
