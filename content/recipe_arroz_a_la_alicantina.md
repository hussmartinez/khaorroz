# Arroz a la Alicantina / Arròs a l'Alacantina

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província d'Alacant](provincial_alicante.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented by El Español Alicante as part of the city's "Alicante Ciudad del Arroz" campaign, which lists the ingredients (and 250 g of chicken in the headline) but gives no times or quantities beyond that. The quantities below are a reconstruction. No town-by-town variants were found.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** L'Alacantí (Alicante city and its coast).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). The source stresses an oven finish so that the grain dries.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A steel paella on a gas or wood fire, finished in the oven.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Alicante's own *mar i muntanya* (sea and mountain) paella. Clams, mussels, langoustines and squid share the pan with **chicken**, peas and red pepper, all bound by a garlic, tomato and olive-oil sofrito and cooked in **fish broth, never water**. That rule separates it from the [paella mixta](recipe_paella_mixta.md) of the tourist coasts, which usually uses water or a light chicken stock. It belongs to the same city tradition as [arroz a banda](recipe_arroz_a_banda.md) and [senyoret](recipe_arroz_del_senyoret.md), which the Ajuntament promotes under "Alicante Ciudad del Arroz".

**Culinary science:** browned chicken supplies gelatin and Maillard depth, and the fish broth supplies iodine and umami (glutamate and inosinate synergy). Passing the paella through the oven at the end evaporates the surface moisture evenly, so the top grains are dry and separate without forcing a heavy socarrat.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Alicante city (El Español / Ciudad del Arroz):** Bomba rice. Clams, mussels, langoustines, squid, chicken, peas, red pepper. Fish broth. Oven finish.
*   **Other towns:** ⚠️ Not documented. Mixed meat-and-seafood rices exist along the coast under generic names.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Alicante city (reconstructed quantities).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 90 ml |
| Chicken thighs, small pieces | 250 g |
| Squid, in rings | 200 g |
| Garlic | 3 cloves |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Red pepper, strips | 1 |
| Fish broth (hot) | 1.2 L |
| Saffron | 0.2 g |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Bomba rice | 400 g |
| Langoustines | 8 |
| Mussels / clams | 300 g / 200 g |
| Peas | 100 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Preparation:** Purge the clams and steam the mussels open (add their liquor to the broth). Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
2. **Sofrito:** Brown the chicken (10 min). Sear the squid and pepper strips (5 min). Add the garlic, then the tomato (5 min).
3. **Rice:** Toast the rice for 1 min. Add the boiling broth, saffron and peas.
4. **Fire:** High heat for 8 min, then medium for 6 min. Lay the langoustines, clams and mussels on top.
5. **Oven:** 5–6 min at 200 °C to dry the grain.
6. **Rest:** 3–4 min.
