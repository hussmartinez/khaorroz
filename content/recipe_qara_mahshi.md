# Qara' Mahshi (Ari'ih, Stuffed Squash) / قرع محشي (عريّة)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** listed among Palestinian national dishes ("rice and minced meat stuffed in pumpkins"). Arabic recipe media confirm stuffed squash as one of the region's *mahashi*, but I found no Palestinian recipe with town attribution. The method is reconstructed, and the transliteration *ari'ih* follows the English list.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Palestinian home cooking, in late summer and autumn when pumpkins and squash ripen.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Stuffed vegetables (*mahashi*). A small pumpkin or squash, hollowed and filled with rice and minced meat, is baked or simmered until its flesh is spoonable and the rice tender and saturated with the squash's sweet juices.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** An oven dish (or the bakery oven) for whole squash, or a pot for squash pieces.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The English-language list of Palestinian dishes includes *ari'ih*, "rice and minced meat stuffed in pumpkins", among the dishes made nationally ([Wikipedia – List of Palestinian dishes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Palestinian_dishes)). Arabic sources treat stuffed squash as one of the vegetables of Levantine *mahshi*, common in Palestine, Syria, Jordan and Turkey ([Mawdoo3 – أكلات فلسطينية](https://mawdoo3.com/أكلات_فلسطينية)). Egyptian recipe media describe the general technique: hollow the squash, fill it with rice and mince without over-packing, seal it and bake ([Akhbar el-Yom – قرع محشي](https://akhbarelyom.com/news/newdetails/3903859/1/طبق-اليوم-طريقة-تحضير-قرع-محشي)).

**Culinary science:** the squash is a self-basting vessel. As it softens, its sugars and water flow into the rice, so the filling needs less added liquid than other *mahashi*. Leaving headroom stops the swelling rice from splitting the shell.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Whole-pumpkin version (feast):** One small pumpkin, filled and baked whole, then cut into wedges at the table.
*   **Squash-piece version (everyday):** Rings or halves of squash, stuffed and simmered in tomato sauce like [mahshi](recipe_mahshi.md).
*   ⚠️ Specific towns are undocumented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: whole stuffed pumpkin (reconstructed).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Small round pumpkin or winter squash | 1 (≈ 2 kg) |
| Butter or samneh | 30 g |
| Onion, chopped | 1 |
| Seven-spice + cinnamon | 1 tsp + ½ tsp |
| Broth or water | 150 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short-grain rice, soaked 20 min | 200 g |
| Minced lamb or beef | 250 g |
| Pine nuts, toasted | 30 g |
| Salt, pepper | to taste |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Cut a lid off the pumpkin, scoop out the seeds and salt the inside.
2. Brown the onion and meat in the butter, then add the spices, drained rice and pine nuts and stir for 2 min.
3. Fill the pumpkin three-quarters full, pour in the broth and put the lid back on.
4. Set it in an oiled dish with a cup of water, cover with foil and bake at 180 °C for **1 h 15 min–1 h 30 min**, until a knife slides through the flesh and the rice is tender.
5. Rest for 10 min. Cut it into wedges at the table so each portion has squash and rice.
