# Southern Thailand / ภาคใต้

> ⬅ [README](README.md)

## 1. Overview
Southern Thailand runs down the Kra Isthmus between two seas: the **Gulf of Thailand** coast (Chumphon, Surat Thani, Nakhon Si Thammarat, Songkhla, Pattani, Narathiwat) and the **Andaman** coast (Ranong, Phang Nga, Phuket, Krabi, Trang, Satun). Two cultures share its rice:
- **Thai-Buddhist south (Nakhon Si Thammarat, Surat Thani, Phatthalung, Songkhla).** Here rice carries the merit-making calendar: the five sweets of **Sat Duean Sip** (the tenth-month festival for the ancestors), the leaf-wrapped **khanom tom** thrown to the monks' boats at **Chak Phra**, and the honeyed **khao yakhu** (*khao mathu payat*) stirred at Nakhon's Phra Borommathat.
- **Malay-Muslim deep south (Pattani, Yala, Narathiwat, parts of Songkhla and Satun).** This was once the Sultanate of Pattani, which shared its kitchen with today's Kelantan and Terengganu. Rice here is **nasi dagae** (coconut-and-fenugreek rice with tuna curry) and **khao yam** dressed with **budu**, a fermented anchovy sauce made in the coastal Muslim villages.
- **Phuket and the Andaman.** Peranakan (Baba) Hokkien-Malay cooking, plus pickled-vegetable khao yam with *nam koei* (shrimp-paste liquor) in Phang Nga, Ranong and Chumphon.

Southern food is the hottest and most turmeric-yellow in Thailand. Rice, whether steamed jasmine or sticky rice for sweets, is the constant beside curries, *budu* and fresh herbs (*phak*).

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **Nakhon Si Thammarat** | Khao yam with turmeric curry-paste dressing; Sat Duean Sip sweets (khanom la from Pak Phanang); khao yakhu at the Phra Borommathat | Nakhon Si Thammarat, Pak Phanang, Hua Sai |
| **Surat Thani & Phatthalung** | Chak Phra festival and khanom tom in kapho-palm leaves | Surat Thani, Phatthalung |
| **Songkhla / Hat Yai** | Market khao yam, Sat Duean Sip, Thai-Chinese-Malay mix | Songkhla, Hat Yai |
| **Pattani, Yala, Narathiwat** (Malay-Muslim) | Nasi dagae, budu khao yam (nasi kerabu), noni-leaf rice | Pattani, Yala, Narathiwat |
| **Phuket & Phang Nga** (Peranakan) | Hokkien-Malay Baba cooking; khao yam with pickles and *nam koei* | Phuket Town, Phang Nga, Takua Pa |
| **Trang, Krabi, Satun** (Andaman) | Khao yam without budu (Satun) | Trang, Krabi, Satun |
| **Chumphon & Ranong** | Gateway to the south; pickled-vegetable khao yam | Chumphon, Ranong |
