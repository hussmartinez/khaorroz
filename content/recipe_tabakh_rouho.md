# Tabakh Rouho / طبخ روحه

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Syria](provincial_syria.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented as a Damascene dish by a Syrian chef's cookbook and English-language Levantine food writers; Arabic-language and town-level documentation was not found, so variants are flagged.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Damascus (Syria).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: lamb and summer vegetables (zucchini, eggplant, tomato) layered in a pot and left to "cook itself" into a soft, juicy stew, served beside plain rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A heavy lidded pot on low heat, largely unattended.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The name means "it cooks itself": the vegetables and meat are layered raw in the pot and left on a low flame to stew in their own juices, with little or no added water and almost no work. It is a Damascene summer dish built on zucchini, eggplant and tomato with lamb, finished in some versions with ghee. Recipes serve it "hot or warm next to plain white rice", and another describes it as layered and "served with steamed rice or bulgur".

The London-based Damascene chef Imad Alarnab includes it in *Imad's Syrian Kitchen: A Love Letter from Damascus to London*.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Damascus (classic):** Lamb pieces at the bottom, then layers of zucchini, eggplant, onion and tomato, salt and allspice; cooked covered on low heat.
*   **Ghee finish:** Some recipes finish with a spoon of ghee at the end.
*   **Rice or bulgur:** Served with plain rice in the city, sometimes with bulgur (⚠️ rural practice).
*   **Vegetable mix:** Recipes vary the vegetables (green pepper, potato).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Damascene tabakh rouho with plain white rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Lamb shoulder, 4 cm pieces | 600 g |
| Zucchini, thick rounds | 400 g |
| Eggplant, thick rounds | 400 g |
| Onions, sliced | 2 |
| Tomatoes, sliced | 4 |
| Green pepper (optional) | 1 |
| Garlic | 4 cloves |
| Allspice, salt, black pepper | 1 tsp each |
| Ghee (to finish) | 20 g |
| Water | 150 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice (medium-grain or basmati) | 300 g |
| Water for the rice | 450 ml |
| Butter or ghee | 25 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Base:** Brown the lamb lightly in the pot, season, then add the onions.
2. **Layer:** Layer the eggplant, zucchini, pepper, garlic and tomato over the meat, seasoning each layer; add the 150 ml water.
3. **Cook itself:** Cover tightly and cook on low heat **75–90 min** without stirring, until the lamb is tender and the vegetables have collapsed.
4. **Rice:** Rinse the rice, fry briefly in butter, add boiling salted water, cook covered 15 min, rest 10 min.
5. **Finish:** Dot the stew with ghee; serve warm beside the rice.
