# Arroz de Montaña Catalán / Arròs de Muntanya (Cerdanya, Terres de Lleida)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Catalunya](provincial_catalunya.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** The Catalan Pyrenees and Lleida: Cerdanya (Puigcerdà), Alt Urgell, Pallars, and the Lleida plain.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Meloso** (creamy). The Cerdanya recipe is cooked about 15 minutes with a picada. A caldós version also exists (see §3).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** An earthenware cassola or deep pan on a wood stove.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
High in the Pyrenees, rice was a bought luxury and the protein came from the farm and the forest. The Cerdanya tourism board's own recipe for **arròs de muntanya** uses **rabbit and pork ribs**, browned until golden, a **sofregit** of onion, garlic and tomato, **peas**, **mushrooms in season**, and a **picada** of garlic, saffron and parsley. In the Lleida lowlands, Josep Lladonosa's *La cuina de les terres de Lleida* records **arròs amb conill i bolets** (rabbit and mushroom rice) as a traditional dish. Lleida's famous **snails** (*cargols*) usually go into other dishes such as the *cutxipanda*, but households sometimes add them to rice.

**Culinary science:** a **picada** added at the end thickens the broth slightly with garlic and herb starch and brings in fresh aroma (saffron, parsley) that a long simmer would destroy. Mushrooms are browned first so they don't water down the rice.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Cerdanya (official recipe):** Rabbit + pork ribs + onion/garlic/tomato sofregit + peas + seasonal mushrooms + picada (garlic, saffron, parsley). About 15 min of cooking.
*   **Caldós version (VilaWeb):** Rabbit + ribs + chicken + sausages + artichokes + peas. Water at **3 times the volume of the rice**. Rest 5 min covered.
*   **Lleida plain:** Arròs amb conill i bolets (Lladonosa). ⚠️ Snails are a household option, not a documented standard.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Cerdanya** (official recipe structure).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 70 ml |
| Rabbit, pieces | 600 g |
| Pork ribs, short | 300 g |
| Onion, chopped | 1 |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Seasonal mushrooms (rovellons, ceps, camagrocs) | 250 g |
| Peas | 150 g |
| Water | 1.4 L (boiling) |
| **Picada:** garlic 1 clove, saffron 0.2 g, parsley | — |

**Extraction:** Brown the rabbit and ribs until golden (**15–20 min**). Sauté the mushrooms separately until dry. Make the sofregit (onion **15 min**, garlic, tomato **5 min**) and return the meat.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 320 g |
| Boiling water or stock | ≈ 1.2–1.3 L |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Stir the rice into the sofregit and meat.
2. Pour in the boiling water and add the mushrooms.
3. After 5 min, add the **peas and the picada**.
4. Boil about **15 min** in total.
5. Rest 3–5 min.
