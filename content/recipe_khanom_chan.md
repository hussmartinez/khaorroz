# Khanom Chan / ขนมชั้น

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** composition is well documented (Thairath, Wongnai). The common claim that it should have nine layers, for luck, isn't backed by a source found here, so it's left out.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand and Bangkok; nationwide at ceremonies and in markets.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** A layered steamed rice-flour sweet. Thin layers of rice-flour, tapioca and arrowroot batter with coconut milk are steamed one on top of another into a springy, peelable cake.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A square tray in a large steamer over boiling water. Each layer is poured and steamed before the next.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Khanom chan* ("layered sweet") is a traditional Thai sweet made from **rice flour**, tapioca starch and arrowroot (*thao yai mom*) flour with coconut milk, sugar and a little salt (Thairath). Old-style khanom chan has only **white and green** (pandan) layers; colours are modern. The Royal Institute's 1982 dictionary defines *khanom* as "food that is not eaten with rice, usually made of flour or rice with coconut milk or sugar". Thai sweets of this family appear in literature as far back as the *Traiphum Phra Ruang*.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Old style:** White and pandan green only.
*   **Modern:** Butterfly-pea blue, multicolour, or a rose-shaped top layer.
*   **Ceremonial:** Made for weddings and merit-making.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **traditional green and white khanom chan** (one 20 cm tray).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice flour | 60 g |
| Tapioca starch | 120 g |
| Arrowroot flour | 20 g |
| Coconut milk | 450 ml |
| Sugar | 200 g |
| Salt | ½ tsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Batter split | half plain, half with 3 tbsp pandan juice |
| Per layer | about 60 ml |
| Layers | 8–9 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Batter:** Knead the flours with a little coconut milk, then whisk in the rest with the sugar and salt until smooth. Strain.
2. **Split:** Divide in two and colour one half with pandan juice.
3. **Steam the first layer:** Oil the tray and heat it in the steamer. Pour one green layer and steam **5 minutes**, until set.
4. **Alternate:** Add white and green layers one at a time, steaming **5 minutes** each. Steam the last layer **15 minutes**.
5. **Cool:** Cool completely before cutting with an oiled knife, then peel and eat layer by layer.
