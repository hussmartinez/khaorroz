# Yakhnet Ardi Shawki / يخنة الأرضي شوكي

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Syria](provincial_syria.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented by Arabic recipe sites, including one presented as Syrian that cooks the rice alongside the stew; no historical or town-specific source was found.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Syria and Lebanon; a spring dish of the artichoke season (frozen artichoke bottoms make it year-round today).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: artichoke bottoms simmered with meat or alone, often with potato, in a lemony or tomato broth, served with rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wide stew pot; a lidded rice pot.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Artichokes (*ardi shawki*, literally "thorny earth") grow along the Syrian and Lebanese coast and in mountain gardens. The bottoms are stewed as **yakhnet ardi shawki**. One Arabic recipe presented as Syrian uses half a kilo of artichoke bottoms ("often available frozen in the markets"). It starts the rice when the stew is done "so that they finish together", with the rice water seasoned with salt, spices and stock. Another recipe adds potato cubes and fries the artichokes golden first, serving them with bulgur or rice.

Spanish readers will recognise the artichoke-and-rice pairing of the Valencian and Murcian huerta; see [arroz con alcachofas de Benicarló](recipe_arroz_con_alcachofas_benicarlo.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Syrian version:** Artichoke bottoms with meat in broth; rice cooked to finish at the same time.
*   **With potatoes (⚠️):** Potato cubes added; artichokes fried golden first.
*   **Lenten / meatless (⚠️ household practice):** Artichokes in lemon and olive oil, eaten with rice or bread.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Syrian-style yakhnet ardi shawki with meat and white rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Artichoke bottoms (fresh or frozen) | 500 g |
| Lamb or beef, small cubes | 400 g |
| Potatoes, cubed (optional) | 300 g |
| Onion, chopped | 1 |
| Garlic + coriander | 3 cloves + small bunch |
| Lemon juice | 60 ml |
| 7-spice, salt | 1 tsp each |
| Olive oil | 40 ml |
| Water or stock | 700 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice (Egyptian or medium-grain) | 300 g |
| Water or light stock for the rice | 450 ml |
| Butter | 30 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Meat:** Brown the meat with the onion, add the water and spices, simmer **50 min**.
2. **Artichokes:** Fry the artichoke bottoms (and potatoes) golden, then add them to the pot.
3. **Stew:** Simmer **20 min**; stir in the garlic, coriander and lemon at the end.
4. **Rice:** Start the rice when you add the artichokes so both finish together: fry the rinsed rice in butter, add the seasoned boiling stock, cook covered 15 min, rest 10 min.
5. **Serve:** Rice with the stew ladled alongside.
