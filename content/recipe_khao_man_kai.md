# Khao Man Kai / ข้าวมันไก่

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok (Pratunam, Silom, Yaowarat) and every Thai town with a Hainanese community; Hat Yai has its own Hainanese school.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** One-plate rice (*ahan chan diao*): **fragrant rice cooked in chicken fat and stock** with garlic and ginger, glossy and separate, under sliced poached chicken, with clear soup and a fermented-soybean sauce.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A tall stockpot for poaching the chicken, a pot or rice cooker for the fat-rice, a cleaver and board for slicing to order; formerly charcoal, now gas.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Khao man kai ("fat rice chicken") came to Bangkok with **Hainanese migrants** from China's Hainan province in the late 19th century. Like Singapore's chicken rice, it descends from the Hainanese *Wenchang* chicken tradition, but it developed separately ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainanese_chicken_rice); [Bangkok Post](https://bangkokpost.com/life/social-and-lifestyle/1883880/how-it-all-started)). The Thai signature is the sauce, **nam jim tao jiao**: fermented soybean paste with ginger, garlic, chilies, sugar and vinegar or soy, which every vendor in Thailand serves ([Hot Thai Kitchen](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/hainanese-chicken-rice)). Family shops in Bangkok, such as Kiak Kok in Prachachuen, advertise recipes handed down from Hainanese grandparents ([Matichon](https://www.matichon.co.th/lifestyle/news_2297460)). Thai academics have documented a distinct Hat Yai Hainanese variant ([TCI Thai Journal](https://so02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/HUSOTSU/article/view/267333)).

**Culinary science:** the chicken is poached gently, just below a simmer, so the muscle proteins set without tightening. Some cooks plunge it into ice water so the skin turns gelatinous. The rice is toasted in **rendered chicken fat** with garlic and ginger before the stock goes in, which coats each grain in fat and keeps it from clumping.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Pratunam (Bangkok) style:** poached chicken, sometimes with a fried-chicken option, tao jiao sauce heavy on ginger, and a bowl of winter-melon soup.
*   **Traditional Hainanese shops (Yaowarat/Sathon):** stress the "original Hainanese" recipe. The sauce uses premium *tao jiao* with salt, sugar and vinegar, Thai-adjusted ([Wongnai review](https://www.wongnai.com/reviews/a5a61888a0d24a6a80a20e472bdfb39c)).
*   **Hat Yai (*bon siao*):** the southern Hainanese school documented as local food wisdom ([TCI](https://so02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/HUSOTSU/article/view/267333)). ⚠️ Details not accessed.
*   **Fried and mixed plates:** *khao man kai thot* (fried chicken), *kai ruam* (poached and fried together).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Bangkok poached-chicken plate**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Whole chicken | 1.6 kg |
| Water | 3 L |
| Ginger (smashed) + garlic + pandan leaves | 50 g + 1 head + 3 leaves |
| Chicken fat (from cavity/skin), rendered | 60 g |
| Salt | 2 tsp |
| **Sauce:** fermented soybean paste (*tao jiao*) | 4 tbsp |
| Sauce: ginger, garlic, bird's-eye chilies, minced | 2 tbsp, 3 cloves, 4 |
| Sauce: sugar, white vinegar, dark soy | 1 tbsp, 2 tbsp, 1 tsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice | 400 g |
| Chicken stock for the rice | 560 ml (1.4 : 1) |
| Cucumber, coriander to garnish | 1 cucumber |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Poach:** bring the water to a boil with the ginger, garlic and salt. Lower in the chicken, return to a bare simmer, cover and cook 40–45 min with the heat just below simmering. Lift it out and plunge it into iced water for 10 min. Keep the stock.
2. **Fat-rice:** render the chicken fat. Fry minced garlic and ginger in it, add the washed and drained rice and toast it 3 min. Add 560 ml hot stock, the pandan and salt. Cook covered on low for 15 min, then rest 10 min.
3. **Sauce:** stir the sauce ingredients together. Adjust sweet, sour and salt.
4. **Soup:** season the remaining stock and add winter melon or daikon if you like.
5. **Serve:** mound the rice, slice the chicken on top and add cucumber and coriander. Serve the sauce and a bowl of soup alongside.
