# Khao Rad Kaeng / ข้าวราดแกง

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok (Banglamphu, Nang Loeng, Sam Yan, Ban Mo) and every Central Thai market town.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Rice with curries: **a plate of steamed jasmine rice with two or three curries and stir-fries ladled over it**, picked from trays at the shop front. The rice soaks up the curry gravies.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** Large pots and woks for the curries, a big rice steamer (*mo hung khao*), and enamel or aluminium trays (*thad*) on the counter.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Khao kaeng* or *khao rad kaeng* ("rice and curry", "curry poured over rice") is how Central Thais eat lunch. The diner chooses from a row of curries and stir-fries, and each is spooned onto the rice. The phrase *khao kaeng* is recorded in a letter of **King Chulalongkorn (Rama V)**, and over a century ago a well-known stall was run by a couple, Ta Peng and Yai Puk, at the **Ban Mo** crossroads ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khao_kaeng)). Today's institutions include **Kao Gaeng Banglamphoo 2517** ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/restaurants/kao-gaeng-banglamphoo-2517)), **Khao Gaeng Rattana** in the historic Nang Loeng market ([Hungry Onion](https://www.hungryonion.org/t/bangkok-thai-chinese-curry-rice-at-khao-gaeng-rattana/6811)), and **Ran Nai Oo** at Sam Yan, serving for more than 50 years ([Eating Thai Food](https://www.eatingthaifood.com/restaurants/sam-yan-market-rice-curry-nai-oo/)).

**Culinary science:** neutral, slightly firm jasmine rice is the carrier. Coconut-milk curries (green, red, *phanaeng*), clear sour curries (*kaeng som*), and salty-sweet stir-fries are combined on purpose so that rich, sour, salty and spicy contrast on one plate.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Banglamphu (Bangkok):** old-style curries, including Thai-Muslim *massaman* and *kaeng khiao wan*.
*   **Nang Loeng (Bangkok):** Thai-Chinese shop curries ([Hungry Onion](https://www.hungryonion.org/t/bangkok-thai-chinese-curry-rice-at-khao-gaeng-rattana/6811)).
*   **Southern-style *khao kaeng* shops in Bangkok:** fierier turmeric curries ([Bangkok Glutton](https://bangkokglutton.com/2022/09/20/curry-rice-bonanza/)).
*   **How to order:** *rad khao* gets you the plate of rice, then you point at two or three curries ([Learn Thai with Mod](https://learnthaiwithmod.com/2014/03/eat-like-a-thai-khao-rat-gaeng/)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **a home "khao kaeng" spread**: green curry with chicken plus a stir-fry.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Green curry paste | 60 g |
| Coconut milk (cream separated) | 600 ml |
| Chicken thigh, sliced | 400 g |
| Thai eggplants, quartered | 200 g |
| Fish sauce, palm sugar | 2 tbsp, 1 tbsp |
| Kaffir lime leaves, Thai basil, red chili | 4, 1 handful, 2 |
| Stir-fry: long beans + minced pork + red curry paste (*phat phrik khing*) | 250 g + 200 g + 2 tbsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice | 400 g (+ 520 ml water) |
| Curry per plate | 1 ladle (≈ 180 ml) |
| Stir-fry per plate | ≈ 100 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** steam or boil the jasmine rice and keep it warm.
2. **Crack the cream:** fry the curry paste in the thick coconut cream until fragrant and the oil separates (5 min).
3. **Curry:** add the chicken, then the thin coconut milk and eggplants. Simmer 10 min. Season with fish sauce and sugar, and finish with lime leaves and basil.
4. **Stir-fry:** fry the red paste, add the pork, then the beans, and season with fish sauce and sugar.
5. **Serve:** put a mound of rice on each plate and ladle the curry over one side and the stir-fry beside it.
