# Arroz de Boquerones y Espinacas / Arròs amb Espinacs i Aladrocs

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província d'Alacant](provincial_alicante.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** The Valencian and Alicante coasts. Alicante calls the fresh anchovy *aladroc* (Spanish *aladroque*), and Alicante Plaza lists *arròs amb espinacs i aladrocs* among the Marina's traditional rices.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry), cooked in the paella. A caldós home version also exists (see §3).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella on wood or gas.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
A thrifty **sea and huerta** rice. The two cheapest things a coastal family could buy were a handful of fresh anchovies and a bunch of winter spinach. The anchovies are opened butterfly-style, fried lightly and laid back on top at the end. The fumet can be made from the anchovy heads and bones or, more intensely, from *morralla*. Valencian home cooking has a whole spinach-rice family: a Universitat de València recipe collection records *paella d'espinacs i alls tendres* (spinach, young garlic and salt cod) and a caldós of spinach with dried beans and ray (*rajada*).

**Culinary science:** spinach collapses to about 10 % of its volume and releases water and oxalates. Sautéing it hard in the same oil used for the anchovies concentrates it and flavours the fat. Anchovy flesh is delicate and oily, so the fillets go on for only the final 2 minutes over raised heat, which steams them on top of the rice.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Comunitat Valenciana classic (Directo al Paladar):** Fresh anchovies + spinach, pepper and tomato sofrito, fumet ≈ 2 × the rice volume, 7 + 7 + 2 min.
*   **Alicante (El Español):** The "authentic" version is said to use **500 g of morralla and sea water** for the broth. ⚠️ Headline only; the article couldn't be verified.
*   **Lent / cod variant (València):** *Paella d'espinacs i alls tendres*: spinach + young garlic + **salt cod** instead of anchovy.
*   **Caldós variant (València):** Spinach + dried beans or chickpeas + **ray** (*rajada*), soupy.
*   **Murcia cousin:** see [arroz con boquerones y pava](recipe_arroz_con_boquerones_y_pava_murciano.md) (anchovies with **cauliflower**).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Comunitat Valenciana paella (scaled from a 3-person recipe).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Fresh anchovies, butterflied | 12 |
| Fresh spinach | 150 g |
| Garlic | 1–2 cloves |
| Tomato, crushed | ½ |
| Red/green pepper | ½ |
| Pimentón dulce | pinch |
| Saffron | pinch |
| Olive oil | 70 ml |
| Fumet (anchovy heads/bones or morralla) | ≈ 2 × rice volume (≈ 0.75–0.8 L) |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Bomba rice | 330 g |
| Fumet | ≈ 0.8 L |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Anchovies:** Open them, remove the spine, fry them lightly in the paella and set aside.
2. **Spinach:** Sauté it in the same oil until it collapses.
3. **Sofrito:** Garlic, tomato and pepper until jammy.
4. **Toast:** Add the rice and stir until pearly.
5. **Liquid:** Add the fumet, saffron and pimentón and level the rice. **7 min high**, then **7 min minimum heat**.
6. **Finish:** Lay the anchovies on top, raise the heat for **≈ 2 min** to dry, then rest **5 min**. Optional allioli or lemon.
