# Kaeng Pa / แกงป่า

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** Sources disagree on its home region (northern forests vs. general 'forested areas'); kept under Central Thailand because of its place in Central curry shops.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Forested hill country of Thailand; widely cooked in Central Thai curry shops.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: a fiery 'jungle curry' with no coconut milk, a thin broth full of wild herbs, green peppercorns, krachai and vegetables, with chicken, pork or fish, eaten with rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A pot or wok; stone mortar (*khrok*).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Kaeng pa* ('forest curry') is described as a curry from the forested areas of Thailand, distinguished by the absence of coconut milk ([Wikipedia – Kaeng pa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaeng_pa)). Traditional versions use whatever the forest provided; today green peppercorns, krachai (fingerroot), Thai eggplants, long beans and holy basil are typical ([ThaiFoodMaster – Into the woods](https://thaifoodmaster.com/thai_food/origin/central_plain_and_bangkok_cuisine/page/9)). It is a classic *kap khao*, eaten with plenty of rice to temper the heat.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Kaeng pa kai:** chicken, the common shop version.
*   **Kaeng pa pla duk:** catfish.
*   **Kaeng pa mu:** pork with bamboo shoots.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Red curry paste (extra chilies, krachai) | 50 g |
| Chicken or pork, sliced | 400 g |
| Water or stock | 800 ml |
| Thai eggplant, pea eggplant, long beans | 300 g |
| Krachai (fingerroot), julienned | 30 g |
| Green peppercorns on the stem | 2 sprigs |
| Fish sauce | 2 tbsp |
| Holy basil, makrut lime leaves | 1 handful, 4 |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) | 400 g, rinsed (+ 520 ml water, absorption method) |
| Curry per plate | ≈ 250 ml |
| Rice per plate | ≈ 200 g cooked |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Fry the paste in a little oil until fragrant.
2. Add the meat and stir; add water and bring to a simmer.
3. Add krachai, peppercorns and vegetables; simmer until tender.
4. Season with fish sauce; add basil and lime leaves.
5. Cook the rice: rinse 400 g jasmine rice until the water runs nearly clear, add 520 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min, then rest 10 min covered (or use an electric rice cooker / *mo hung khao*).
6. Serve with rice.
