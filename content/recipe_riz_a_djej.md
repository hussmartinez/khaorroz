# Riz a Djej / رز ع دجاج

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the dish is very well documented in recipes, but **no published source traces its history**, and regional and town variants are not documented. §3 records the household variations that recur across Lebanese family recipes.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** All of Lebanon (Beirut, Mount Lebanon, the North and South), with close relatives in Syria (*riz bi djaj*) and Palestine.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Festive rice with meat and chicken. Separate, spiced *mfalfal* grains cooked in chicken stock, topped with shredded chicken and toasted nuts.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A large pot for poaching the chicken and a heavy pot for the rice, on the stove. Served on a big platter in the middle of the table.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Riz a djej* (Lebanese colloquial *rizz ʿa djēj*, "rice on chicken") is the **celebration rice** of Lebanese households. Family memoirs and recipe writers describe it as the dish of Easter feasts, Sunday family lunches and big gatherings: food that can be made in advance and set in the middle of the table. A whole chicken is poached with aromatics to make the stock. The rice is cooked in that stock with **hashweh** (minced beef or lamb browned with warm spices), and the platter is crowned with shredded chicken and a shower of toasted **almonds, pine nuts and cashews**. It is served with yoghurt or *toum* (garlic sauce) and a salad.

The hashweh (literally "stuffing") is the same spiced meat-and-rice mixture used to stuff chickens, lamb and vegetables. Riz a djej is in effect the stuffing served as a dish in its own right. Its Spanish counterparts are the festive meat rices such as [arroz con pava](recipe_arroz_con_pava.md), the turkey cocido turned into rice.

**Culinary science:** poaching the whole chicken gives a gelatin-rich stock. The minced meat is browned hard before the rice goes in, so its Maillard flavour coats the grains. Lebanese *bharat* (allspice-led seven-spice) and cinnamon are fat-soluble and bloom in the browning fat. Nuts are toasted separately in butter or oil and added only at the end, so they stay crisp.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **With hashweh (the classic):** Minced beef or lamb, 7-spice and cinnamon, rice cooked in chicken stock. Some families **omit the meat** and use only chicken.
*   **Nut mix:** Pine nuts and almonds are standard. Some families add cashews or pistachios.
*   **Festive "smoked rice" (modern):** Contemporary chefs make *riz al djaj* with smoked rice and tahini yoghurt. A restaurant riff, not traditional.
*   **Regional variants:** ⚠️ Not documented in published sources. Questions for local cooks: the meat choice (beef vs lamb), the spice blend, and whether chickpeas are included.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Lebanese household riz a djej with hashweh**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Whole chicken | 1 (≈ 1.4 kg) |
| Water | 2.5 L |
| Onion (halved), carrot, cinnamon stick, bay leaves, cardamom pods, peppercorns | 1 / 1 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 6 |
| **Hashweh:** minced beef or lamb | 250 g |
| Butter or ghee + oil | 30 g + 2 tbsp |
| Lebanese 7-spice (*bharat*) | 1½ tsp |
| Ground cinnamon | ½ tsp |
| Salt | to taste |

**Extraction:** Simmer the chicken with the aromatics for **50–60 min**, skimming. Lift it out, shred the meat and discard the skin and bones. Strain the stock: you need **≈ 900 ml**.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Long-grain rice (or medium-grain), rinsed | 400 g |
| Chicken stock | ≈ 800 ml (≈ 1 : 2 by volume) |
| Toasted almonds + pine nuts (± cashews) | 100 g total |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Hashweh:** Brown the minced meat in the butter and oil, breaking it up, until **well browned (8–10 min)**. Add the 7-spice, cinnamon and salt.
2. **Toast:** Add the rice and stir for **2 min**.
3. **Liquid:** Add the boiling stock and check the salt. Bring back to the boil.
4. **Fire Manipulation:** Cover and cook on the **lowest heat for 18 min**. Rest **10 min** covered.
5. **Chicken:** Meanwhile, warm the shredded chicken in a ladle of stock with a pinch of 7-spice. Toast the nuts golden in a little butter.
6. **Plate:** Mound the rice on a platter, then the chicken, then the nuts. Serve with yoghurt or toum and a salad.
