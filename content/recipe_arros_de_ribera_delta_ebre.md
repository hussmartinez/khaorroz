# Arroz de Ribera del Delta del Ebro / Arròs de Ribera (Arròs a Banda del Delta, amb Ànec i Anguila)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Catalunya](provincial_catalunya.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Terres de l'Ebre, specifically the Delta de l'Ebre: Deltebre, Poblenou del Delta, Amposta, Sant Jaume d'Enveja.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Meloso** (creamy). It is served slightly brothy and firms up to a creamy texture as it rests.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella or deep pan over gas or wood.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The Ebro Delta is Catalonia's rice bowl, protected by the **D.O.P. Arròs del Delta de l'Ebre**. Its traditional dishes come from the wetland itself: **duck** from the lagoons, **eel** from the channels, **snails** from the banks, and "conill de canyar" (reed-bed rabbit). According to *Surt de Casa*, the **arròs a banda or arròs de ribera** is "a typically Delta formula with snails, eel and duck". Delta menus also list a *paella deltebrenca* with eel, langoustine, rabbit and duck. The D.O.P.'s regulatory council once ran a competition to find the most traditional recipe and concluded that "there are many, many recipes".

**Culinary science:** eel is very fatty and full of collagen. Added after the duck has browned, it gives the rice a **silky, creamy texture**. Duck browned slowly in its own fat releases flavour into the sofregit, and the snails add earthiness.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Poblenou del Delta (Ca'l Faiges, a long-standing house for arròs de ribera):** Sofregit of **green pepper, tomato and onion**. Duck browned slowly until golden, then eel, then snails. Served slightly brothy.
*   **Deltebre:** The *paella deltebrenca* combines eel + langoustine + reed-bed rabbit + duck.
*   **Amposta:** ⚠️ **Arròs amb anguiles** (eel rice) as a stand-alone signature dish, according to the Ruta de la Sega festival listing. Details unverified.
*   **Arròs a banda (Delta usage):** Here the name can also mean a dry rice cooked in fish and shellfish broth, which is a different dish from the duck rice.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Poblenou del Delta** (duck, eel, snails).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 60 ml |
| Duck, jointed | ½ (≈ 800 g) |
| Eel, cleaned, 4 cm pieces | 400 g |
| Snails, purged and blanched | 24 |
| Green pepper, chopped | 1 |
| Onion, chopped | 1 |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Water or duck stock | 1.6 L |
| Saffron / pimentón | 0.2 g / 2 g |

**Extraction:** Render and brown the duck slowly (**20 min**). Add the green pepper and onion (**10 min**), then the garlic and tomato (**5 min**). Add the water and snails and simmer **25 min**. Add the **eel** for the last **8 min**.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Delta rice (D.O.P.: bomba, bahía or similar) | 340 g |
| Stock | ≈ 1.3 L (≈ 4:1, meloso) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Bring the stock to a boil, add the rice and saffron, and stir once.
2. Cook at a strong simmer for 9 min, then medium for 8 min.
3. Serve slightly brothy. It thickens as it rests for **2–3 min**.
