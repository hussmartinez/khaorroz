# Arroz de Langosta Menorquín / Arròs de Llagosta (Fornells)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Illes Balears](provincial_baleares.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** the parent dish, the **caldereta de llagosta**, is well documented. The rice version appears on Fornells restaurant menus as *arroz meloso* and *arroz caldoso de langosta*, but I found no published canonical recipe. The master recipe below applies the caldereta's documented base to rice.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Menorca, especially **Fornells** and the north coast (Es Mercadal municipality) and Ciutadella.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Caldoso** (soupy), following the caldereta tradition. Meloso versions are also served.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A clay or iron **caldero** (pot), originally on the fishing boats.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The **caldereta de llagosta** (spiny lobster stew) began as a fishermen's dish cooked **in a caldero on their own boats**. Can Burdó in Fornells, said to be Menorca's oldest restaurant (founded 1872), began serving it to travellers. Its base is simple: **tomato, onion, garlic and olive oil**, cooked slowly, with peppers and parsley added in some versions. It is served with thin slices of bread to soak up the broth. **Arròs de llagosta** is its close relative, made on the same stock. Fornells restaurants such as Sa Llagosta and Café Balear serve it meloso or caldoso.

**Culinary science:** the Mediterranean spiny lobster (*Palinurus elephas*) gives off its flavour through its shell and head fat. Cooking the halved lobster briefly in the sofrito and then simmering it lets that fat colour the broth orange. The rice is cooked in the same broth, with the lobster returned at the end so the meat stays tender.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Fornells (documented):** Caldereta base (tomato, onion, garlic, oil). Arroz caldoso or meloso on restaurant menus.
*   **Ciutadella:** ⚠️ Some cooks use a picada of almonds and garlic. Not confirmed.
*   **Caldereta vs. arròs:** in the caldereta the bread is the starch, and in the arròs the rice is.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: reconstructed Fornells arroz caldoso.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Spiny lobster (llagosta) | 1.2–1.5 kg (1–2 lobsters) |
| Olive oil | 100 ml |
| Onion, finely chopped | 2 |
| Garlic | 4 cloves |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 400 g |
| Green pepper (optional) | 1 |
| Parsley | 1 tbsp |
| Fish fumet | 1.8 L |

**Extraction:** Halve the lobster and keep the coral and juices. Sear the pieces in the oil for **2 min** and set aside. Cook the onion and pepper slowly (**20 min**), then the garlic and tomato (**15 min**, until dark). Add the coral, the fumet and the parsley and simmer **10 min**.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Round rice | 280 g |
| Broth | ≈ 1.7 L (caldoso) |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Bring the broth to a boil and add the rice.
2. Simmer **12 min**. Return the lobster pieces.
3. Cook **4–5 min** more.
4. Serve immediately in deep plates.
