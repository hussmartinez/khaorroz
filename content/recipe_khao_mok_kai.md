# Khao Mok Kai / ข้าวหมกไก่

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** all sources agree on South Asian (biryani) ancestry. How the dish reached Thailand is contested: Indian Muslims, Malay-Muslim traders along the Phetchaburi-to-Pattani coast (16th–19th c.), or Persian Muslims in Phuket. The "khao buri" ← biryani etymology rests on a single article. Town variants are partly reconstructed.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok's Thai-Muslim communities, and the Muslim south (Pattani, Yala, Satun, Phuket). Now found nationwide.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Thai-Muslim spiced rice: **yellow, curry-spiced rice cooked with the chicken buried in it** (*mok* = to bury). The rice is fragrant and fluffy with separate grains. It comes with fried shallots, a sweet-sour green chili-mint sauce, cucumber, and often a clear oxtail or chicken soup.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A heavy covered pot (*mo*), sealed and cooked on low heat over charcoal or gas, sometimes with embers on the lid.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Khao mok ("buried rice") is Thailand's biryani. Austin Bush calls it "doubtlessly Indian in origin", with more recent roots in the predominantly **Muslim south**. Bangkok's Muslim districts (Bang Rak, Ban Khrua and the Chao Phraya riverside) made it a city staple, and Buddhist Thais now cook it too ([Austin Bush – Biryani, Bangkok style](https://www.austinbushphotography.com/blog/blog/biryani-bangkok-style.html); [Thai PBS World](https://www.thaipbsworld.com/?p=239684)). Southern cooks make it for **Ramadan** and celebrations ([Pranee's Thai Kitchen – Phuket chicken biryani](https://praneesthaikitchen.com/2010/09/08/phuket-chicken-baryani-rice-recipe-kao-mok-gai)).

**Culinary science:** whole spices (cardamom, cinnamon, clove, star anise) and turmeric-rich curry powder are bloomed in fat, so the fat-soluble aromatics coat the rice. The chicken is marinated in yogurt, whose acid tenderises it. Steaming the rice and meat together under a sealed lid lets the chicken juices season the grains. The sauce of green chili, mint, sugar and vinegar cuts through the richness.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Bangkok (Thai-Muslim shops):** chicken *khao mok kai*. The goat (*khao mok phae*) and beef (*khao mok nuea*) versions come with oxtail soup.
*   **Phuket / the South:** strongly spiced, sometimes with raisins and fried shallots ([Pranee's Thai Kitchen](https://praneesthaikitchen.com/2010/09/08/phuket-chicken-baryani-rice-recipe-kao-mok-gai)).
*   **Ayutthaya:** ⚠️ no specific source found, despite the old capital's historic Muslim quarter.
*   **Diaspora:** Thai-Muslim cooks abroad, e.g. Kruang Tedd in Los Angeles ([LA Weekly](https://www.laweekly.com/kruang-tedd-restaurants-chef-cant-eat-her-own-muslim-thai-chicken-and-rice-but-you-can/)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Bangkok Thai-Muslim chicken khao mok**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chicken legs, halved | 4 (≈ 1.2 kg) |
| Plain yogurt | 120 g |
| Thai curry powder + turmeric | 2 tbsp + 1 tsp |
| Garlic, ginger, shallot, pounded | 2 tbsp paste |
| Whole spices: cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, bay | 4, 1 stick, 4, 2 |
| Ghee or oil | 60 ml |
| Fried shallots | 60 g |
| Sauce: green chilies, mint, sugar, vinegar, salt | 4, 1 handful, 3 tbsp, 4 tbsp, ½ tsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine (or basmati) rice, washed | 400 g |
| Chicken stock | 600 ml (1.5 : 1) |
| Raisins (optional, southern) | 40 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Marinate** the chicken with yogurt, curry powder, turmeric, the garlic-ginger paste and salt for at least 2 h.
2. **Brown:** heat the ghee, fry the whole spices, then brown the chicken on all sides.
3. **Rice:** add the drained rice and fry 2 min until coated and yellow. Pour in the hot stock.
4. **Bury and seal:** nestle the chicken into the rice. Cover tightly and cook on low for 25 min, then rest 10 min off the heat.
5. **Sauce:** blend the chilies, mint, sugar, vinegar and salt to a bright green, sweet-sour sauce.
6. **Serve:** put rice and chicken on each plate, scatter fried shallots, and add cucumber, the green sauce and a clear soup.
