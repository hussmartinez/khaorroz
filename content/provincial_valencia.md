# Província de València / Provincia de Valencia

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Alacant](provincial_alicante.md) · [Castelló](provincial_castellon.md)

## 1. Overview
The province of Valencia is the historic heart of Valencian rice. The **Albufera** wetland and the lowlands of **La Ribera** hold the bulk of the **D.O. Arròs de València** paddies (varieties Senia, Bahía, Albufera, Bomba, J. Sendra). Rice farming here goes back to the Andalusi period, when the irrigation networks (*séquies*) still governed by the **Tribunal de les Aigües** (UNESCO Intangible Heritage) were laid down. Valencian rice cooking is organised around **wood fire**, the **wide shallow paella** and the produce of the *horta*: *bajoqueta*, *garrofó*, tomato, artichoke, and in the past rabbit, chicken, duck and snails.

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region (comarca) | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **L'Horta de València** (Nord, Sud, Oest) | Paella valenciana, arròs amb bledes, fesols i naps | València, Meliana, Alboraia, Torrent, Benissanó (Camp de Túria edge) |
| **L'Albufera / La Ribera Baixa** | Paella with duck, all i pebre country, rice-field paellas | Sueca, El Palmar, Cullera, Silla, Catarroja |
| **La Ribera Alta** | Long-browned paellas, winter caldosos | Alzira, Algemesí, Carcaixent |
| **La Costera / La Vall d'Albaida** | **Arròs al forn** heartland | Xàtiva, Ontinyent |
| **La Safor** | Seafood rices, caldosos, arròs negre | Gandia, Oliva, Xeraco |
| **Valencia city coast** | Arròs negre, senyoret, a banda | El Cabanyal, El Grau, La Malva-rosa |
| **Interior (Utiel-Requena, Serranos, Camp de Túria)** | Sarmiento-fired rabbit-snail rices, heavier matança caldosos | Requena, Utiel, Llíria, Chelva |
