# Arroz al Horno con Pasas y Garbanzos / Arròs al Forn amb Panses i Cigrons (Arròs Viudo)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Province of València. Recorded in Valencian home cookbooks, with a vigil variant in Tavernes de la Valldigna (La Safor).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). Baked dry in about 20 minutes.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A flat earthenware *cassola*, soaked beforehand, in an oven at 220–250 °C.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The **Lenten twin** of [arròs al forn](recipe_arroz_al_horno.md). It is an *arròs viudo* ("widowed rice") "que no se acompaña de ninguna carne", in which **raisins** and **chickpeas** replace the pork, sausages and morcilla. Valencia Bonita calls it "un plato típico de Cuaresma", little known in many comarques. The recipe it publishes comes from the aunt of Salvador Gayá Sastre. A Universitat de València recipe collection also records *arròs al forn amb cigrons i panses*, cooking the rice in the chickpea liquid and frying the raisins in the oil first.

In **Tavernes de la Valldigna** the same base becomes the **arròs de vigília**, with **salt cod and cod meatballs** (*mandonguilles de bacallà*) added.

**Culinary science:** frying soaked raisins briefly caramelises their sugars, giving a sweet-savoury depth that stands in for meat. Half the raisins go in at the start and half on top, so some melt into the broth and some stay plump. Chickpea cooking water carries starch and savour into the rice.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Valencia Bonita family recipe:** Crushed garlic fried and removed. Half the raisins + 125 g grated tomato + chickpeas + 1 tbsp *pimentón de hojilla*, then rice, the fried garlic, and broth (total ≈ 2× the rice volume). The rest of the raisins on top. 220–250 °C, about 20 min.
*   **Universitat de València collection:** Chickpeas boiled with saffron in 1 L of water. Raisins fried in oil, then the rice. Baked with about 700–750 g of chickpea liquid.
*   **Tavernes de la Valldigna (arròs de vigília):** The same, plus **bacallà** and **mandonguilles de bacallà**. ⚠️ The full local recipe isn't published online.
*   **Ontinyent echo:** In Ontinyent arròs al forn is the protagonist of the Puríssima festivities and is called *cassola*. ⚠️ No raisin attribution found there.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: the Valencia Bonita family recipe, with the Universitat de València liquid ratio.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 5 tbsp (75 ml) |
| Garlic, crushed | 4 cloves |
| Raisins, soaked 1–2 h | 100 g (half now, half on top) |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 125 g |
| Cooked chickpeas (+ cooking liquid) | 250 g |
| Pimentón de hojilla | 1 tbsp |
| Saffron | pinch |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 400 g |
| Chickpea broth (hot) | ≈ 750–800 ml (2 : 1 by volume) |
| Oven | 220–250 °C |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Garlic:** Fry the crushed garlic in the oil and remove it.
2. **Raisins and tomato:** Add half the raisins, then the tomato, and cook down.
3. **Chickpeas and paprika:** Add the chickpeas, then the pimentón, then **one glass of broth at once** so it doesn't burn. Boil briefly.
4. **Cassola:** Transfer to the soaked cassola. Add the rice, the fried garlic and the rest of the hot broth. Stir and scatter the remaining raisins on top.
5. **Oven:** **≈ 20 min** at 220–250 °C, until dry.
6. **Rest:** 5 min. [**Valldigna vigília:** nest 250 g of desalted cod pieces and 8 cod meatballs into the rice before baking.]
