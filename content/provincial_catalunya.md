# Cataluña / Catalunya

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de Castelló](provincial_castellon.md) · [Illes Balears](provincial_baleares.md) · [Región de Murcia](provincial_murcia.md)

## 1. Overview
Catalan rice cooking is built on the **cassola** (a deep earthenware pot) rather than the flat paella. It uses a slow, dark onion **sofregit**, a **picada** (pounded garlic, parsley, nuts or bread, saffron) stirred in at the end, and the **mar i muntanya** pairing of meat and seafood in the same pot. The usual texture is **melós**. Rice is grown in two places. The **Delta de l'Ebre** is protected by the D.O.P. Arròs del Delta de l'Ebre and is the largest rice area in Catalonia. The **Baix Empordà** grows **Arròs de Pals** under the Productes de l'Empordà guarantee mark. Its cultivation dates to at least 1452, was abandoned in 1838 over malaria, and was revived around 1900.

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **Barcelona & central coast** | Arròs a la cassola (mar i muntanya), arròs negre, parellada | Barcelona, Maresme, Garraf |
| **Empordà / Costa Brava** | Arròs de Pals a la cassola, arròs negre de l'Empordà, cuina de barca | Pals, Palamós, L'Escala, Roses |
| **Terres de l'Ebre / Delta** | Arròs de ribera (duck, eel, snails), arrossejat, galeres | Deltebre, Amposta, Poblenou del Delta, L'Ametlla de Mar |
| **Pyrenees & Lleida** | Arròs de muntanya (rabbit, ribs, mushrooms, picada) | Puigcerdà, La Seu d'Urgell, Lleida |

**Not yet documented:** *Arròs amb espardenyes* (sea cucumber rice, a Costa Brava luxury) was requested, but I found no source for it in this research, so it has no file yet. Arròs de Pals is often called an IGP, but no source in this research confirms that status.
