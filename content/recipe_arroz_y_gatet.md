# Arroz y Gatet (Arroz con Pintarroja) / Arròs amb Gatet

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província d'Alacant](provincial_alicante.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented in the *Revista Española de Nutrición Comunitaria*'s 2022 survey of **forgotten dishes** of the Valencian Community (recipe credited to David Ariza Abad, Freelandcook). **Note an error in that source:** it gives the scientific name as *Hemiscyllium ocellatum*, which is an Australian epaulette shark. The Mediterranean *gatet* / *pintarroja* is the small-spotted catshark, ***Scyliorhinus canicula***. Town-level variations beyond Santa Pola are not documented.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Santa Pola (El Baix Vinalopó coast).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Caldoso** (soupy). The source specifies double the broth of a paella.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A deep pot (*olla*) on the hob. A paella can be used if you work with that much broth.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The *gatet* (small catshark, Spanish *pintarroja*) was a low-value bycatch of the Santa Pola trawlers. Like the morralla of the [caldero](recipe_caldero_santa_pola_tabarca.md), it fed fishing families rather than going to market. Its firm, boneless, cartilaginous flesh holds together in a long-cooked broth, which suits a soupy rice. Its skin is rough and its flesh can smell of ammonia, because sharks retain urea in their tissues. Santa Pola cooks therefore **pre-cure it** in salt and brown sugar before cooking. This is the dish's most distinctive technique.

The rice is a "sea and huerta" combination. Garden vegetables (cauliflower, peas, broad beans, artichoke, green and red pepper, *ajos tiernos*) cook in fish broth with the local **salmorreta**. The source notes that in Santa Pola the fishermen's guild (*Cofradía de Pescadores*) fish shop sells ready-made salmorreta.

**Culinary science:** a 15-minute cure in 4:1 salt and sugar draws out surface moisture and urea-derived amines and firms the flesh, much like a quick gravlax. Rinsing afterwards removes the excess salt. The vegetables go in **after** the broth has boiled with the salmorreta, so they keep some bite.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **The Santa Pola Style (RENC source):** Salt-and-sugar cure. Salmorreta made from ñora, garlic, parsley and tomato. Mixed huerta vegetables. **Musolas** (small hound-shark, *Mustelus*) pieces alongside the gatet. Served with allioli.
*   **Other coastal towns:** ⚠️ Undocumented. Catshark rices appear informally along the Alicante coast, but no other town-specific recipe was found.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Santa Pola (RENC 2022)**. The source lists only 160 g of rice for 4, which is a light, very soupy portion; scale to 280 g for a main course.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Gatet (catshark) + 3–4 musolas, skinned, in pieces | ≈ 800 g |
| Cure: coarse salt + brown sugar | 1 kg + 250 g |
| Fish stock | 2 L (+ 1.25 L water) |
| Olive oil | 80 ml |
| **Salmorreta:** ñoras (seeded, fried), 4 garlic cloves, parsley, salt, 3 tbsp crushed tomato | 2 ñoras |
| Onion | 1 |
| Tomatoes, grated (or 5 tbsp crushed) | 2 |
| Saffron or colourant | pinch |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 160 g (source) – 280 g (main course) |
| Broth : rice | **≈ 6 : 1** ("double a paella") |
| Ajos tiernos | 1 bunch |
| Green Italian pepper / red pepper | 1 / ½ |
| Broad beans (pods) / artichokes | 4 / 2 |
| Cauliflower / peas | 100 g / 150 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Cure:** Bury the fish pieces in the salt-sugar mix for **15 min**. Rinse well and cut them into chunks.
2. **Salmorreta:** Fry the ñoras until golden and pound them with salt, parsley and garlic. Fry this paste with 3 tbsp of crushed tomato.
3. **Base:** In the pot, fry the grated tomato, onion and saffron in the oil. Add three-quarters of the fish stock and boil hard for **10 min**.
4. **Build:** Add the salmorreta and the rest of the stock. Boil **10 min**, then cut the heat. Add the ajos tiernos, artichokes, cauliflower, pepper and peas, then the remaining tomato, the fish and the rice.
5. **Cook:** Simmer in plenty of broth for about **20 min**.
6. **Finish:** Serve straight away, soupy, with allioli.
