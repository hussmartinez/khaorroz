# Arroz con Acelgas / Arròs amb Bledes (Arròs en Banderetes)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** L'Horta Nord (Meliana, Alboraia), L'Horta Sud, La Ribera. It is made across the whole Comunitat, including inland Alicante.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Meloso** (creamy). Thick and spoonable, with little free broth, between a soup and a risotto.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A clay **cassola** or a deep *paella honda*. In Meliana it was cooked over wood for generations.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Arròs amb bledes is the definitive **Lenten** (*Quaresma*) rice. It contains no meat and is built on chard, white beans and potato, all huerta products available through late winter. The nickname *arròs en banderetes* ("little flags") refers to the chard leaves poking out of the rice. In Lent households it is often enriched with **salt cod** (*bacallà*). Outside Lent it is often made with **snails** (*arròs amb bledes i caragols*), and in some villages with a little pork.

**Culinary science:** chard releases a lot of water and some oxalic acid. Frying it hard with garlic and pimentón in the sofrito first drives off that water, concentrates its mineral sweetness and prevents a "boiled greens" flavour. Potato and pre-cooked beans both add starch, which together with gentle stirring gives the meloso body without butter or cheese.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)

*   **The Meliana / Horta Nord Style:** Wood-fired. Chard + white beans + potato, and **caracoles** (*vaquetes*) are common outside Lent. Pimentón and saffron.
*   **The Lenten Valencia City Style:** **Desalted salt cod** crumbled into the sofrito. No snails. Often some **turnip** or **cauliflower**.
*   **The La Ribera Style:** Some families use **red beans** (*fesols roig*) or *garrofó*, and add **cardoon**.
*   **The Alicante interior / Vega Baja echo:** *Arroz con habichuelas y acelgas*, often with **ñora** in the base and cooked slightly more caldoso.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **L'Horta (Lenten)**, with optional cod or snails.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 70 ml |
| Chard (*bledes*), leaves + stalks, chopped | 600 g |
| Garlic | 3 cloves, sliced |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 100 g |
| Pimentón dulce | 4 g |
| Potato, in 2 cm chunks | 250 g |
| Cooked white beans (*fesols*) + their liquid | 300 g |
| Water or bean cooking liquid | 1.3 L |
| Saffron | 0.2 g |
| [Optional Lent] Salt cod, desalted 36 h, crumbled | 200 g |
| [Optional] Snails, blanched | 24 |

**Extraction:** Fry the chard **stalks** first (3 min), then the leaves, with the garlic, until wilted and the water has evaporated (**8 min**). Add the pimentón (5 s), then the tomato (4 min). Add the potato and the water, the beans and their liquid, and the saffron (and the snails if using). Simmer for **15 min** until the potato is half-cooked.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short-grain rice | 320 g |
| Broth | ≈ 1.2 L (≈ 3.75:1, meloso) |
| Chard | 600 g raw |
| Beans (cooked) | 300 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Preparation:** Cook the beans in advance (or use good jarred ones).
2. **Fondo:** As above. Add the cod at the end of the sofrito if you are using it.
3. **Rice Addition:** Bring to a boil and add the rice.
4. **Fire Manipulation:** Medium-strong simmer for **16–18 min**, stirring gently every 3–4 min.
5. **Finish/Resting Phase:** Rest **2 min** covered. It should be loose and creamy, not dry. Serve in deep plates.
