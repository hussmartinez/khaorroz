# Arroz con Perdiz / Arroz Caldoso de Perdiz (Sierras de Jaén y Córdoba)

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Andalucía](provincial_andalucia.md) · [Castilla-La Mancha & Extremadura](provincial_castilla_la_mancha_extremadura.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** partridge is the emblematic game of Sierra Morena (La Carolina in Jaén has restaurants that specialise in it), but I found no Jaén-specific canonical rice recipe. The variations below combine published general versions with reconstructed local practice.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Sierra Morena (La Carolina, Andújar), Sierra de Cazorla, Los Pedroches (Córdoba), and the Montes de Toledo and Ciudad Real on the Castilian side.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Caldoso** (soupy). Oven-baked dry versions also exist.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A clay *cazuela* over a holm-oak (*encina*) fire in the cortijo.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The red-legged partridge (*perdiz roja*) is the defining small game of the dehesa and the olive-grove sierras, and partridge hunting is a centuries-old social ritual in Jaén. In La Carolina, restaurants specialise in partridge as pâté, pickled (*escabeche*), stewed or with onions. A caldoso rice is the household way to stretch one or two birds across a family. A long braise with wine and bay leaf tenderises the lean, sinewy meat, and its broth then cooks the rice.

**Culinary science:** partridge is very lean and high in myoglobin, so it toughens if seared hard and then cooked quickly. It needs a **long, gentle braise** (60–90 min for wild birds). Wine acids and bay help break down connective tissue. Some versions use **amontillado** sherry, whose nutty oxidative notes match game.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial — partly reconstructed)
*   **La Carolina / Sierra Morena (Jaén):** Partridge braised with **onion, garlic, bay and white wine**. Caldoso. Often finished with a little vinegar (a nod to the *escabeche* tradition).
*   **Los Pedroches (Córdoba):** Partridge + **wild mushrooms** in autumn.
*   **Montilla-Moriles orbit (Córdoba):** **Amontillado** or **fino** in the broth.
*   **Montes de Toledo (Castilla-La Mancha):** Partridge + **red wine** + thyme. Sometimes dry, baked in the oven.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Sierra Morena caldoso** (white wine, bay leaf).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Partridges, cleaned, quartered | 2 (≈ 800 g) |
| Olive oil | 80 ml |
| Onion | 1 large |
| Green pepper | 1 |
| Garlic | 1 head (halved) + 2 cloves minced |
| Bay leaves | 2 |
| Thyme | 1 sprig |
| Dry white wine (or amontillado) | 200 ml |
| Water or chicken stock | 2 L |
| Pimentón dulce | 3 g |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |

**Extraction:** Brown the partridge pieces gently (**10 min**). Add the onion, pepper and garlic (10 min), then the wine and reduce by half. Add the water, bay and thyme. Simmer covered for **60–90 min** until tender. Lift out the meat and strip it from the bone if you prefer. Strain the broth; you should have ≈ 1.8 L. In the cazuela, fry the minced garlic, pimentón and tomato (5 min).

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Round rice | 300 g |
| Partridge broth | 1.6–1.8 L (≈ 5.5:1) |
| Partridge meat | ≈ 500 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Toasting:** Add the rice to the tomato base and stir for 1 min.
2. **Liquid:** Pour in the boiling broth and return the partridge.
3. **Fire:** Steady simmer for **17 min**.
4. **Finish:** Rest 2 min. Optionally add a few drops of sherry vinegar. Serve in deep plates.
