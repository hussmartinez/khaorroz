# Arroz con Bacalao de Vigilia (La Mancha y Extremadura) / Arroz de Cuaresma

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Castilla-La Mancha & Extremadura](provincial_castilla_la_mancha_extremadura.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** I found **no documented regional recipe** for a Manchegan or Extremaduran arroz con bacalao. The Lenten classics of these regions are **potaje de vigilia** (chickpeas, cod, spinach), **atascaburras** and **bacalao al ajoarriero**, and Extremadura's *potaje de vigilia* uses **noodles**, not rice. This file documents the rice version that inland households make in the same Lenten logic, and it is a **reconstruction**.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Inland Castilla-La Mancha (Cuenca, Albacete, Ciudad Real, Toledo) and Extremadura.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Caldoso** (soupy).
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A clay *olla* or *cazuela* on the stove.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Salt cod was the protein of Lent across inland Spain, far from fresh fish. It arrived by mule and rail, preserved in salt. Manchegan Lenten cooking produced the *potaje de vigilia* with chickpeas, cod and spinach, *castañuelas de bacalao* in Cuenca (a forgotten Semana Santa dish documented by the RENC), and *atascaburras* (cod, potato, walnuts, egg). Swapping chickpeas or noodles for **rice** gives the arroz con bacalao de vigilia.

**Culinary science:** desalting (36–48 h with water changes) rehydrates the cod. Gentle poaching keeps its flakes intact. Fried garlic and pimentón are the aromatic backbone, and spinach adds minerality.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial — reconstructed)
*   **Cuenca:** Cod + **chickpeas** + rice (a hybrid with the potaje).
*   **Ciudad Real / Albacete:** Cod + **spinach** + hard-boiled egg on top.
*   **Extremadura:** Cod + potato + **pimentón de la Vera**.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Manchegan Lenten caldoso** (reconstructed).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Salt cod, desalted, in pieces | 300 g |
| Olive oil | 70 ml |
| Garlic | 4 cloves |
| Onion | 1 |
| Ripe tomato, grated | 150 g |
| Pimentón (de la Vera in Extremadura) | 4 g |
| Spinach | 300 g |
| Cooked chickpeas (optional) | 200 g |
| Water or cod-poaching water | 1.8 L |
| Hard-boiled eggs | 2 |

**Extraction:** Poach the cod in the water for 3 min, then remove the cod and keep the water. Fry the garlic and onion (10 min), the pimentón (5 s) and the tomato (5 min).

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Round rice | 300 g |
| Liquid | ≈ 1.7 L |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** Add the rice to the sofrito, then the poaching water (and the chickpeas if using).
2. **Fire:** Simmer **12 min**. Add the spinach and cook 3 min, then the cod for the last **2 min**.
3. **Finish:** Garnish with chopped egg and serve immediately.
