# Mehshi Felfel / محشي فليفلة

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** stuffed peppers are documented across Lebanese recipe media, but no source localises them to particular towns; the variants below are those the sources describe (meat vs Lenten lentil filling, spice blends).

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Lebanon, especially as a summer dish when sweet peppers are cheap; part of the wider Levantine and Egyptian *mahshi* family.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Stuffed vegetables (*mahashi*): bell peppers filled with raw rice, minced meat and spices, simmered upright in tomato sauce until the rice inside is soft and the sauce has thickened.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A deep pot that holds the peppers snugly upright, on the stove.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Mehshi felfel* (Lebanese *flayfleh mehshiyeh*) belongs to the summer *mahashi* repertoire alongside zucchini and eggplant: the filling is raw rice mixed with minced meat and Lebanese seven spices or cinnamon and allspice, and the peppers are covered with tomato sauce and simmered about 45 minutes ([A Pinch of Adventure – Lebanese stuffed bell peppers](https://apinchofadventure.com/lebanese-stuffed-bell-peppers); [The Lemon Bowl – Stuffed bell peppers with Lebanese spices](https://thelemonbowl.com/stuffed-bell-peppers-with-lebanese-spices/)). The same dish is called *mahshi felfel* in Egypt ([Wikipedia – Stuffed peppers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuffed_peppers)). For Lent and for vegetarian households, a meatless version swaps the meat for lentils and more rice ([Plant Based Folk – Lebanese stuffed capsicum](https://plantbasedfolk.com/vegan-stuffed-peppers-lebanese-stuffed-capsicum/)).

As in all rice-filled *mahashi*, the rice swells inside the vegetable, absorbing the pepper's juices and the tomato sauce; leaving room in each pepper keeps them from bursting. Compare the Palestinian [mahshi](recipe_mahshi.md) and the Aleppine [mahshi halabi](recipe_mahshi_halabi.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Meat version (most common):** beef or lamb, seven spices or cinnamon and allspice, tomato sauce ([The Lemon Bowl](https://thelemonbowl.com/stuffed-bell-peppers-with-lebanese-spices/)).
*   **Lenten / vegan version (flayfleh mahshiyeh):** lentils instead of meat, olive oil, tomato ([Plant Based Folk](https://plantbasedfolk.com/vegan-stuffed-peppers-lebanese-stuffed-capsicum/)).
*   **With lemon and mint:** some cooks finish the sauce with lemon juice and dried mint, as for zucchini ([Recipes are Simple – Mahshi in tomato sauce](https://recipesaresimple.com/mahshi-stuffed-vegetables-tomato-sauce-middle-eastern)).
*   **Egypt (mahshi felfel):** same name, often with dill and a looser rice filling ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuffed_peppers)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Lebanese meat version.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Medium bell peppers, tops cut off and seeded | 8 |
| Minced beef or lamb | 300 g |
| Tomatoes, finely chopped (for the filling) | 2 |
| Lebanese seven spices (or allspice + cinnamon) | 1½ tsp |
| Tomato paste | 3 tbsp |
| Water or stock | 700 ml |
| Garlic, crushed + dried mint | 2 cloves + 1 tsp |
| Olive oil, salt, pepper | 30 ml / to taste |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short- or medium-grain rice (raw, rinsed) | 200 g |
| Rice : meat in the filling | 2 : 3 by weight |
| Fill each pepper | ¾ full |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Filling:** mix the raw rice, meat, chopped tomato, spices, salt, pepper and oil.
2. **Stuff:** fill the peppers three-quarters full (the rice swells) and replace the tops.
3. **Sauce:** stand the peppers upright in the pot; dissolve the tomato paste in the water, season and pour over until nearly covered.
4. **Simmer:** bring to a boil, cover and simmer gently about 45 minutes until the rice inside is tender.
5. **Finish:** stir the garlic and mint into the sauce for the last 5 minutes; rest 10 minutes and serve with the sauce.
