# Arroz y Pollo con Alcaciles / Arroz Huertano de Pollo y Alcachofas

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Región de Murcia](provincial_murcia.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** La Huerta de Murcia.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). This is my classification: the chicken is covered with water and boiled, the rice cooks 15–20 min, then rests.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A paella or wide pan on the stove.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Alcacil* (from Andalusi Arabic *al-qabsil*) is the Murcian and Andalusian word for **artichoke**, and the huerta grows some of Spain's best. This everyday rice pairs half a farmyard chicken with artichokes, red and green peppers and tomato. It is recorded by the Federación de Peñas Huertanas in the Ayuntamiento de Murcia's cookbook *Un paseo por la huerta*. The artichokes are dropped into **water with chopped parsley** so they don't darken. This Murcian alternative to the Valencian lemon-water trick is recorded in the cookbook.

**Culinary science:** artichoke polyphenols oxidise when cut. Acidulated or herb water slows the reaction, and frying the pieces separately before they meet the rice sets their colour and caramelises their edges.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Huerta de Murcia (Peñas Huertanas):** Chicken + alcaciles + red and green pepper + parsley + tomato + saffron.
*   **Vega Baja cousin:** [Arroz de verduras de la Vega Baja](recipe_arroz_de_verduras_vega_baja.md) (artichoke-led, often with habas).
*   **Castellón cousin:** [Arroz con alcachofas de Benicarló](recipe_arroz_con_alcachofas_benicarlo.md).
*   ⚠️ Other Murcian towns are not documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Peñas Huertanas (cookbook uses 1 kg rice; scaled to 400 g).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Chicken, in pieces | ¼–½ (≈ 600 g) |
| Alcaciles, quartered, in parsley water | 3 |
| Red pepper / green pepper, strips | ½ / ½ |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Ripe tomatoes, grated | 200 g |
| Parsley | small bunch |
| Saffron, olive oil, salt | to taste |
| Water | to cover (≈ 1.2 L) |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice | 400 g |
| Water at rice-in | ≈ 1.1 L |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Prep:** Cut the peppers into strips. Quarter the alcaciles into water with chopped parsley.
2. **Fry:** Fry the peppers, then the chicken. Sauté the alcaciles and remove them.
3. **Sofrito:** Garlic, then tomato. Return the chicken.
4. **Boil:** Cover with water, season, add saffron and boil **10–12 min**.
5. **Rice:** Add the rice, alcaciles and peppers. Cook **15–20 min** depending on the rice variety.
6. **Rest:** A few minutes, then serve.
