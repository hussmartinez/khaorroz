# Khanom Sot Sai / ขนมสอดไส้

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** recipes are documented (Wongnai user recipe, Pholfoodmafia for the related *khanom ko*), but I found no dated history.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand, sold at markets nationwide.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Glutinous rice-flour sweet: a soft, chewy dumpling around a coconut and palm-sugar filling, sitting in salty coconut cream, wrapped in a pyramid of banana leaf and steamed.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** Banana-leaf cones pinned with bamboo, steamed over a charcoal stove.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Sot sai* means "with something tucked inside". The dough is glutinous rice flour worked with coconut cream and pandan juice; the filling is mature-young coconut cooked with palm sugar. The finished sweet is "chewy and soft, with a sweet filling, rich and slightly salty from the coconut cream" ([Wongnai](https://www.wongnai.com/recipes/ugc/3bc902ce7be047a4a67817f6388ed662)). It belongs to the family of glutinous-flour and coconut sweets that includes *khanom ko kathi*, dumplings in sweet coconut milk ([Phol Food Mafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=22571)), and *khanom daek nga*, rolled in black sesame ([Wongnai](https://www.wongnai.com/recipes/ugc/e2bf9b55f4d5473a8fe7d3970ea492f9)).

**Culinary science:** glutinous rice flour's amylopectin turns elastic and translucent when steamed. The salty coconut cream sets around it and balances the sweet filling.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Classic market version:** banana-leaf pyramid with white coconut filling ([Wongnai](https://www.wongnai.com/recipes/ugc/3bc902ce7be047a4a67817f6388ed662)).
*   **Pandan-green dough:** dough coloured and scented with pandan.
*   **Related: khanom ko kathi** (dumplings in sweet coconut milk) ([Phol Food Mafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/?p=22571)) and **khanom daek nga** (sesame-rolled) ([Wongnai](https://www.wongnai.com/recipes/ugc/e2bf9b55f4d5473a8fe7d3970ea492f9)).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Glutinous rice flour | 300 g |
| Coconut cream (dough) | 200 ml |
| Pandan juice | 50 ml |
| Grated coconut (filling) | 200 g |
| Palm sugar (filling) | 150 g |
| Coconut cream + salt (top) | 300 ml + 1 tsp |
| Banana leaves | about 20 pieces |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Dough per sweet | 30 g |
| Filling per sweet | 1 tsp |
| Sweets | about 20 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Filling:** Cook the coconut with the palm sugar until sticky; roll into small balls.
2. **Dough:** Knead the flour with coconut cream and pandan juice until soft; wrap each filling ball.
3. **Wrap:** Fold banana leaf into cones, add 1 tbsp of salted coconut cream, set the dumpling on it, add another spoonful and close into a pyramid.
4. **Steam:** Steam 15–20 min until the dough is translucent. Serve warm or at room temperature.
