# Kaeng Khua Sapparot Hoi Malaeng Phu / แกงคั่วสับปะรดหอยแมลงภู่

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** No dated history found; documentation is recipe-level.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Central Thailand and the upper Gulf coast (mussel farming).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: a red coconut curry, sweet and sour with fresh pineapple, with green mussels, eaten with jasmine rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wok or pot; stone mortar (*khrok*).

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Kaeng khua* is a family of red coconut curries; paired with pineapple and shellfish it becomes a sweet-sour curry typical of the Central coast. A Thai recipe describes frying red curry paste, seasoning with fish sauce and palm sugar, simmering pineapple until it releases its juice and adding mussels and makrut lime leaf ([Phol Food Mafia](https://www.pholfoodmafia.com/recipe/mussel-and-pineapple-spicy-curry-%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B1%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A2%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%A0%E0%B8%B9%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%AA/)). Thai PBS's *Mho Khao Mho Gang* showed a community version with river snails (*hoi than*) at Khlong Lao Ngoen ([Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/program/MhoKhaoMhoGang/episodes/89485)), and pickled mussels are a known side dish of the Gulf coast ([Matichon](https://www.matichon.co.th/lifestyle/food-travel/news_1010827)).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **With green mussels:** coastal Central style.
*   **With river snails (*hoi than*):** canal communities, as shown on Thai PBS.
*   **With prawns or crab:** restaurant variants (⚠️ general).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Red curry paste | 50 g |
| Coconut milk | 600 ml |
| Green mussel meat | 300 g |
| Fresh pineapple, in small chunks | 200 g |
| Fish sauce, palm sugar | 2 tbsp, 1 tbsp |
| Makrut lime leaves, red chili | 4, 1 |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice (*khao hom mali*) | 400 g, rinsed (+ 520 ml water, absorption method) |
| Curry per plate | ≈ 200 ml |
| Rice per plate | ≈ 180 g cooked |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. Fry the paste in a little oil and the coconut cream.
2. Season with fish sauce and palm sugar; add pineapple and simmer until it softens.
3. Add the rest of the coconut milk, then the mussels and lime leaves; cook 2 min.
4. Cook the rice: rinse 400 g jasmine rice until the water runs nearly clear, add 520 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 15 min, then rest 10 min covered (or use an electric rice cooker / *mo hung khao*).
5. Serve with rice.
