# Khao Man Som Tam / ข้าวมันส้มตำ

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Central Thailand](provincial_central_thailand.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Bangkok and the Central Plains (central-Thai som tam shops).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Rice set: coconut-cream rice (*khao man*) with sweet-sour green-papaya salad (*som tam Thai*), a red chicken curry and sweet shredded beef — eaten together, a spoonful of each.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A pot or steamer for the coconut rice (*mo hung khao*); a clay mortar (*khrok din*) and wooden pestle for the som tam.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Silpa Watthanatham describes the central-Thai *samrap* (set) of *khao man*, *som tam*, red chicken curry and sweet fried shredded beef, sometimes with a tamarind relish, and notes its clear resemblance to the Malay *nasi lemak* set ([Silpa-mag](https://www.silpa-mag.com/?p=184)). Som tam itself is generally held to have come from Isan (*tam mak hung*) and to have been adapted, sweeter and with peanuts and dried shrimp, in Bangkok ([Silpa-mag – when som tam came to Bangkok](https://www.silpa-mag.com/?p=5140); [Michelin Thailand](https://guide.michelin.com/th/th/article/features/decoding-the-delicious-som-tam)). ⚠️ No source dates the combination itself.

**Culinary science:** salt and a little sugar in the coconut cream make the rice rich and slightly sweet, a soft cushion for the papaya salad's lime acidity and chili heat.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Bangkok set:** khao man + som tam Thai + kaeng phet kai + nuea foi (sweet shredded beef) ([Silpa-mag](https://www.silpa-mag.com/?p=184)).
*   **With tamarind relish (*nam phrik som makham*):** an occasional extra in the same set ([Silpa-mag](https://www.silpa-mag.com/?p=184)).
*   **Isan counterpart:** sticky rice with *tam mak hung* — see [khao niao nueng](recipe_khao_niao_nueng.md).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **Bangkok set**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Coconut cream (for the rice) | 250 ml |
| Salt / sugar (rice) | 1 tsp / 1 tbsp |
| Green papaya, shredded | 400 g |
| Garlic, bird's-eye chilies, dried shrimp, roasted peanuts | 3 cloves, 3, 2 tbsp, 3 tbsp |
| Lime juice / fish sauce / palm sugar | 3 tbsp / 2 tbsp / 2 tbsp |
| Cherry tomatoes, long beans | 8, 4 pods |
| Red chicken curry, sweet shredded beef | to serve (≈ 400 ml, 120 g) |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Jasmine rice | 400 g raw |
| Coconut cream : water for rice | 250 ml : 300 ml |
| Som tam per plate | ≈ 100 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Coconut rice:** cook 400 g rinsed jasmine rice with 300 ml water; when the water is absorbed, fold in the coconut cream warmed with salt and sugar, cover and steam 10 min.
2. **Dressing:** in the clay mortar pound garlic and chilies, add palm sugar, fish sauce and lime and stir to dissolve.
3. **Som tam:** add long beans and tomatoes, bruise lightly, then the papaya, dried shrimp and peanuts; toss with pestle and spoon.
4. **Set:** serve each plate with coconut rice, som tam, a bowl of red curry and a little sweet beef.
