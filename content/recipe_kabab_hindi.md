# Kabab Hindi / كباب هندي

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Syria](provincial_syria.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md) · [Jordan](provincial_jordan.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented by Arabic recipe sites (and a Lebanese Cookpad entry naming it "meatballs in tomato sauce or kabab hindi"); the "Indian" name is not explained by any source found. Regional attributions are flagged.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Syria and Lebanon, common across the Levant and Iraq.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: meatballs or fingers of spiced minced meat simmered in a tomato sauce, served with vermicelli rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A frying pan and a wide pot, or an oven tray for the baked version.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Despite the name, *kabab hindi* ("Indian kebab") is a Levantine weekday dish: minced meat seasoned with onion, parsley and spice, rolled into balls or fingers, browned, then simmered in a sauce of fresh tomatoes and tomato paste. Arabic recipe sources serve it "usually with vermicelli rice" (*al-ruz al-mufalfal bi-l-sha'iriyyeh*). A Lebanese cook's Cookpad entry is titled "meatballs in tomato sauce, or kabab hindi", which shows the two names are used interchangeably. Some versions bake the fingers in the oven, or add potatoes.

It is a close relative of [daoud basha](recipe_daoud_basha.md), the Lebanese meatballs in tomato with pine nuts.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Syria / Lebanon (balls):** Meatballs browned, then cooked in tomato sauce; vermicelli rice.
*   **Fingers or tray version:** Meat shaped into fingers and baked in tomato sauce.
*   **With potatoes:** Potato cubes added to the sauce (*kabab hindi ma' batata*).
*   **Distinction from daoud basha (⚠️):** Daoud basha usually adds onions and pine nuts and is more strongly claimed by Lebanon; the boundary is blurry in home cooking.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Levantine kabab hindi (meatballs) with vermicelli rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Minced beef or lamb | 600 g |
| Onion, grated + parsley | 1 + ½ cup |
| 7-spice, salt, pepper | 1.5 tsp total |
| Fresh tomatoes, grated | 600 g |
| Tomato paste | 2 tbsp |
| Garlic | 3 cloves |
| Olive oil | 40 ml |
| Water | 300 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice (medium-grain) | 300 g |
| Vermicelli | 50 g |
| Water for the rice | 450 ml |
| Butter | 30 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Meatballs:** Mix the meat, onion, parsley and spices; roll walnut-sized balls and brown them in the oil.
2. **Sauce:** In the same pan, fry the garlic, add the grated tomatoes, paste and water; simmer 10 min.
3. **Simmer:** Return the meatballs and cook covered **20 min** until the sauce coats them.
4. **Rice:** Toast the vermicelli in butter, add the rice and boiling salted water, cook covered 15 min, rest 10 min.
5. **Serve:** Meatballs and sauce over the rice.
