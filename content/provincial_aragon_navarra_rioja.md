# Aragón, Navarra y La Rioja / Valle del Ebro

> ⬅ [README](README.md)

## 1. Overview
The middle **Ebro valley** grows rice along the lower Cinca and the Ebro (Huesca and Zaragoza paddies), but its rice cooking is **stew-based**: rice is added to *cazuelas*, rabbit and snail stews and vegetable dishes rather than cooked in a flat pan. Signature elements include **borraja** (borage, Aragón's emblematic vegetable), the **chilindrón** pepper base, **snails** (a Riojan Christmas Eve and festival food), steppe **rabbit**, and the region's olive oils and wines (with **sarmiento** fires in wine country). The *Revista Española de Nutrición Comunitaria* supplement on Aragón's forgotten dishes records a pastoral rice-and-stew culture, including *colas de cordero* (Good Friday lamb tails, with rice as a variant) and the now-forbidden Monegros **arroz con fardacho** (ocellated lizard, a protected species, documented only as history and not given a recipe here).

## 2. Cultural Sub-Regions
| Sub-region | Rice personality | Key towns |
|---|---|---|
| **Zaragoza & Ribera del Ebro** | Borrajas con arroz y almejas, chilindrón rices | Zaragoza, Alagón |
| **Bajo Aragón & Monegros** | Rabbit and snail rices, pastoral stews | Alcañiz, Caspe, Sariñena |
| **Pyrenees & Somontano (Huesca)** | Lamb, longaniza de Graus | Graus, Barbastro, Jaca |
| **La Rioja** | Snails in Riojan sauce, sarmiento fires | Haro, Logroño |
| **Ribera de Navarra** | Huerta vegetables (artichoke, cogollos) | Tudela |
