# Yakhnet Kousa / يخنة الكوسا

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md) · [Jordan](provincial_jordan.md) · [Syria](provincial_syria.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented by Arabic recipe sites as a household stew served over rice; no town-specific or historical source was found, so regional variants below are flagged.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** The Levant household kitchen (Palestine, Jordan, Syria, Lebanon); summer, when zucchini are cheapest.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: rounds or chunks of zucchini simmered with lamb or beef in tomato until soft, poured over white or vermicelli rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A wide stew pot on the hob; a lidded pot for the rice.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
Zucchini (*kousa*) are the Levant's most versatile summer vegetable: hollowed and stuffed as [kousa mahshi](recipe_mahshi.md) or [kousa bi-laban](recipe_kousa_bi_laban.md), and the cores and spare zucchini simmered as **yakhnet kousa**. Arabic recipe sources describe it as lamb or beef with zucchini slices and tomato, served "with white rice, or vermicelli, or bread", and give the classic service: the basmati or Egyptian rice cooked while the stew simmers, then the stew poured over it.

It is one of the cheapest of the *ruz w yakhneh* family; see [yakhnet batata](recipe_yakhnet_batata.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Basic Levantine version:** Meat cubes, zucchini slices, tomato, garlic, dried mint.
*   **Oven version (⚠️):** One recipe fries the zucchini first, mixes it with minced meat and tomato sauce, and bakes it; served with rice.
*   **Palestine (⚠️ household practice):** Often made with the scooped-out zucchini cores (*lubb al-kousa*) left over from mahshi, a thrift dish.
*   **Syria / Lebanon (⚠️):** Finished with dried mint and garlic fried in oil.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: basic Levantine yakhnet kousa with vermicelli rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Lamb or beef, cubed | 500 g |
| Zucchini (small, pale Levantine), in 2 cm rounds | 1 kg |
| Onion, chopped | 1 |
| Garlic | 4 cloves |
| Tomato paste + crushed tomatoes | 2 tbsp + 400 g |
| Dried mint + 7-spice | 1 tsp each |
| Olive oil | 40 ml |
| Water | 700 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice (Egyptian or basmati) | 300 g |
| Vermicelli (optional) | 50 g |
| Water for the rice | 450 ml |
| Butter | 30 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Meat:** Brown the meat with the onion, add water and spices, simmer **60 min**.
2. **Zucchini:** Add the zucchini, tomato paste and tomatoes; simmer **20 min**, until tender but holding shape.
3. **Rice:** While the stew simmers, toast the vermicelli in butter, add the rice and boiling salted water, cook covered 15 min, rest 10 min.
4. **Finish:** Fry the garlic and dried mint in a spoon of oil and stir into the stew.
5. **Serve:** Rice in the dish, stew poured over.
