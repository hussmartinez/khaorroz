# Txuritabeltz (Relleno Navarro y Sangrecilla) / Txuri-Beltz

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Aragón, Navarra y La Rioja](provincial_aragon_navarra_rioja.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented only briefly in the *Revista Española de Nutrición Comunitaria*'s 2022 survey of **forgotten dishes of Navarra**: one paragraph with ingredients and occasion, but no method or quantities. It is included because its "white" half is a **rice preparation** (Navarre's *relleno*). The method below is a reconstruction; verify it with Baztán cooks.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Northern Navarra, in the **Valle de Baztán** (Elizondo and the valley's villages).
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). The rice *relleno* is set (bound with egg), sliced and fried beside blood.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A pot for the relleno and a frying pan.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Txuritabeltz* means "white and black" in Basque. It pairs Navarre's **relleno**, a rice preparation made "as in the old days" with **lamb suet, rice, saffron and egg** (pork fat is usual today), with ***sangrecilla*** (cooked, crumbled blood). It is still prepared for the **festivals of the Baztán valley**, together with ***baztanzopa***, a soup of lamb heads. It is part of the same rice-in-charcuterie culture as the *morcillas de arroz* of Burgos and Villada, but here the rice is served as a dish rather than stuffed into a casing.

**Culinary science:** rice cooked in fat and saffron, then bound with beaten egg, sets firm as it cools, like a savoury rice cake. It can then be sliced and fried until golden. Its pale colour and mild taste contrast with the dark, iron-rich blood.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Baztán (RENC):** Relleno (lamb suet in the old days, pork fat now; rice, saffron, egg) + sangrecilla. Festival dish, paired with baztanzopa.
*   ⚠️ No village-level differences documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **reconstructed Baztán relleno + sangrecilla**.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Lamb suet (traditional) or pork fat, finely chopped | 120 g |
| Saffron | 0.2 g |
| Water or light lamb stock | 600 ml |
| Salt | to taste |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Round rice | 250 g |
| Eggs (binder) | 3 |
| Cooked lamb or pork blood (*sangrecilla*), in slices | 400 g |
| Onion (for the blood) | 1 |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** Melt the suet, add the rice and saffron, then the water and salt. Cook **≈ 18 min** until dry.
2. **Bind:** Off the heat, stir in the beaten eggs. Press into a tray and leave to set and cool.
3. **Slice & fry:** Cut the relleno into slices and fry until golden.
4. **Sangrecilla:** Fry the onion, add the crumbled cooked blood, and heat through.
5. **Serve:** Put the white relleno and the black sangrecilla side by side.
