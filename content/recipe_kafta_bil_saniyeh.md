# Kafta bil-Saniyeh / كفتة بالصينية

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** documented by Lebanese and Palestinian home-recipe sources; the rice service is explicit in the Lebanese sources, while Palestinian versions are documented without a stated side.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** Lebanon and Palestine (common across the Levant and its diaspora).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Eaten with rice: kafta patties baked in a tray with potatoes, onions and tomatoes in tomato sauce, served over or beside vermicelli rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A round oven tray (*saniyeh*); traditionally the bakery oven, today the home oven or the hob under foil.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
The *saniyeh* (tray) dish is the Levantine family's oven meal: patties of kafta laid over sliced potatoes, onions and tomatoes, sauced and baked. A Lebanese recipe calls it "a traditional Lebanese oven-baked dish of kafta and potatoes in tomato sauce, served over a white vermicelli rice". A Cookpad Lebanon version cooks the tray on the hob under foil for about 30 minutes. Palestine in a Dish publishes a Palestinian *kiftah bil-saniyeh* with vegetables.

It is the tomato twin of [kafta bi-tahini](recipe_kafta_bi_tahini.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Lebanon (oven):** Fried or baked kafta patties layered with sliced potatoes, onions and tomatoes; tomato sauce; vermicelli rice.
*   **Lebanon (stovetop, Cookpad):** Same layers, covered with foil and cooked on the hob ~30 min.
*   **Palestine:** Kafta with mixed vegetables in the tray (*kiftah bil-saniyeh*).
*   **Egyptian-influenced trays (⚠️):** Ready kafta and four potatoes, baked under foil, are common in Arabic recipe sites.

### Lebanese home style (served over vermicelli rice)
*(Merged from the Lebanon research.)*

*   **Tomato-broth tray (most common):** kafta, potato, onion, tomato, tomato broth ([Simply Lebanese](https://www.simplyleb.com/recipe/kaftacasserole/)).
*   **With eggplant:** some cooks add fried eggplant slices or skip the onion ([The Matbakh](https://thematbakh.com/kafta-bil-sanieh/)).
*   **Tahini tray (kafta bi-tahini):** a related version bakes the kafta under tahini sauce instead of tomato (mentioned by recipe writers; ⚠️ not documented here in detail).

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Lebanese kafta bil-saniyeh with vermicelli rice.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Minced beef or lamb | 700 g |
| Onion, grated + parsley | 1 + 1 cup |
| 7-spice, salt, pepper | 1.5 tsp total |
| Potatoes, sliced | 4 large |
| Tomatoes, sliced | 3 |
| Onion, sliced | 1 |
| Tomato paste + water | 2 tbsp + 300 ml |
| Olive oil | 40 ml |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice (medium-grain) | 300 g |
| Vermicelli | 50 g |
| Water for the rice | 450 ml |
| Butter | 30 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Kafta:** Mix the meat, grated onion, parsley and spices; shape 12–16 flat patties and brown them quickly.
2. **Layer:** Oil the tray; layer potatoes, onion and tomatoes; set the patties on top.
3. **Sauce:** Pour the tomato paste loosened in the water over everything.
4. **Bake:** Cover with foil and bake at 190 °C for **40 min**, uncover for 10 min.
5. **Rice:** Toast the vermicelli in butter, add the rice and boiling salted water, cook covered 15 min, rest 10 min.
6. **Serve:** Rice on the plate with kafta, potatoes and sauce alongside.
