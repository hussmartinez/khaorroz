# Kibbeh Labanieh / كبة لبنية

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Lebanon](provincial_lebanon.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** All of Lebanon; a winter and Ramadan dish in Beirut, the mountains and the South.
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Stew over rice, yogurt style. Small bulgur-and-meat kibbeh balls simmered in a warm garlic-and-mint yogurt sauce **thickened with short-grain rice cooked in the sauce**. Eaten hot or cold, with plain or vermicelli rice.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A heavy pot on a low flame, the yogurt stirred constantly in one direction so it does not split.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Labanieh* means "made with *laban*" (yogurt): it names the garlicky cooked-yogurt sauce. The dish uses the Lebanese meat-and-bulgur kibbeh, shaped into small balls (or stuffed *kibbeh qras* or *arnabieh*-style shells), and finishes them in the same cooked-yogurt sauce as [shakriyeh](recipe_shakriyeh.md). Rice plays two roles: a handful is cooked in the yogurt to thicken it, and the dish is served with plain or vermicelli rice. Without kibbeh, the rice-thickened yogurt soup alone (*labanieh*) is a side dish in its own right. L'Orient-Le Jour publishes Tara Khattar's family *kebbé labniyé*.

**Culinary science:** yogurt protein curdles above about 80 °C unless it is stabilised. Lebanese cooks whisk in cornstarch or an egg and stir constantly, in one direction, until it boils. Starch from the rice cooked in it acts as a second stabiliser and thickener. Dried mint fried in butter with garlic (*ta'leyeh*) goes in at the end for aroma.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Rice-thickened (common):** Short-grain rice half-cooked, then simmered in the yogurt.
*   **Egg-stabilised:** One egg instead of (or with) cornstarch; goat's or cow's yogurt.
*   **Without kibbeh:** More rice, served as a side dish (*labanieh*).
*   **L'Orient-Le Jour family version (Tara Khattar):** A published Beirut home version.
*   ⚠️ Regional (town-level) differences are not documented.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: kibbeh balls in rice-thickened yogurt.*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Ready kibbeh balls (lean meat + fine bulgur), small | ≈ 24 (500 g) |
| Plain full-fat yogurt (cow or goat) | 1 kg |
| Cornstarch (or 1 egg) | 1 tbsp |
| Short-grain rice, rinsed | 60 g |
| Water | ≈ 300 ml |
| Garlic, crushed with salt | 4 cloves |
| Dried mint | 1 tbsp |
| Butter or ghee | 2 tbsp |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Rice in the sauce | ≈ 60 g per kg of yogurt |
| Rice on the side (plain or vermicelli) | 250 g |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Rice:** Simmer the rice in the water until half-cooked.
2. **Yogurt:** In a heavy pot, whisk the yogurt with the cornstarch (or egg). Heat it, stirring **constantly in one direction**, until it boils.
3. **Combine:** Add the half-cooked rice with its water and simmer **≈ 10 min**, stirring.
4. **Kibbeh:** Add the kibbeh balls and simmer gently **10–15 min**, until cooked through.
5. **Ta'leyeh:** Fry the garlic and dried mint in the butter for 1 min, stir it into the pot, and serve hot (or cold) with rice.
