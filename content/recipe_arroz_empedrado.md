# Arroz Empedrado / Arròs Empedrat

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Província de València](provincial_valencia.md)

> ⚠️ **Source-confidence note:** this dish is well known in Valencian home cooking (L'Horta, La Ribera), but few verified recipes are online. My searches mostly returned the unrelated Catalan *empedrat* (a cold cod-and-bean salad). The variations below reflect common Valencian household practice. Cross-check them against a local cookbook, and treat the reference links as technique references.

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** L'Horta de València and La Ribera Alta/Baixa. A Lenten (Quaresma) dish.
*   **Liquid & Texture Profile:** **Seco** (dry). Baked or paella-finished, loose grains studded with beans.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A clay *cassola* in the baker's oven (like arròs al forn), or a paella over wood.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
"Empedrat" means "cobbled" or "paved with stones". The name refers to the **white beans** dotting the surface of the rice like cobblestones. It is the Lenten equivalent of arròs al forn: the pork and sausage are replaced with **salt cod** (*bacallà*), which was the inland Catholic household's fasting protein. Beans, tomato, garlic, potato and parsley complete it. In Valencia it is traditional on Fridays in Lent and on **Good Friday** (*Divendres Sant*), when a whole head of garlic often sits in the centre as in arròs al forn.

**Culinary science:** desalting salt cod (36–48 h with water changes) rehydrates the myofibrillar proteins. Frying the flaked cod briefly in the oil before the tomato releases the gelatinous juices that bind the rice. Pre-cooked beans add starch without needing broth, so the bean-cooking liquid is the ideal cooking stock.

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)

*   **The L'Horta Style:** Oven-baked in a cassola. Cod, white beans, tomato halves on top, a whole head of garlic in the centre, potato rounds. Pebre roig. Broth from the bean liquid.
*   **The La Ribera Style:** Cooked in a paella. **Garrofó** is sometimes used instead of white beans, and spinach or chard is often added.
*   **The Marina Alta echo:** Cod + cauliflower or *pebrot* (pepper), close to the Alicante *arròs amb bacallà i coliflor*.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: **L'Horta** (oven, cassola).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Salt cod, desalted 36–48 h, flaked | 250 g |
| Cooked white beans | 300 g |
| Bean cooking liquid (or water) | 800 ml |
| Olive oil | 80 ml |
| Garlic: 1 whole head + 2 cloves minced | — |
| Ripe tomato: 1 grated + 1 halved | 250 g total |
| Potato, 0.5 cm rounds | 200 g |
| Pebre roig | 3 g |
| Parsley, chopped | 1 tbsp |
| Saffron | 0.2 g |

**Extraction:** Fry the potato rounds and set aside. Fry the halved tomato and the whole garlic head and set aside. Fry the minced garlic, add the cod for **1 min**, then the pebre roig (5 s) and the grated tomato (4 min). Add the parsley.

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short-grain rice | 400 g |
| Hot bean liquid | 800 ml (2:1) |
| Oven | 230–240 °C |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Preparation:** Desalt the cod, cook the beans and preheat the oven.
2. **Toasting:** Add the rice to the cod sofrito in the cassola and toast for 2 min. Add the beans.
3. **Assembly:** Garlic head in the centre, tomato halves and potato rounds on top.
4. **Liquid Addition:** Boiling bean liquid with saffron. Check the salt: the cod carries salt, so taste before adding more.
5. **Oven:** **18–20 min**.
6. **Finish/Resting Phase:** Rest 5 min.
