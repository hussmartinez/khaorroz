# Fatteh Ghazawiyya / الفتة الغزاوية

> ⬅ [README](README.md) · [Palestine](provincial_palestine.md)

## 1. Geographic & Texture Profile
*   **Primary Geographic Zone:** The Gaza Strip (Gaza City, Khan Yunis, Rafah).
*   **Dish Type & Texture:** Feast platter of layered bread, rice and lamb. Toasted bread softened with broth, a layer of buttery short-grain rice, lamb on top, nuts, and a fiery garlic-chilli-lemon *dugga* on the side.
*   **Traditional Cooking Vessel & Fuel:** A stockpot for the lamb broth, a heavy pot for the rice and a wide round tray (*sidr*) for serving. Bread is toasted in the oven or over embers.

## 2. Historical & Cultural Context
*Fatteh* ("crumbled") names a family of Levantine dishes built on toasted or stale bread soaked in broth or yogurt. Lebanese and Syrian *fatteh* are usually chickpea-and-yogurt breakfast dishes. **Gaza's version is a feast:** bread, rice and lamb, sharpened with Gaza's signature chilli. The Bethlehem chef Fadi Kattan writes that "the Gaza cuisine is very different from the Bethlehem cuisine", shaped by "sea air, chilies, and long coastal meals". He learned this fatteh from his aunt's cook in Gaza, the late Im Khader, "quite possibly the best cook in Gaza".

The **dugga** (pounded fresh red chilli, garlic, salt and lemon) is what makes it Gazan. It belongs with the dill, green chilli and lemon that *The Gaza Kitchen* identifies as the coastal palate, "more herby, spicy, peppery, lemony" than inland cooking. The dish sits between [mansaf](recipe_mansaf.md) (rice over bread with lamb) and the Chilean-Palestinian wake [fate](recipe_fate_chilena.md).

## 3. Local Town-by-Town Variations (Crucial)
*   **Gaza City (Kattan / Im Khader):** *Shrak* or *rkak* thin bread, lamb broth with cardamom, allspice and cinnamon, short-grain rice in ghee, almonds and pine nuts, dugga on the side.
*   **Southern Gaza (Khan Yunis, Rafah) (⚠️):** Families report spicier dugga and more garlic. Not documented in writing.
*   **Lebanese / Damascene fatteh (comparison):** Chickpeas, yogurt and tahini on bread, no rice. A different dish sharing the name.

## 4. The Master Recipe (4-Person Batch Blueprint)
*Variant represented: Gaza City (after Fadi Kattan, quantities halved).*

### The Flavor Base (El Fondo)
| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Bone-in lamb pieces | 4 × 250 g |
| Water | 2.5 L |
| Olive oil | 1 tbsp |
| Onion, quartered | ½ |
| Garlic cloves | 2 |
| Bay leaf, black peppercorns, cardamom pods, cinnamon stick, allspice berries | 1 / 1 tsp / 5 / 1 / ¾ tsp |
| Salt | 1½ tsp |
| **Dugga:** fresh red chillies, garlic, lemon juice, salt | 3 / 1 clove / 2½ tsp / pinch |

### Assembly Ratios
| Element | Quantity |
|---|---|
| Short-grain rice, soaked 30 min | 300 g (1¼ cups) |
| Water + strained lamb broth for the rice | 250 ml + 125 ml |
| Ghee | 1 tbsp |
| Shrak/rkak or thin pita | 1½ large sheets |
| Almonds / pine nuts, fried | ½ cup / 1 tbsp |

### Step-by-Step Cooking Blueprint
1. **Broth:** Brown the garlic, onion and lamb in the oil, add the spices and water, bring to a boil and simmer covered for about 1.5 hours. Salt and strain, keeping the broth.
2. **Rice:** Toast the drained rice briefly in the ghee, add the water and broth, and simmer covered until absorbed (≈ 18 min). Fluff.
3. **Nuts and dugga:** Fry the almonds and pine nuts separately. Pound the chillies and garlic with salt, then add the lemon.
4. **Bread:** Tear the bread and toast it at 180 °C until crisp.
5. **Assemble:** Spread the bread on a tray and moisten it with broth. Cover with rice, lay the lamb on top and scatter the nuts. Serve the dugga on the side.
